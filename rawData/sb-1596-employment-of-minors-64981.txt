Discussion Name:
SB 1596: Employment of Minors

1. Thesis:
The bill revises employment restrictions for minors, aged 16 and 17 years, by altering the hours during which they can be employed. It allows these minors to work before 5:30 a.m. and until midnight on days preceding school days, formerly 6:30 a.m. to 11 p.m. It also restructures the maximum permitted work hours for minors and authorizes the Department of Business and Professional Regulation to grant waivers for certain restrictions, upon conditions like economic necessity or family emergency.

  1.1. Pro:
  Exemption clauses recognize the diverse circumstances of minors, including those who are already high school graduates, in a home education program, or in immediate domestic service, showing an understanding of the need for a nuanced approach to minor labor laws.

  1.2. Con:
  Extended working hours for minors might lead to longer days that could conflict with their commitments to education and could negatively impact their overall well-being if proper balance is not maintained.
