Discussion Name:
Are Humans fundamentally different from other animals?

1. Thesis:
Humans are fundamentally different from other animals.

  1.1. Con:
  Humans do not have enough comprehension about animal communication and reasoning to know for certain whether humans are fundamentally different from animals.

    1.1.1. Pro:
    Arguments have been made that dolphins are as intelligent, if not more so, than humans, but are limited by their lack of limbs and communication. Currently we teach dolphins to respond to American Sign Language, a form of communication they can not produce themselves.

    1.1.2. Con:
    This argument does not mean that humans are not, in fact, fundamentally different from animals. It would only imply that we do not know, and not knowing is different from something actually not being the case in reality.

    1.1.3. Pro:
    Meercats were observed to have specific sounds to refer to dangers. They give a surprising amount of information through the sounds they make. They even go as far as to identify specific predators. [1] It is unclear what the calls mean but it is clear they have a meaning.

    1.1.4. Con:
    Gorillas, Bonobos, and Orangutans are all able to learn sign language. A chimpanzee was observed teaching their child the sign for "come here". [2] This confirms they can use it as a communication tool.

  1.2. Pro:
  Humans are physically and biologically unique.

    1.2.1. Pro:
    Humans have an advanced neo-cortex (The mental parts we share with other animals have allowed us the safety within which to evolve this.).

      1.2.1.1. Con:
      Chimpanzees [3] also have an advanced neo-cortex, even if the human's one is just bigger.

    1.2.2. Con:
    Every living creature is physically and biologically unique.

    1.2.3. Pro:
    Although on a biological continuum with other animals, it is humans' unique constellation of features (embodied form, idealizing imagination, cumulative culture and tools, language, advanced cognition, etc) that set them distinctly apart from other animals.

      1.2.3.1. Con:
      The fact that all of these things have been observed in other species suggests that humans are, at best, ahead of other species in terms of inevitable evolutionary advancement. Such distinction could be made between humans a few thousand years ago and humans today.

      1.2.3.2. Con:
      No single human has the ability to make a satellite. This is the cumulative outcome of advances in civilization over thousands of years, enabled by greater intelligence and cognition, and not a fundamental difference from animals.

      1.2.3.3. Pro:
      The ability to conceptualise specific ideas and thoughts about another being's existence is a form of consciousness only humans have.

        1.2.3.3.1. Pro:
        A dog, for example, cannot conceptualise what it means to be a human, but a human is able to conceptualise what it means to be a dog.

      1.2.3.4. Pro:
      Other animals have use of tools, but this does not compare with humans using very advanced tools. A bird or chimpanzee may use a stick for fairly short-term effect, but  a human may make satellites to predict the weather etc.

        1.2.3.4.1. Con:
        If a comparison can be made, then we are talking about a difference of degree, not of kind.

        1.2.3.4.2. Con:
        A stick is a tool. It was the original usage of the stick that allowed humanity to survive and thus create satellites. Prior technology paves advancement.

        1.2.3.4.3. Con:
        -> See 1.2.3.2.

      1.2.3.5. Con:
      All of these can also be found in the whale/dolphin family.

      1.2.3.6. Con:
      Other animals also have uniqe constellations of features (other features), that set them distictly apart from other animals.

    1.2.4. Con:
    Humans are practically identical to chimpanzees and other primates in terms of genetics

      Many humans have died as a result of environmental or climate conditions.

        1.8.7.3.1. Pro:
        Many humans have died as a result of either being too cold or because of diseases caught due to being affected by cold weather conditions.

        1.8.7.3.2. Pro:
        Many humans have died as a result of natural disasters.

          1.8.7.3.2.1. Con:

        1.8.7.3.3. Pro:
        Many humans have either died or had their living expectancy shortened as a result of pollution.

      1.8.8.2. Pro:
      “Modern human societies strongly depend on high levels of cooperation between individuals, something we experience every day. Although some of these mechanisms are shared with other animal species, for others there is little or no evidence in non-human animals.” (Source) [58]

    1.8.10. Pro:

      1.8.10.1. Pro:

(Evolution of Human Uniqueness, The Spanish Journal of Psychology, Volume 19, January 2016).

      1.8.10.2. Con:
