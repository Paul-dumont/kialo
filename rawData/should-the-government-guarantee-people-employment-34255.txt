Discussion Name:
Should the government guarantee people employment?

1. Thesis:
The government should not guarantee people employment.

  1.1. Pro:
  Encourages people to not try as hard to find a job.

    1.1.1. Con:
    Free college and little to no interest on student loans can make a huge impact in helping people to obtain sustainable employment

  1.2. Con:
  It will help the lower class.

  1.3. Con:
  Guaranteed jobs are better than government welfare programs.

    1.3.1. Pro:
    Jobs guarantee a sustained income for an indefinite period of time. Welfare programs, such as cash grants, only provide short term relief.

    1.3.2. Con:
    Unlike welfare programs, a guaranteed job system could be disruptive to private employers [1].

      1.3.2.1. Pro:
      Guaranteed jobs could establish a baseline job-and-benefit employment package [2] that private employers are unable to compete with.

  1.4. Pro:
  The government does not have a responsibility to provide people with employment.

    1.4.1. Pro:
    The government's job is to create a prosperous society, not guarantee individual jobs.

    1.4.2. Con:
    The right to work [3] and engage in productive employment is a human right and it is the responsibility of the state to achieve it through guaranteeing meaningful employment.

    1.4.3. Con:
    It's unethical to stand by starving people and not do anything about it.

  1.5. Con:
  Guaranteed jobs would protect people from economic and social shocks that threaten a minimum standard of living.

    1.5.1. Pro:
    The measures instituted to contain the COVID-19 pandemic [4] resulted in a huge loss of livelihood. In absence of an employment guarantee, many countries would have seen far greater unemployment than they did.

    1.5.2. Pro:
    Capitalism [5] is prone to economic shocks that result in mass unemployment. The government should step in to provide a safety net by guaranteeing employment.

    1.5.3. Pro:
    Increased automation [6] directed at increasing productivity can entail a massive rise in long-term unemployment. The government would have to protect people from losing livelihood by guaranteeing employment.

      1.5.3.1. Con:
      Loss of livelihood due to technological unemployment [7] can be curbed through other ways such as increased safety net and universal basic income.

  1.6. Pro:
  It would be too expensive for the government.

    1.6.1. Pro:
    In the US, providing jobs with a standardized salary to all jobseekers is estimated to cost only 2% of its GDP [8].

  1.7. Pro:
  Guaranteeing employment can result in wasteful projects that don't contribute meaningfully to society.

  1.8. Pro:
  The government can't make jobs appear from nothing.

    1.8.1. Pro:
    Governments cannot easily create jobs without increasing public spending.

    1.8.2. Con:
    Cutting payroll taxes [9] for newly hired employees creates jobs.

    1.8.3. Con:
    Governments can boost job growth by reducing interest rates [10].

    1.8.4. Con:
    Investing in public construction projects [11] can create thousands of jobs.

    1.8.5. Pro:
    Shifts in labor employment and availability are determined by the markets [12] and not the state.

      1.8.5.1. Con:
      The state can inject demand into the economy by increased spending [13], which in turn influences labour demand and employment.
