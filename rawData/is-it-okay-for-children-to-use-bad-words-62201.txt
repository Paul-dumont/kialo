Discussion Name:
Is it okay for children to use bad words?

1. Thesis:
Children should not use bad words.

  1.1. Con:
  I think that adults and kids do it because they don't know of any other ways to get their feelings out but you have to find a healthier way to get out their bad feelings inside.

    1.1.1. Con:
    Research has found that swearing is a healthy way to express pent-up emotions [1], so there is no reason to find an alternative way to release them.

    1.1.2. Con:
    Though many people do struggle with finding the right words to express themselves. There are plenty of words out there for them to choose from on the spot without blurting out curse words

  1.2. Con:
  Using those vulgar words can make you look like a fool, uneducated and disrespectful. Words can be offensive, use the wrong ones and you will quickly turn out to be rude and mean people.

  1.3. Con:
  Swearing can leave someone with a bad impression, signal a lack of control and indicate a bad attitude or immaturity.

    1.3.1. Con:
    Those who swear may be judged solely because of the dominance of moralistic values in society. In a free society, this may not be the case.

  1.4. Con:
  Adult swearing tends to apply to issues such as verbal abuse, sexual harassment, and discrimination. When children enter the picture, offensive language becomes a problem for parents and a basis for censorship in media and educational systems. [2]

  1.5. Con:
  Kids could get in trouble for yell and bad words.

  1.6. Pro:
  Young children often swear because they're exploring language. They might be testing a new word, perhaps to understand its meaning. Sometimes swearing happens accidentally when children are learning to say words. Children might also be trying to express a feeling like frustration.

    1.6.1. Con:
    If children young enough to still explore a language are using swear words to express their feelings, this means they are learning from adults who swear to express their feelings. This is not a safe and nurturing environment for children.

    1.6.2. Con:
    Though it is beneficial for young children to explore language and learn, that doesn't necessarily mean they must learn swear words and that the use of them is ok. Even in the "adult world" today many adults are opposed to swear words as they see them as violent and demeaning. There are many other words for the children to use to express how they feel without the need to include cursing.

  1.7. Con:
  Often teens or kids do it because they think it makes them look cool, but does it really not? It just makes it harder to make friends.

    1.7.1. Con:
    Swearing may make it difficult to make friends with those who do not approve of swearing, but this is unlikely to be a problem with those who are comfortable with this.
