Discussion Name:
Are video games art?

1. Thesis:
Video games are art.

  1.1. Pro:
  Video games make use of multiple recognized art forms.

    1.1.1. Pro:
    Most video games rely on the creation of concept art during development and for advertising purposes. There are entire communities dedicated to concept art as an art form.

      1.1.1.1. Pro:
      Example: Concept Art .org [1]

      1.1.1.2. Pro:
      Example: Concept Art World .com [2]

    1.1.2. Pro:
    3D modeling has been recognized as an art form similar to sculpture.

    1.1.3. Pro:
    Video games that convey a narrative are fiction in the same way that books and cinema do.

    1.1.4. Pro:
    Voice acting is a recognized art form present in games.

    1.1.5. Con:
    Just because something contains or makes use of art does not make that thing art.

      1.1.5.1. Con:
      Video games do more than just contain or make use of art, they combine a variety of well-recognized art forms to create something new- ultimately creating a whole new art form unto itself.

      1.1.5.2. Con:
      Advertisements, and even the designs of corporate logos, have been recognized as art forms and have been displayed in galleries.

      1.1.5.3. Con:
      If art can be made from things that are not art objects, then it is possible to make art from other art objects.

      1.1.5.4. Pro:
      An art museum is not an artwork.

        1.1.5.4.1. Con:
        Several important art galleries around the world are examples of architectural art, such as the Guggenheim Museum [3].

      1.1.5.5. Pro:
      An advertisement is not an artwork.

        1.1.5.5.1. Con:
        That is debatable. [4]

        1.1.5.5.2. Con:
        Deviantart utilizes art as advertisements.

        1.1.5.5.3. Con:
        Just because something has been created for a purpose does not mean that it is not art. Advertisements can be intended to sell things to consumers and still be art, just as video games can be entertaining and still be art.

    1.1.6. Pro:
    Video games make extensive use of animation, which is a recognized art form.

      1.1.6.1. Con:
      -> See 1.1.5.

      1.1.6.2. Con:
      Much of the animation in video games is minor or arbitrary, with the final product being contingent on the user's input. There is no artistry on the behalf of the creators in that emergent process.

        1.1.6.2.1. Con:
        Quite a lot of animation is actually put into games, especially with cutscenes, which disallows user input to control it [5].

    1.1.7. Pro:
    Design(character, environment, UI and world), storytelling and 3D Modelling are only a few art forms that are implemented in video games, on all platforms.

    1.1.8. Pro:
    A person once modded a version of Tetris to create an art generator [6].

    1.1.9. Pro:
    Pixel art is commonly used within the indie community and has created a lot of influential titles.

      1.1.9.1. Pro:
      The title 'Shovel Knight' was a big success on Nintendo consoles.

      1.1.9.2. Pro:
      Doom (originally an Indie game series) was a heavily influential title to the entire gaming industry.

        1.1.9.2.1. Pro:
        It also has been the most-ported title in history [7], from tech older than DOOM [8], to 2D games with fan-made 3D engines.

      1.1.9.3. Pro:
      Minecraft is a game designed to be based on pixel art, with cubes and pixelated textures.

        1.1.9.3.1. Pro:
        Many communities have ported pixel art into Minecraft, with map art, pixel art made of blocks, and more.

      1.1.9.4. Pro:
      AAA developers like Nintendo are also using pixel art to create new games.

        1.1.9.4.1. Pro:
        Super Mario Odyssey had a major feature that involved playing the game like the original NES title in some sections [9].

        1.1.9.4.2. Pro:
        Super Mario Bros. 35 [10] is basically a 'what if SMB1 was a Battle Royale game?'.

          1.1.9.4.2.1. Con:
          The game is shut-down and now unavailable.

        1.1.9.4.3. Pro:
        Pac-man Championship Edition has many themes that make the game similar to old Namco titles like DigDug [11] or Pac-Mania [12].

        1.1.9.4.4. Pro:
        Big developer Team 17 has 'The Escapists [13]', a retro-looking prison escape simulator.

          1.1.9.4.4.1. Pro:
          The game was so successful that it warranted spin-offs like 'The Survivalists [14]' and 'The Escapists: The Walking Dead [15]'.

        1.1.9.4.5. Pro:
        Big developer SEGA brought forth Sonic Mania [16], a game that calls forth the nostalgia of the original 16-bit games on the Genesis.

        1.1.9.4.6. Pro:
        Sheltered [17] was a pixelated hit developed by Team 17 and Unicube.

    1.1.10. Pro:
    Most video games incorporate a musical score, which has been recognized as an art form.

  1.2. Con:
  Violent video games promote violent behavior and are a health hazard, not art works.The APA considers violent video games to be a violent behavior risk. [18]

    1.2.1. Con:
    The evidence supporting a connection between violent video games and violent behavior is inconsistent at best.

    1.2.2. Con:
    Many mental health professionals disagree with the APA's stance. [19]

    1.2.3. Con:
    The claim that new media is corrupting the youth is hardly new. The claim has been directed at rock music, movies, television, comics, and even poetry during the time of Socrates. These claims have never proven to be justified, and each of the above media have been recognized as art forms.

    1.2.4. Con:
    Even if a work of art causes harm, that doesn't invalidate it as a work of art. For example, graffiti is recognized as vandalism but many works of graffiti have become protected public art pieces.

      1.2.4.1. Pro:
      This article, for example, [20]which references a man who destroyed many works of graffiti while redeveloping his property and was successfully sued by the artists.

    1.2.5. Pro:
    The harm violent video games can cause was recognized when the ESRB was created to rate games for age categories.

      1.2.5.1. Con:
      The ESRB was not originally created to regulate distribution however, only to inform parents. It's legislators who have created laws which depend on ESRB ratings. If the ESRB were to ever disappear, which is possible as it is not a government agency, those laws would be defective.

      1.2.5.2. Con:
      Game designers are not obligated to have their games rated by the ESRB, and a sizable portion of games are never rated.

    1.2.6. Con:
    Not all violent offenders play violent video games. The Sandy Hook shooter, for example, spent hours playing Dance Dance Revolution [21].

      1.2.6.1. Con:
      One example cannot stand as evidence against the majority of violent offenders.

      1.2.6.2. Con:
      There were violent video games found in the Sandy Hook shooter's files [22]. Therefore, there is a possibility that they contributed to his violent behavior.

        1.2.6.2.1. Con:
        The article states that "Lanza was fond of video games, both violent and non-violent.", which could neglect the games being connected to the behavior.

  1.3. Con:
  Video games are created primarily as shallow and meaningless entertainment.

    1.3.1. Pro:
    Many games contain little more than empty power fantasies with no literary value.

      1.3.1.1. Con:
      Some art has no literary value; it's just pleasant to look at. Some video games are similar, but this does not discredit it from being a form of art.

      1.3.1.2. Pro:
      Examples like Duke Nukem [23], Borderlands [24], and Mortal Kombat [25] are nothing more than dark humor and violence.

        1.3.1.2.1. Con:
        Duke Nukem can be seen as a work of ironic comedy, poking fun at action movies and other first person shooter games at the time.

        1.3.1.2.2. Con:
        The original Mortal Kombat used digitized photos of actors in costume carrying out martial arts maneuvers as sprites.

        1.3.1.2.3. Con:
        Borderlands partly has a quite creative [26] open world setting [27] that in many respects may be more artistic than the worldbuilding and fantasy worlds of novels for example and includes many scifi tropes.

        1.3.1.2.4. Con:
        The examples given are not directed at children, as they are rated for teenage or adult audiences by the ESRB [28].

    1.3.2. Con:
    Video games that present controversial material can spark significant outrage and public debate.

      1.3.2.1. Pro:
      Example: Grand Theft Auto Franchise Controversies [29]. Nearly all versions of the game have been criticized or banned due to graphic violence, and some have been additionally criticized for sexual content as well.

        1.3.2.1.1. Pro:
        A title within the GTA series even had a fully 18+ feature (hot coffee) that was removed, but the residual code from it was leftover and was converted into a mod, it has sparked many controversies.

      1.3.2.2. Pro:
      Example: Entire wikipedia page dedicated to just Mortal Kombat controversies. [30]

      1.3.2.3. Pro:
      Example: Controversies surrounding video games in general. [31]

      1.3.2.4. Con:
      If the creator of the game didn't intend or wished to have its game being used or exploited in the public debate, it can't be used as an argument for being a work of art.

    1.3.3. Con:
    Survival horror games like Silent Hill [32] can produce a powerful emotional response of genuine fear.

    1.3.4. Con:
    The intent behind its creation does not exclude it from being viewed as an art form.

    1.3.5. Con:
    Many people find themselves so attached to the events and characters in a game, that the events of the game's story can bring the audience to tears.

      1.3.5.1. Pro:
      The death of Mordin Solus in Mass Effect [33] emotionally impacted the fans of the game.

      1.3.5.2. Pro:
      Many fans were emotionally impacted [34] by the death of Aeris in Final Fantasy 7.

      1.3.5.3. Pro:
      Visual and auditory art forms [35] often evoke emotion.

      1.3.5.4. Con:
      People can attach themselves to things that are generally meaningless.

    1.3.6. Pro:
    Many games are purely a competitive activity, more like a sport or highly advanced board game.

      1.3.6.1. Pro:
      Many games of this sort, like Overwatch, League of Legends, Star Craft, Super Smash Bros., and Call of Duty 4, are used as tournament games in which virtual athletes compete for cash prizes in front of a spectator audience.

        1.3.6.1.1. Con:
        Nintendo has canceled numerous of those events, especially in 2020 [36], which can be disconcerting for users trying to keep basically unavailable titles relevant.

      1.3.6.2. Pro:
      Sports and highly-advanced board games are in fact the opposite of shallow and meaningless.

      1.3.6.3. Con:
      Being a competitive activity does not make something "shallow and meaningless."

    1.3.7. Con:
    Some would say that existence itself is shallow and meaningless [37]- video games, then, are just an expression of that.

    1.3.8. Con:
    This is dependent on a case-by-case scenario. Animation is determined to be art, but some companies may make extremely shallow and meaningless art, such as seen in the corporate art style [38].

    1.3.9. Con:
    Video games can have a positive impact [39] on society.

    1.3.10. Con:
    Many game franchises have been marketed for their in-depth story and lore.

    1.3.11. Con:
    Similarly, the most successful franchises of films, music, books, and other media formats have been criticized as "shallow and meaningless entertainment", yet it is well understood that these are art forms.

    1.3.12. Pro:
    Many games are devoid of any content of meaning aside from an activity, such as pong, tetris, and candy crush.

      1.3.12.1. Con:
      -> See 1.3.1.1.

      1.3.12.2. Con:
      Many other games do still have meaning. Games can carry messages, reconsider our moral judgments and be emotionally resonant, just like other art forms.

  1.4. Con:
  Video games are created primarily as a product to make money for huge corporations, it is an industry after all.

    1.4.1. Pro:
    The US video game market alone was worth 25.1 billion dollars [40] in 2010.

    1.4.2. Pro:
    Indeed as it is true, most games are like that now, most of bethesda's games are now pay-to-win and the same can be said with the "Bedrock" Editions of Minecraft (owned by microsoft) of which has a currency you have to buy in order to buy textures, skins, or heck, even just a map

    1.4.3. Con:
    Being made for profit does not invalidate a work as a piece of art. Artists deserve to be paid adequately for their contribution to society.

      1.4.3.1. Con:
      When something is created for profit, it is created based on what is most likely to sell well, not what the creator desires. As such, anything created for profit can not be art, because it is not a true or meaningful expression of the creator's vision.

        1.4.3.1.1. Con:
        Artists create art that are both 'for profit' and an expression of their vision. The two can coexist.

      1.4.3.2. Pro:
      Michelangelo was paid "astronomical [41]" sums for painting the Sistine Chapel and the tomb of Pope Julius II, but this does not diminish the artistic value of these works.

    1.4.4. Con:
    There is an indie game design community who are not funded by major publishers and rarely break even on the games they produce.

      1.4.4.1. Pro:
      Anyone can create short, story-based games using engines like Twine [42], for example. Being accessible to anyone, this is akin to [43] zine making.

      1.4.4.2. Pro:
      There are generalized game design software, like the RPG Maker series [44], which enable even non-programmers to participate in game design.

    1.4.5. Con:
    There is a hobby game design community who do not make any money whatsoever from the games they produce.

    1.4.6. Con:
    The same applies to films, music, even books but those things are generally accepted as art.

  1.5. Pro:
  The creation of modern AAA video games [45] requires so much technical skill, that it must be considered artistry.

    1.5.1. Con:
    Plenty of recently released AAA titles like Fallout 4, Fallout 76, and Skyrim (tESO) all were huge market flops.

      1.5.1.1. Pro:
      This pattern has continued to fester as well, titles such as Cyberpunk 2077 [46], Halo Infinite [47] [1] [48], Battlefield 2047 [49], and far, far more were massive flops on release. [1] [50] [2] [51]

    1.5.2. Pro:
    Minecraft: Dungeons, a spin-off AAA title, uses quite a lot of technical skill as compared to its original [52].

    1.5.3. Pro:
    The AAA series Halo has gone on strong for 19+ years, with many innovations to come from the title.

      1.5.3.1. Con:
      This was true up until the release of Halo Infinite [53], which ended up harming the playerbase immensely [54]. [1] [55]

    1.5.4. Con:
    Technical skill alone does not make something art.

      1.5.4.1. Pro:
      Examples of things that take great skill but are not considered artistry include industrial welding, surgery, Olympic wrestling, landing the space shuttle, or practicing law.

  1.6. Pro:
  Video games can have a significant impact on a person's emotions and values, similar to art.

    1.6.1. Pro:
    -> See 1.3.2.

    1.6.2. Pro:
    -> See 1.3.3.

    1.6.3. Pro:
    -> See 1.3.5.

    1.6.4. Pro:
    Games also help the expressiveness of communities of color, sex, religion, and more, providing a greater sense of inclusion with audiences.

    1.6.5. Con:
    A similar statement could be used about a person standing in the middle of a forest, upon a mountain, or on a shoreline watching the ocean. It does not make anything automatically into a work of art.

  1.7. Con:
  Video games have not produced any examples that have had a significant impact on culture at large the way traditional art forms have.

    1.7.1. Pro:
    Some art, such as Michaelangelo's Madonna [56], are notable works that have influenced religious people around the world.

    1.7.2. Pro:
    Some art can depict social challenges [57] facing a nation, thus having a thought-provoking impact on society.

    1.7.3. Con:
    Technological development has improved far greatly with video games.

      1.7.3.1. Pro:
      The military once modded the FPS title DOOM to train their soldiers, the mod eventually became DOOM 2.

      1.7.3.2. Pro:
      The Xbox 360 controllers are quite used commonly within the police (namely bomb squads) to control their machines.

      1.7.3.3. Pro:
      The Xbox 360 Kinect (or the Xbox One Kinect) is commonly used for motion tracking, it can be seen within medical scenarios quite often.

        1.7.3.3.1. Pro:
        OMSI also uses the Kinect accessory, but for the simulation of landmasses.

    1.7.4. Con:
    Pokemon Go [58] has lead to the passing of bylaws, injuries, arrests, and has been mentioned in presidential campaign speeches.

    1.7.5. Con:
    The highly publicized violent video game controversies in the 90s and early 2000s (regarding Mortal Kombat and Grand Theft Auto, respectively) resulted in the creation and expansion of the ESRB.

      1.7.5.1. Pro:
      Atari 2600 [59] (originally in arcades) had a game known as "Death Race [60]" that involved major controversies all because it was a game about a car driving over people. Many people panicked due to the violence in that game.

      1.7.5.2. Pro:
      The game title 'Night Trap' had a big case within federal courts.

      1.7.5.3. Pro:
      The FPS title Doom (and technically Wolfenstein) have had federal court cases due to the blood and violence within the title(s).

    1.7.6. Con:
    Many videos games have developed communities dedicated to supporting and appreciating these art-forms that break from traditional cultural boundaries of race, language, religion and nationality.

      1.7.6.1. Pro:
      There are millions of users within DeviantArt that work with the nostalgic art style of pixel art.

      1.7.6.2. Con:
      The formation of insular subculture groups is not the same as affecting culture at large.

        1.7.6.2.1. Con:
        The support and appreciation from these communities have led to mainstream adaptions and interpretations of multiple games.

          1.7.6.2.1.1. Pro:
          The game franchise Resident Evil, first released in 1996 en.wikipedia.org [61], currently has six movie adaptions by the same name en.wikipedia.org [62].

          1.7.6.2.1.2. Pro:
          Pokemon, originally released as a game on the gameboy in 1996, is now a massive phenomenon with global reach. There are movies, anime and a trading card game attached to the gaming franchise. en.wikipedia.org [63]

          1.7.6.2.1.3. Pro:
          Video game characters are routinely a source of inspiration for cosplayers. Many professional cosplayers use characters from multiple media, such as movies, anime, comic books etc. to create their costumes.

    1.7.7. Con:
    Not all art forms are traditional, and are yet considered art [64] and have a form of impact on the viewer.

    1.7.8. Con:
    Minecraft has been used to design structures that would later be built in real life.

      1.7.8.1. Pro:
      There is a large community of builders currently rebuilding the earth in 1:1 scale [65].

      1.7.8.2. Pro:
      The Block by Block Foundation [66] has funded and activated dozens of public space projects in more than 30 countries around the world, improving the lives of hundreds of thousands of people in the process.

      1.7.8.3. Pro:
      According to many sources, Minecraft [67] has been used to help repair ecosystems like a river down in Mexico

  1.8. Pro:
  Video games are infinitely diverse, like art.

    1.8.1. Pro:
    Like art, video games are only creatively limited by one's imagination.

  1.9. Pro:
  Many video games include complex storytelling, a widely recognized art form.

    1.9.1. Pro:
    An example is the Halo series [68], which has a narrative spanning multiple games, books, and comics.

    1.9.2. Pro:
    The Last of Us is a perfect example [69] of beautiful and enthralling storytelling, no different from film. It then simply includes the aspect of personal interaction from the viewer, something that only adds to the experience and increase its qualification as art.

    1.9.3. Pro:
    Though hard to know, there are many storytelling aspects hidden within the 'The Legend of Zelda' series.

      1.9.3.1. Pro:
      'Breath of the Wild' has quite a bit more storytelling going on, such as with flashbacks and hidden books that tell of Hylian history.

    1.9.4. Pro:
    The lego game development team 'Traver's tales [70]' create in-depth stories based on movie franchises, such as DC [71], Star Wars [72], and Marvel [73].
