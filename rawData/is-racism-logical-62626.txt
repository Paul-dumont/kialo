Discussion Name:
Is racism logical?

1. Thesis:
Racism is rational and valid.

  1.1. Con:
  Having different ethnic and racial groups provides genetic variation and thus prevents genetic similarity which is harmful for our species.

  1.2. Pro:
  It's common sense, if a wolfs have different sub-species because they adapted to their different enviroments, they have differences, The same with humans.

  1.3. Con:
  Although having a readily identifiable adversary is preferable to their being indistinguishable the future of mankind depends on the acceptance of all races. There are too many divisive forces, and one or two less is a survival advantage.

  1.4. Pro:
  The main reason it isn't considered valid and thus not practised is because of its violation on common moral principles such as utilitarianism and ethical altruism.

    1.4.1. Pro:
    Most logical inferrences are often taboo. (Ex: atheism)

      1.4.1.1. Con:
      Calling racism an inference is racist [1], and it implies entitlement to feel superior to others.

    1.4.2. Con:
    The very basis of the moral principles that prohibit these logical inferrences are logical inferrences themselves.

  1.5. Pro:
  A society that proiritizes progress would adapt racism.

    1.5.1. Pro:
    A hierarchy that organizes it's people from the most to least effiecient races would lead to great progress in society.

      1.5.1.1. Con:
      The idea of having a less efficient races is illogical and racist. There are less efficient people, but the color of the skin doesn't affect one's ability to perform tasks [2].

    1.5.2. Con:
    Modern nation states have all made successful progress while simultaneously reducing the amount of structural racism in their societies.

      1.5.2.1. Pro:
      Countries that actively promoted racism, such as apartheid South Africa [3], were neither successful nor progress-oriented places.

  1.6. Con:
  Racist views are stemmed from individual anecdotes, ethnocetrism and other biases that are antithetical to and distorts rational thought.

    1.6.1. Pro:
    Racist views are stemmed from competition and the need to feel superior by necessary having to user power and dominance on others. [4]
