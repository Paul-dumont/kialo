Discussion Name:
Should fascist and nazi movements be banned?

1. Thesis:
Fascist and nazi movements and organizations should be banned

  1.1. Con:
  Banning an ideology only forces its adherents underground, making them even more dangerous.

    1.1.1. Con:
    By banning an ideology, adherents' voices would be limited, and they would find it more difficult to spread their message.

      1.1.1.1. Pro:
      The UK has banned some far-right extremist groups with "toxic ideologies [1]". After the ban, openly supporting these organisations is criminal offence.

    1.1.2. Pro:
    Banning the Far-Right could accelerate an extremist underground [2] where the extremist rhetoric could become more appealing.

  1.2. Pro:
  Fascist and Neo-nazi groups are a threat to democracy.

    1.2.1. Pro:
    In December 2020, Germany [3] banned pro-Nazi groups because they threatened democratic structures.

    1.2.2. Con:
    Democracy is not necessarily the best way of governing and hence, the idea of fascism should not be considered intolerable. Both have drawbacks, but no form of governance is purely devastating enough to be 'banned'.

    1.2.3. Pro:
    In Weimar-Germany, nazis were able to use the democratic system and free press to eventually become powerful enough to implement a dictatorship.

  1.3. Pro:
  These ideologies seek to abolish a tolerant society

    1.3.1. Pro:
    Fascism tends to highlight national values [4] which directly oppose "us" against "them" [5] resulting in an intolerant society.

    1.3.2. Con:
    It could be argued that other types of ideologies that are currently in power in many countries have not manage to create a tolerant society either.

      1.3.2.1. Pro:
      The United States is still fighting racism. [6]

  1.4. Con:
  Fascist movements tend to be the result of cultural unrest. The issues should be identified and solved in order to avoid these movements, instead of banning them.

    1.4.1. Pro:
    In times of societal crises, fascist movements tend to be on the rise [7].

      1.4.1.1. Pro:
      Fascism has been on the rise in Europe [8] for the last couple of years.

      1.4.1.2. Pro:
      Fascism uses people's discontent with inequality and poverty together with their desire for security and promises nationalistic "measures to protect them [9]".

      1.4.1.3. Con:
      It would be hard to define "times of societal crises" in order to find a correlation with fascist movements.

    1.4.2. Con:
    Fascism [10] is an ideology independently of the context.

  1.5. Con:
  Our society values freedom of speech

    1.5.1. Con:
    Allowing organizations that seek to abolish all freedom of speech to exist is a danger to this value.

      1.5.1.1. Con:
      The right to freedom of speech [11] is not absolute [12] and limited by governments in many cases.

        1.5.1.1.1. Con:
        If you are not allowed to think and express unpopular opinions, then we do not have freedom of speech at all.

        1.5.1.1.2. Pro:
        The European Union has banned hate speech [13].

      1.5.1.2. Pro:
      Fascists tend to weaponize free speech [14], which can endanger the practice of free speech in a democratic and harmonious way.

    1.5.2. Pro:
    Allowing fascist and nazi groups to express their views gives an opportunity to publicly challenge their ideas.

    1.5.3. Con:
    There is a difference between exercising free speech and inciting violence, which Nazi groups do.

      1.5.3.1. Pro:
      Free speech [15] cannot be used as an excuse to incite violence or cause harm.

      1.5.3.2. Con:
      It is very difficult to draw a line [16] between expressing views and inciting violence.

    1.5.4. Con:
    Fascist and nazi do not value freedom of speech, so they deserve to get what they wish to others.

      1.5.4.1. Pro:
      Nazis and fascists inherently oppose and promote infringing upon free speech and many other fundamental human rights, and must be prevented from doing so.

    1.5.5. Con:
    Freedom of a speech hostile to any part of the society is destroying the society. Starting from kids, who can't protect themself from such dangerous statements.

    1.5.6. Pro:
    Political freedom [17] is one of the most important features of democratic societies.

  1.6. Pro:
  Fascism and nazism are inherently violent ideologies

    1.6.1. Con:
    Many ideologies advocate violence in certain situations. Democratic countries also engage in warfare, communists often seek wealth expropriation. Even imprisonment can be regarded as a form of violence.

    1.6.2. Pro:
    Fascism [18] rejects the idea that violence is inherently negative.

      1.6.2.1. Pro:
      Fascists [19] hold that political violence can help bring about national rejuvenation.

    1.6.3. Pro:
    Nazism was responsible for the genocide [20] of Jews during World War II.

    1.6.4. Pro:
    Sweden [21] has considered banning certain neo-nazi groups to contain the threat of violence.

    1.6.5. Pro:
    Many neo-nazi groups can be considered terrorist [22] organizations and should therefore be banned.

      1.6.5.1. Pro:
      The British government deemed National Action [23], a neo-nazi group, as a terrorist organization.

  1.7. Pro:
  Yes, because the fact that they're anti-democratic and are a threat to democracy outweighs freedom for free speech. We shouldn't allow free speech of trying to get rid of free speech, it bites its own tail.
