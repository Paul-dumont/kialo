Discussion Name:
Should Muslim countries continue to boycott French products?

1. Thesis:
Muslim countries should continue to boycott French products.

  1.1. Pro:
  The boycott strengthens international solidarity against growing Islamophobia [1] and resultant discrimination.

      Skewed media depictions of Muslims fuel Islamophobia [5].

        1.1.1.2.1. Pro:
        On TV shows or films, characters that don't pass the Riz test [6] misrepresent the many nuances of a Muslim identity; instead, Muslim characters are often relegated to stereotypes and tired tropes.

      1.1.1.3. Con:
      There is no direct evidence of Islamophobic attacks being inspired by mainstream media. Anti-Muslim radicalisation derives from fringe information sources and online communities [7].

      1.1.1.4. Pro:
      The mainstream media's disproportionately negative coverage of Muslims opens the door to more radical and dangerous manifestations of Islamophobia [8].

        1.1.1.4.1. Pro:
        The prevalence of casual antisemitism in Germany laid the foundation [9] for the much harder and more dangerous antisemitism of the Nazis.

        1.1.1.4.2. Con:
        Mainstream media cannot be held responsible for the content of fringe outlets or online discussion boards.

          1.1.1.4.2.1. Con:
          When mainstream media outlets openly downplay [10] the threat fringe beliefs, they become complicit.

          1.1.1.4.2.2. Con:
          Contemporary political discourse, of which the mainstream media forms a significant part, has become functionally tied [11] to toxic sources of media, like 4chan.

          1.1.1.4.2.3. Con:
          Mainstream news outlets may be inadvertently but largely responsible [12] for the dissemination of fake news.

          1.1.1.4.2.4. Pro:
          Some mainstream news outlets, like National Pubic Radio in the United States, intentionally promote media literacy [13] in order to moderate political discourse.

        1.1.1.4.3. Pro:
        Disproportionately negative media coverage of Muslims effectively moves the Overton Window [14], making Islamophobia appear less extreme.

        1.1.1.4.4. Con:
        Most mainstream media outlets skew slightly to the political left [15]; thus, it is unlikely that they would contribute to Islamophobia, which is generally a right-wing phenomenon [16].

          1.1.1.4.4.1. Con:

          1.1.1.5.1.1. Con:

          1.1.1.5.2.1. Con:

        1.1.1.5.3. Pro:

          1.1.1.5.3.1. Con:
