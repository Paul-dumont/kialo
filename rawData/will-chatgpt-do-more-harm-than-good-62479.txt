Discussion Name:
Will ChatGPT do more harm than good?

1. Thesis:
ChatGPT will do more harm than good.

  1.1. Con:
  ChatGPT will make life easier for everyone.

    1.1.1. Pro:
    It can be used to improve writing [1] such as corporate emails or works of art, or help create such.

    1.1.2. Pro:
    It can help create diagrams, designs, and scripts.

    1.1.3. Con:
    Dependence on ChatGPT would make life harder in instances when it cannot be accessed.

      1.1.3.1. Pro:
      Significant parts of the world have either no or intermittent internet access [2] and cannot take full advantage of ChatGPT.

        1.1.3.1.1. Pro:
        Internet access and speeds in most of the developing world are years behind those in the developed world. According to experts, universal internet access is unlikely [3] until at least 2050.

        1.1.3.1.2. Con:
        Despite the surface of the Earth being comprised of 70% water, not even water [4] is universally accessible. Therefore, universal accessibility is not a fair expectation for ChatGPT.

        1.1.3.1.3. Pro:
        More than 95% of the planet could theoretically connect via 3G or 4G signals, but most residents of the world's poorest countries cannot afford [5] the digital devices or the exorbitant costs of wireless data.

      1.1.3.2. Pro:
      Despite widespread adoption of ChatGPT in privileged nations, the rest of the world would likely end up using less-reliable substitutes or having limited access to free ChatGPT tools.

        1.1.3.2.1. Con:
        As ChatGPT is currently a free tool [6], it is unlikely that people from poorer nations would use substitutes.

        1.1.3.2.2. Pro:
        If underprivileged nations use less-reliable substitutes, it will increase global inequality and information asymmetry.

          1.1.3.2.2.1. Con:
          It will increase global inequality by developed nations falling for a commercial marketing hype and undermining their nation's education system which already is barely linked to their economies' and people's reality.

        1.1.3.2.3. Pro:
        As ChatGPT develops exponentially, privileged nations have an incumbency advantage [7].

      1.1.3.3. Con:
      Decreased demand for earlier ChatGPT substitutes would make them more accessible and improve the lives of the less fortunate.

        1.1.3.3.1. Pro:
        As earlier substitutes become more accessible, the playing field is levelled because the less fortunate will have access to tools and resources that were previously reserved for those with greater means.

        1.1.3.3.2. Con:
        Decreased demand for earlier substitutes for ChatGPT would stifle their innovation and make them obsolete.

      1.1.3.4. Con:
      This is an argument against technological advancement of any kind. Dependence on smartphones makes life more difficult [8] when they can't be accessed, but that doesn't mean smartphones are not a massive benefit to society.

        1.1.3.4.1. Pro:
        The difficulty of living without technology points to its immense relevance rather than its faults.

    1.1.4. Pro:
    ChatGPT can be used for personal entertainment and enjoyment.

      1.1.4.1. Pro:
      ChatGPT can help come up with concepts and characters for roleplaying games such as Dungeons & Dragons [9].

        1.1.4.1.1. Pro:
        These concepts and characters provide a more diverse gaming universe for players who have a variety of preferences.

        1.1.4.1.2. Con:
        These concepts and characters make game masters far lazier because they can become reliant on ChatGPT.

      1.1.4.2. Pro:
      ChatGPT can create original jokes [10].

        1.1.4.2.1. Pro:
        These jokes can provide comedians with new content and ideas.

        1.1.4.2.2. Con:
        These original jokes will likely be less funny and meaningful because people enjoy connecting [11] to a particular human experience behind a joke.

          1.1.4.2.2.1. Con:
          Such original jokes are more likely to resonate with a wider range of people, owing to the fact that they are free from societal norms and the baggage of someone's prior personal experience.

      1.1.4.3. Con:
      There are many other forms of equally stimulating entertainment that do not pose a danger to their users [12] like ChatGPT and other ChatGPT-like AI tools.

        1.1.4.3.1. Con:
        People have a variety of preferences that are dynamic and conducive to different kinds of stimulation; we should not prioritise one set of preferences over another.

        1.1.4.3.2. Pro:
        Other forms of entertainment also have decades of regulation [13] behind them, whereas ChatGPT is very new and carries uncertain consequences.

        1.1.4.3.3. Pro:
        One man used ChatGPT to write his suicide note [14], which could have led to his death if his friends and family had not intervened.

      1.1.4.4. Con:
      ChatGPT has demonstrated a bias [15] in favour of the political left, meaning it may be less enjoyable to use for people who are politically right-wing.

        1.1.4.4.1. Con:
        If ChatGPT, which theoretically has access to all of the world's information [16] prior to 2022, has chosen the political left, then it has determined that left-leaning ideas have the most merit overall.

          1.1.4.4.1.1. Con:
          The data sets that ChatGPT are trained with are subject to bias [17] from the authors of the materials.

          1.1.4.4.1.2. Con:
          This could also mean that most of its training data [18] comes from the political left.

          1.1.4.4.1.3. Con:
          Even if left-leaning ideas are somehow superior, ChatGPT is meant to be a relatively unbiased tool. The dissemination of ideas from the most meritorious political wing could further alienate right-wingers, especially extremists, keeping them from interacting with other ideologies.

          1.1.4.4.1.4. Pro:
          An example of ChatGPT's preference for leftist ideas is its pro-environmental inclination [19], which is a stance backed by reliable data.

        1.1.4.4.2. Con:
        Shutting out those who are politically right-wing would prevent a societal shift to the radical right and fascism [20].

          1.1.4.4.2.1. Con:
          Shutting them out would only worsen such potential problems. People would likely opt to use and/or build more heavily biased tools and feel like they have been treated unfairly.

            1.1.4.4.2.1.1. Pro:
            Alternatives with different political leaning scores do exist, e.g., LLMs of or based on Davinci GPT-3 [21] and LAION [22]. The neutrality of the planned 'truth-oriented' alternative(s), mainly the planned 'TruthGPT' [23], is questioned.

          1.1.4.4.2.2. Con:
          An AI tool should not be allowed to decide which ideas and groups are represented in a democratic society.

        1.1.4.4.3. Pro:
        Politically right-wing people often reject change [24] and frequently disparage active attempts to force them to alter their behavior. As such, they would likely be less interested in using ChatGPT, which they might feel is an attempt to indoctrinate them.

        1.1.4.4.4. Pro:
        People are less likely [25] to want to patronise a program that they believe might skew information rather than present it accurately.

      1.1.4.5. Con:
      ChatGPT has a very basic interface, meaning people may get bored with it quickly compared to visually appealing video games.

        1.1.4.5.1. Pro:
        People are used to instant gratification [26] and dopamine hits from colours and movement; ChatGPT does not provide people with any of these and would be less interesting to people accustomed to visual appeal.

          1.1.4.5.1.1. Con:
          Simply because a certain segment of the population may prefer more visually appealing media over ChatGPT does not lower its effectiveness as a tool for personal entertainment; it merely changes the target audience.

            1.1.4.5.1.1.1. Con:
            It greatly reduces the target audience size.

        1.1.4.5.2. Con:
        People do not come to ChatGPT to play video games; they come to ChatGPT to seek out information. So if they wanted to play video games, they would have gone to play video games anyway.

        1.1.4.5.3. Con:
        ChatGPT, even when used for amusement, acts as a conversationalist. The quality, relevance, and relatability of its replies are of greater importance compared to the visual interface.

      1.1.4.6. Pro:
      Introverted people can benefit from ChatGPT and ChatGPT-like tools.

        1.1.4.6.1. Pro:
        ChatGPT can be an engaging personal conversation partner [27] for people with social phobias or introverted people who desire interaction but dislike talking to other people in real life.

        1.1.4.6.2. Con:
        Introverted people might become more accustomed to chatting [28] with the ever-available ChatGPT and decide to venture out into society less frequently, which would be detrimental to their mental health.

          1.1.4.6.2.1. Con:
          Introverted people, by definition [29], need to venture out less frequently into society to preserve their mental health [30] and energy. ChatGPT can provide them with companionship without the discomfort of social events.

    1.1.5. Con:
    Due to its limited [31] technical capabilities, ChatGPT has very few practical applications.

      1.1.5.1. Pro:
      The fundamental architecture leads to [32] persistent inaccuracies and falsehoods – indifference to semantic truth.

      1.1.5.2. Pro:
      ChatGPT lacks [33] emotional intelligence.

        1.1.5.2.1. Con:
        Its lack of emotional intelligence means that ChatGPT can provide its users with impartial information that is not biased.

          1.1.5.2.1.1. Con:
          Despite lacking EQ, ChatGPT is still subject to multiple biases [34].

        1.1.5.2.2. Pro:
        ChatGPT is less able to tailor a solution to a consumer because it cannot meaningfully connect to them.

      1.1.5.3. Pro:
      ChatGPT struggles to perform multiple tasks [35] at the same time.

        1.1.5.3.1. Con:
        ChatGPT doesn't need to be good at performing multiple tasks to be effective. Many users have simple and individual tasks that are clear and easy to provide answers to.

        1.1.5.3.2. Pro:
        If users have complex issues that require multiple tasks to be performed at once, ChatGPT can unintentionally give them wrong or misleading answers.

      1.1.5.4. Con:
      ChatGPT will free up time spent on more mundane tasks so people can focus on more complex endeavours.

        1.1.5.4.1. Pro:
        People will be less stressed when they have less tasks overall to complete.

        1.1.5.4.2. Con:
        If ChatGPT saves time by automating routine tasks that would otherwise be completed by people, it could result in a significant loss of jobs.

    1.1.6. Con:
    Only one in ten [36] global workers have key AI skills, meaning that ChatGPT will raise the bar for participation in the workforce.

      1.1.6.1. Pro:
      The more skills that people have to learn to participate in the workforce, the harder it will be for them to maintain jobs.

        1.1.6.1.1. Con:
        If it becomes increasingly hard for the majority of society to maintain a job, there will be a stronger societal push [37] for the government to provide a universal basic income (UBI).

      1.1.6.2. Con:
      ChatGPT is intuitive [38] and easy to learn to use.

        1.1.6.2.1. Con:
        ChatGPT is a text-based tool, which means that it lacks the visual cues that users might be accustomed to from other user interfaces.

        1.1.6.2.2. Pro:
        ChatGPT uses natural language processing to understand and respond to user inputs in a conversational manner, which can be more intuitive and familiar for users compared to traditional interfaces like buttons and menus.

    1.1.7. Pro:
    ChatGPT can be a useful tool for neurodiverse people [39].

      1.1.7.1. Pro:
      A great deal of online content, including videos and pictures, does not come with captions or explanations. ChatGPT can increase the usefulness [40] of this content for some blind and deaf users by explaining what is going on in certain photos and videos.

        1.1.7.1.1. Pro:
        Once blind and deaf users have access to more online content, they can better connect with their friends, family, and the rest of society.

        1.1.7.1.2. Con:
        ChatGPT is a generalised platform and is inferior to other disability programs that are uniquely designed to serve the needs of disabled people.

      1.1.7.2. Pro:
      ChatGPT can help autistic people [41] with communication difficulties.

        1.1.7.2.1. Pro:
        ChatGPT can provide personalised relationship advice [42] by informing people of differences in communication preferences or styles, which can help autistic people maintain friendships and relationships.

        1.1.7.2.2. Pro:
        ChatGPT can help autistic people become accustomed to unfamiliar social situations, by providing them the opportunity to experiment with conversations in a low-pressure environment.

        1.1.7.2.3. Pro:
        People with autism frequently have difficulty finding the right words to use in certain situations. By giving ChatGPT the scaffolding [43] to create for them, autistic people have found it easier to express themselves.

        1.1.7.2.4. Con:
        Some have described their interactions with ChatGPT as communicating with someone who is on the autism spectrum [44].

          1.1.7.2.4.1. Con:
          The fact that ChatGPT itself has been found to exhibit [45] autistic behaviour potentially makes it a more relatable conversation partner for people with autism.

        1.1.7.2.5. Con:
        Some autistic people might choose to spend time primarily with ChatGPT, rather than with other human beings.

      1.1.7.3. Pro:
      ChatGPT can provide dyslexic people [46] with reading and writing assistance, for example by suggesting synonyms for commonly misspelt words, simplifying text for easier reading, or providing a spelling and grammar check.

        1.1.7.3.1. Pro:
        Dyslexic people may feel discouraged from requesting reading and writing assistance, but now they can feel confident in seeking out help anonymously through tools like ChatGPT.

        1.1.7.3.2. Con:
        ChatGPT's responses are tailored to be as general as possible [47], which does not suit the particular needs of those with dyslexia.

      1.1.7.4. Con:
      Tools like ChatGPT could cause neurodiverse people to hide behind technology and become more reclusive.

        1.1.7.4.1. Con:
        ChatGPT provides a wide range of information from all walks of life that could help integrate neurodiverse people.

        1.1.7.4.2. Pro:
        The more reclusive neurodiverse people become, the more isolated and disconnected they become from the rest of society.

      1.1.7.5. Pro:
      ChatGPT can help people with ADHD stay focused and organised [48], by providing tools to help them manage their daily lives more effectively.

        1.1.7.5.1. Pro:
        ChatGPT can provide reminders and prompts to help people with ADHD stay on task and assist in time management and goal setting, which are real struggles that people with ADHD face.

        1.1.7.5.2. Con:
        ChatGPT lacks personalization, so its general-purpose language model does not take into account the unique needs and preferences of individuals with ADHD.

      1.1.7.6. Con:
      Neurodivergent people represent only 15-20% [49] of the world's population, meaning that from a purely consequentialist view, if ChatGPT makes the world worse for everyone else, these benefits are less important.

        1.1.7.6.1. Pro:
        From behind a veil of ignorance [50], we would choose an outcome that is better for the majority of people, given that we do not know which group we will be born into.

          1.1.7.6.1.1. Con:
          From a veil of ignorance, we would choose a system that enables everyone to thrive, especially people at risk of marginalization.

        1.1.7.6.2. Con:
        Consequentialism is not legitimate because it could also be used to justify slavery by punishing a small minority to benefit the majority.

      1.1.7.7. Con:
      Other technologies, like assistive reading technology [51], can provide similar benefits without carrying the dangers that interacting with ChatGPT does.

        1.1.7.7.1. Con:
        Other technologies do not integrate ChatGPT into the wider neurotypical community and isolate people.

        1.1.7.7.2. Pro:
        Other technologies are also not associated with the bad press that ChatGPT has.

    1.1.8. Pro:
    ChatGPT has valid informal educational applications.

      1.1.8.1. Pro:
      ChatGPT is capable [52] of performing advanced searches that people can use to deeply explore a given topic.

        1.1.8.1.1. Con:
        It is very often inaccurate [53] and it is hard to know when it is partly false or misleading [54] since it generally sounds plausible.

        1.1.8.1.2. Pro:
        Comparatively to conventional search engines, ChatGPT provides quicker and more definitive [55] search results.

        1.1.8.1.3. Con:
        ChatGPT relies on a limited set of data and may be unable to apply [56] recent information.

      1.1.8.2. Con:
      Reliance on ChatGPT instead of conventional academic training might cause learners to develop skewed perspectives as a result of the biases and limitations [57] of ChatGPT.

        1.1.8.2.1. Pro:
        Biases and skewed perspectives compound over time as there is less and less of a checkback against them.

        1.1.8.2.2. Con:
        Conventional academic training can also be biased and contain a skewed perspective, but ChatGPT incorporates a much more diverse set of perspectives from all walks of life.

      1.1.8.3. Con:
      Unlike traditional educational institutions and resources, ChatGPT is not regulated to ensure it provides appropriate age-specific information.

        1.1.8.3.1. Con:
        ChatGPT uses generally accessible language and does not produce adult content, making it largely appropriate for all age groups.

        1.1.8.3.2. Pro:
        If children are exposed to information that is not age-specific, it can lead to unintended trauma or confusion.

      1.1.8.4. Pro:
      ChatGPT can summarize lengthy articles [58], allowing people to quickly get the gist of something without spending hours reading and attempting to understand it.

        1.1.8.4.1. Pro:
        When people can digest articles quicker, they can read more of them from varying perspectives to better understand a complex issue.

        1.1.8.4.2. Con:
        People will become complacent and lazy about understanding issues if the articles are quickly summarised and no effort is required.

    1.1.9. Pro:
    ChatGPT will make the criminal justice system more efficient.

      1.1.9.1. Pro:
      People have used ChatGPT to successfully appeal fines.

        1.1.9.1.1. Con:
        More people might be willing to commit minor infractions if they believe that ChatGPT can help them escape paying a fine.

          1.1.9.1.1.1. Pro:
          People are more likely [59] to commit offenses when they believe they are likely [60] to get away with them.

          1.1.9.1.1.2. Con:
          People don't usually intend to commit minor infractions; rather, they usually arise as a result of mistakes. ChatGPT can help these people avoid undue fines, which is more important than the prospect of a very small number of people misusing the AI program.

        1.1.9.1.2. Con:
        Fines imposed by the state typically go to public services [61]. If more people opt out of paying fines using ChatGPT, the state will have less money for public services that benefit everyone.

          1.1.9.1.2.1. Con:
          The amount of money that the state collects from fining individuals is a negligible [62] part of any public works budget (p. 1 [63]).

          1.1.9.1.2.2. Con:
          The state budget should not [64] depend on the commission of crimes; rather, it should be built through taxes with fines providing the government with a surplus.

          1.1.9.1.2.3. Pro:
          Since ChatGPT would not be as accessible [65] to the general public as government services are, it would not be a net benefit to society if the use of ChatGPT came at the cost of government revenue.

          1.1.9.1.2.4. Pro:
          UK councils in 2021 made a record profit [66] off of car park charges and fines (£1.76bn), a significant amount of income that would be otherwise lost.

        1.1.9.1.3. Pro:
        A student in York, England, used ChatGPT to successfully appeal [67] an incorrectly issued parking ticket.

          1.1.9.1.3.1. Pro:
          As more people are able to use ChatGPT to appeal incorrect parking tickets, it will decrease the incentive for law enforcement to issue frivolous and unnecessary fines.

          1.1.9.1.3.2. Con:
          ChatGPT may create a false promise to others and help them appeal claims that should otherwise not be appealed.

        1.1.9.1.4. Pro:
        An individual from Brighton, England, used ChatGPT to write a successful appeal [68] for a parking fine imposed at an airport.

          1.1.9.1.4.1. Pro:
          As ChatGPT makes legal services more accessible, low-income individuals will not have to face disproportionate punishment in the legal system.

          1.1.9.1.4.2. Con:
          With ChatGPT making legal services more widely accessible [69], many lawyers will stand to lose their jobs and livelihoods.

      1.1.9.2. Con:
      ChatGPT can be used to assist in crimes.

        1.1.9.2.1. Pro:
        People with low skill in searching the Web could use, usually sophisticated, workarounds to research dangerous knowledge using ChatGPT [70].

        1.1.9.2.2. Con:
        OpenAI has put restrictions on ChatGPT [71] to prevent it from being used for criminal purposes.

          1.1.9.2.2.1. Con:
          ChatGPT can be jailbroken [72] to bypass the restrictions placed on it.

          1.1.9.2.2.2. Pro:
          ChatGPT cannot provide answers that could facilitate the commission of crimes, such as prompts related to the production of bombs. [73]

          1.1.9.2.2.3. Con:
          Restrictions are only as good as their authors. Human's aren't great at anticipating all possible misuse scenarios or at framing restrictions perfectly/without error. Errors or holes in restrictions made them easily bypassable in the DPD AI chatbot recently following a simple update [74].

        1.1.9.2.3. Pro:
        ChatGPT can be used [75] for fraud and impersonation.

          1.1.9.2.3.1. Pro:
          ChatGPT can be used [76] to mimic human interactions, thereby making phishing scams more believable.

            1.1.9.2.3.1.1. Pro:
            LLM phishing and online fraud can be created faster, much more authentically, and at significantly increased scale [77]. For example, via the capacity to impersonate the style of speech of specific individuals

              1.1.9.2.3.1.1.1. Pro:
              This can threaten privacy and security as well as be used for propaganda and disinformation.

          1.1.9.2.3.2. Con:
          The increase in AI detection tools [78] can also ensure that fraud is more easily detected.

            1.1.9.2.3.2.1. Con:
            AI detection tools have been found to be highly ineffective, with some of the best tools being accurate only 50% of the time. [79]

            1.1.9.2.3.2.2. Pro:
            There are many AI detection tools that, when used together [80], can detect the use of AI and therefore show that an email is likely not authentic.

  1.2. Pro:
  ChatGPT will disrupt [81] or potentially eliminate entire businesses or industries, causing harm to the economy.

    1.2.1. Con:
    ChatGPT, combined with other generative AI programs, could add trillions [82] to the global economy.

      1.2.1.1. Pro:
      Some businesses are using ChatGPT to boost productivity and enhance their profit margins.

        1.2.1.1.1. Pro:
        Hedge fund managers are improving profitability by incorporating ChatGPT [83] into their daily routines and company protocols.

          1.2.1.1.1.1. Con:
          Increased use of ChatGPT would likely negate these benefits, as all competitors would be able to use the tool to achieve similar effects.

        1.2.1.1.2. Con:
        Boosting productivity and enhancing profit margins in markets with tight competition could still lead to businesses going bankrupt.

          1.2.1.1.2.1. Con:
          Businesses that do not adapt to the times [84] are destined to fail, regardless of market conditions.

        1.2.1.1.3. Pro:
        ChatGPT is extremely useful for the consulting industry [85].

          1.2.1.1.3.1. Con:
          This is an exaggeration since at most the mentioned applications would make it merely slightly useful even though that is debatable due its unreliability, frequently generating false or "hallucinated" information.

          1.2.1.1.3.2. Pro:
          ChatGPT can perform [86] complex analyses that would supercharge the ability of consultants to advise clients.

          1.2.1.1.3.3. Con:
          ChatGPT might be used for business analysis by companies, thereby reducing the demand for consultants.

            1.2.1.1.3.3.1. Con:
            Companies will still want to employ external firms to disaggregate accountability for any decision that ends poorly for them, meaning consulting firms will still be needed in a post-ChatGPT world.

      1.2.1.2. Con:
      ChatGPT itself is a business, owned by OpenAI, through a partnership with Microsoft [87]. If either of them were to go out of business or shut the project down, it could cascade into disastrous economic results for thousands of individual companies and small businesses.

        1.2.1.2.1. Pro:
        With semi-frequent service outages [88], ChatGPT is an unreliable form of boosting profits; during outages [89], businesses could collectively stand to lose millions.

          1.2.1.2.1.1. Pro:
          ChatGPT is a frequent target of DDoS attacks [90] from hackers, which will cause instability for years to come.

          1.2.1.2.1.2. Con:
          Over time, OpenAI will improve the defenses for ChatGPT and make it harder for hackers to disrupt service, annulling this issue.

        1.2.1.2.2. Con:
        OpenAI is investing more into ChatGPT, ensuring that the business will be successful for years to come.

          1.2.1.2.2.1. Pro:
          OpenAI is reportedly looking to invest in making its own AI chips [91] to reduce costs and further guarantee the future success of ChatGPT and other in-house AI projects.

          1.2.1.2.2.2. Con:
          Investing more capital into any given business does not guarantee success [92].

      1.2.1.3. Con:
      The share of ChatGPT in this is likely to be small – writing poetry, fiction drafts or misinformation-containing explanatory blog posts only provide a certain small level of real economic value.

      1.2.1.4. Pro:
      Generative AI can be used create or modify art [93] or discover pharmaceuticals such as senolytics [94] and antibiotics [95].

    1.2.2. Pro:
    ChatGPT will eliminate many well-paying jobs [96] by replacing the work done by freelance and full-time employees, causing significant disruption to major industries.

      1.2.2.1. Con:
      Automation, raising the productivity of jobs, and making some jobs redundant are not intrinsically harmful.

        1.2.2.1.1. Con:
        AI automation, while potentially beneficial, is currently harmful because of the human casualties, who, by the way society is designed, might have to suffer prolonged unemployment and bankruptcy if replaced or devalued by AI.

          1.2.2.1.1.1. Con:
          If increasing the efficiency of labour has negative impacts on the public, then it is as a result of the lack of social welfare policies that have resulted in people needing to work, even inefficiently, so as to earn enough to survive.

        1.2.2.1.2. Pro:
        AI automation can give workers more agency, creative freedom, and leisure.

          1.2.2.1.2.1. Pro:
          It can or could mean that people ultimately have more time to work on more important things.

            1.2.2.1.2.1.1. Pro:
            There are lots of major unsolved problems in the world that need far more people to participate. For example, Wikipedia needs far more people who can write well to write good-quality, up-to-date articles on topics of significance that people of all kinds could benefit from.

            1.2.2.1.2.1.2. Pro:
            ChatGPT is unlikely to be able to automate complex tasks that tend to be more meaningful.

            1.2.2.1.2.1.3. Pro:
            Some have proposed ChatGPT as a text-generating AI may cause some disruption in some sectors of writing industries, shifting labor, although a significant impact of that kind seems unlikely.

          1.2.2.1.2.2. Pro:
          It can or could mean that people can ultimately focus on more meaningful steps of the given work.

            1.2.2.1.2.2.1. Pro:
            Generative AI may raise the importance of ideas, creative thinking, and so on over more mechanical artistic execution, as such AIs can make them easier, more accessible, or, in some cases, largely automate them. For example, one can come up with ideas and/or creatively stitch them together and edit them instead of taking very long times for each idea-to-final-result process.

            1.2.2.1.2.2.2. Pro:
            -> See 1.1.5.4.

          1.2.2.1.2.3. Con:
          Having more leisure time does not equate to engaging in useful activities, and it could even mean people gravitate towards social vices.

            1.2.2.1.2.3.1. Pro:
