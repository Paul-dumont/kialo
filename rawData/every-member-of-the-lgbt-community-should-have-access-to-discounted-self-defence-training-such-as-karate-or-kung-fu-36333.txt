Discussion Name:
Every member of the LGBT community should have access to discounted self defence training (such as Karate or Kung Fu)

1. Thesis:
Every member of the LGBT community should have access to affordable self-defense training.

  1.1. Pro:
  Learning self-defense may lead to a gradual shift in attitude that is causing some to be hostile towards the LGBT community.

    1.1.1. Con:
    If the LBGT community defends itself and hurts their attacker, it's likely that people will misconstrue it as hostility on part of the community itself - which could increase [1] the violence.

    1.1.2. Pro:
    The realisation that it would be fruitless to attack an LGBT individual will lead to a possible change in attitude towards the LGBT community as a whole.

      1.1.2.1. Pro:
      If something is bad for us and there is a penalty or a ramification attached to it, then human behavior [2] suggests that said individual would think twice about engaging in penalising behaviors [3] before even getting into it.

      1.1.2.2. Con:
      A possible change in attitude could only come from education and respect [4], so no person would feel entitled to attack an LGBT individual.

        1.1.2.2.1. Con:
        Education does not undo the attitudes that have been nurtured from early on. Therefore, it does little to curb the attitudes of the attacker(s).

        1.1.2.2.2. Con:
        Education does not allow the offender to naturally respect the victim or his/her attitude towards the victim.

        1.1.2.2.3. Con:
        These aren't entitled attacks as these types of hate crimes occur on instinct and on 'the spur of the moment' type of instances.

        1.1.2.2.4. Con:
        Even if the offender is educated, the respect given will be fake.

        1.1.2.2.5. Pro:
        Education [5] is key in teaching peace and tolerance.

    1.1.3. Con:
    All people have the right to defend themselves in whatever manner they choose no matter what the "community" is.

    1.1.4. Con:
    It could be argued that violence and hostility can hardly change a hostile attitude.

    1.1.5. Pro:
    It will send a strong message to those who have an intention of attacking said LGBT member randomly when publicly spotted.

      1.1.5.1. Con:
      It could cause people with dissenting opinions to refrain from voicing said opinion due to the fear of being counter attacked.

        1.1.5.1.1. Con:
        If LGBT individuals attack someone unduly, they can still be persecuted under the law.

        1.1.5.1.2. Pro:
        This would infringe on their freedom of speech [6].

          1.1.5.1.2.1. Con:
          Hate speech [7] shouldn't be considered under the umbrella of "freedom of speech" as it is discriminatory. (p.17)

        1.1.5.1.3. Con:
        If a member of the LGBTQ+ was attacked for being themselves, they have a right to defend themselves. The goal here is to not have to defend yourself because no one would attack you based on your gender/sexual orientation.

      1.1.5.2. Con:
      Self-defense can lead people to engage in dangerous situations [8].

      1.1.5.3. Pro:
      One of the key elements of self-defense is learning spatial awareness [9], which can prepare a person for long-range attacks.

      1.1.5.4. Con:
      The attacker may have no way of knowing the LGBT member knows self-defense.

      1.1.5.5. Pro:
      In 2007, 1265 [10] hate crimes against members of the LBGT community were reported to the FBI.

        1.1.5.5.1. Con:
        This number is small in comparison to race hate crimes, which make up over 50% [11] of all reported hate crimes in 2007.

        1.1.5.5.2. Pro:
        Anti-LGBT hate crimes [12] have risen more than 300% since 2011 and anti-trans crimes have risen by more than 700% from 2011 to 2020. (p.17).

  1.2. Con:
  In order to access discounted self-defense training, the LGBT community would have to create a membership database.

    1.2.1. Pro:
    Unless there's a membership database for every single LGBT member, the discounted self-defense training could be accessed by non-LGBT members pretending to be part of the community.

    1.2.2. Con:
    There are already many LGBT rights organizations [13].

      1.2.2.1. Con:
      One shouldn't have to be part of an organization

  1.3. Con:
  This puts the responsibility to be safe on the LGBT community, rather than investing in educating the general population.

    1.3.1. Pro:
    Providing safety from violence for all citizens should be a primary responsibility of the state.

      1.3.1.1. Pro:
      The police should take proactive steps [14] to protect those who are targeted more.

    1.3.2. Con:
    It is the responsibility of each individual to take safety precautions for their life. This notion transcends sexual orientation or gender identity.

    1.3.3. Pro:
    If the responsibility to be safe is put on the LGBT community, it could be seen as a sort of victim blaming [15], instead of putting the responsibility on the perpetrators.

      1.3.3.1. Pro:
      Teaching self-defense only to the LGBT community could create the wrong image of them being inferior.

        1.3.3.1.1. Con:
        Putting more focus on the LGBT community on teaching them self-defense is a sign of strength and not an inferiority complex.

        1.3.3.1.2. Con:
        We aren't instilling a message of inferiority or weakness in the LGBT community.

        1.3.3.1.3. Con:
        Teaching self-defense will help the LGBT community better fend for themselves.

        1.3.3.1.4. Pro:
        Only teaching self-defense to the LGBT could stigmatize [16] them and create the image of a victim that needs to learn how to defend themselves.

    1.3.4. Pro:
    Giving access to self-defense training to every member of the LGBTQ community won't make much difference in improving their lives as stereotypes will still exist.

    1.3.5. Con:
    Educating the general public does not protect LGBT members from the attacks of those who do not wish to learn.

    1.3.6. Pro:
    The focus should be on reducing hate against minority groups, rather than pushing minority groups to feel that they must defend themselves because no one else will.

      1.3.6.1. Con:
      No matter how much focus is directed at the reduction of hate towards minority groups, the problem of the attitude of people who hate still exists. It can only be dealt with through empowerment and minority groups being prepared.

      1.3.6.2. Con:
      Without a tangible action which can be uniquely identified, 'targeting' means nothing. Crimes or offenses aren't any worse just for targeting a group, minority or otherwise.

      1.3.6.3. Pro:
      The focus should be put on education and awareness to reduce hate against minority groups [17]. Self-defense would only perpetuate the idea that hate can only be fought with violence.

        1.3.6.3.1. Con:
        The idea of self defense education and training is so that someone can defend themselves against unprovoked attacks and this is the main objective of the thesis.

    1.3.7. Pro:
    Treating people differently based on their sexual orientation is exactly what we are trying to avoid

    1.3.8. Con:
    Education on tolerance and self-defense are not incompatible and can happen simultaneously since both would be beneficial.

    1.3.9. Pro:
    Providing self-defense for the LGBT community does not help to resolve the violence against them.

    1.3.10. Con:
    This would reduce the pressure on governments to introduce reforms and offer greater protections to the LGBT community through legislation.

      1.3.10.1. Pro:
      If successful, this policy would further reduce pressure on the government because there would be less statistical evidence to point out when less (successful) attacks are reported and documented.

      1.3.10.2. Con:
      Governments have a responsibility to protect all citizens [18], therefore it would be unjust to put the responsibility to be safe on the LGBT community.

  1.4. Pro:
  Every LGBT member will be encouraged to defuse, avoid or exercise self-control before defending themselves.

    1.4.1. Pro:
    Self-defense would give tools to the LGBT community to efficiently prevent violence [19]. (p.3)

      1.4.1.1. Pro:
      Self-defense prepares people to efficiently react against any potential attack.

    1.4.2. Pro:
    Training self defence/martial arts has several direct and side effects that might help LGBT persons. When a situation is escalating, being calm is very important. Stay calm if hit by an offender another, Learn how to get out of a situation with minimal or no violence. While being confident that one can defend, it is easier to talk and deescalate without violence.

    1.4.3. Con:
    Learning self-defense is not necessary for this, as it is likely that LGBT people attempt to defuse an attempted attack before it starts.

      1.4.3.1. Pro:
      Everyone should get a discount.

      1.4.3.2. Con:
      The self-awareness ability learned from the self-defense class will allow the LGBT member to predict or sense any attempts of an attacker trying to isolate the victim and then attack them - like what happened with Matthew Shepard [20].

    1.4.4. Con:
    Learning self-defense will only give them the skills to defend themselves in the event of a physical attack.

      1.4.4.1. Pro:
      Some attacks cannot be properly prepared for, such as being arrested and harassed [21] by law enforcement. Defending oneself could lead to more brutal treatment.

        1.4.4.1.1. Pro:
        The trauma suffered can be emotionally distressing, making a person commit suicide [22].

        1.4.4.1.2. Con:
        Self-defense is still needed in the event these individuals experience violence on the street.

      1.4.4.2. Pro:
      Verbal attacks and blatant discrimination can be emotionally harmful, having a detrimental mental effect.

        1.4.4.2.1. Con:
        Self-defense can boost individual confidence, increasing mental resilience [23].

        1.4.4.2.2. Pro:
        Some [24] LGBTQ youth commit suicide due to verbal bullying. Learning self-defense will not help with this.

          1.4.4.2.2.1. Con:
          Self-defense can help people feel part of a group, which can provide a supportive community, a very important element to prevent suicide [25].

          1.4.4.2.2.2. Pro:
          Verbal bullying [26] is more prevalent than physical bullying, therefore resources should focus efforts on diminishing this.

            1.4.4.2.2.2.1. Con:
            Self-defense can teach the victim from shielding themselves from verbal bullying as an added bonus.

      1.4.4.3. Con:
      Learning self-defense may embolden some to initiate a physical assault on their bullies rather than respond to them.

        1.4.4.3.1. Pro:
        Some members may use what they've learned in self-defense as retaliation.

  1.5. Con:
  Someone could be lying about their identity, and take the training for free, and thus abuse the system.

    1.5.1. Con:
    There are risk management strategies in place that will deal with people who do try to cheat the system. Such people will be appropriately dealt with.

  1.6. Pro:
  If every member of the LGBT community has access to affordable self defense training, then it will send a stronger message that it is not okay to attack someone just because they belong to the LGBT community.

    1.6.1. Con:
    Attacking someone unnecessarily is wrong regardless of group affiliation.

  1.7. Con:
  Lbgt activists stand on a platform of equality therefore discounted self defense training should be fair fed to everyone regardless of gender or sexual preference.

    1.7.1. Con:
    LGBT activists are not the question here. It is the LGBT members that are at a higher risk of verbal and physical abuse by homophobes.

    1.7.2. Con:
    Wanting to protect and defend yourself from physical harm when you are more likely to be attacked [27] is a perfectly good reason to offer discounted self defense training to those who can take advantage of it.

  1.8. Con:
  Self defense classes already aren't particulary expensive or hard to find. There's no reason to think making it cheaper would significantly increase the number of LGBT people taking these classes.

  1.9. Con:
  You could say this about ANY minority, and then every minority will be given this and more people who know how to fight more fights happen.

    1.9.1. Con:
    LGBTQ individuals are at an increased risk [28] of being attacked than ANY other minority group.

  1.10. Pro:
  It is imperative that the LGBTQ+ community engages in self defense techniques to protect themselves against unwarranted and legislative persecutions like what is happening in Russia.

  1.11. Pro:
  Engaging in self defense will not naturally lead to violence unless it is being used for malicious intent.

  1.12. Pro:
  Schools and other educational facilities should encourage their LGBTQ+ students to engage in self defense and explain its purpose and provide caviats.

  1.13. Pro:
  The LGBT member will have an increased chance of defending themselves against unfounded or random attacks on them.

    1.13.1. Pro:
    It will boost confidence with the LGBT member and help fight against hatred and self-hatred.

      1.13.1.1. Con:
      Knowing self-defense does not diminish hate, it simply gives those being attacked the ability to fight back.

      1.13.1.2. Pro:
      It will make the haters think twice before attacking - be it on impulse or some other reason.

        1.13.1.2.1. Pro:
        Most hate crimes occur on impulse rather than by use of strategy.

      1.13.1.3. Con:
      Escalating confrontations into physical violence will cause more problems in the long terms.

      1.13.1.4. Pro:
      It will help them try to avoid the situation before taking any action.

      1.13.1.5. Pro:
      Along with the boost in confidence comes self esteem and positive self worthiness.

    1.13.2. Con:
    Unarmed self-defense techniques are less effective and practical, especially for the physically disadvantaged, than being armed.

      1.13.2.1. Pro:
      Some members of the LGBT community cannot physically defend themselves. Offering this will not help these individuals.

        1.13.2.1.1. Pro:
        Many LGBT members have disabilities [29] that prevent them from physically defending themselves against violent attacks.

        1.13.2.1.2. Pro:
        Offering self-defense to this community is not the right solution if everyone cannot utilize this as a resource.

        1.13.2.1.3. Con:
        Self-defense training is not always solely physical, but it is also about learning how to avoid dangerous situations [30].

      1.13.2.2. Con:
      There are many countries where it is illegal to be armed [31].

        1.13.2.2.1. Con:
        There are also countries [32] where same-sex intercourse is illegal, or even a capital offense, so a policy prescription of discounted self-defense training for LGBT people is already disregarding some countries' laws; "arm LGBT people instead" just disregards a few more countries' laws.

        1.13.2.2.2. Con:
        Law does not determine morality; we are discussing a "should" claim, i.e. a moral question.

      1.13.2.3. Pro:
      Guns are often used as an effective means to intimidate [33].

      1.13.2.4. Con:
      Carrying weapons increase the risk of escalating conflicts.

        1.13.2.4.1. Pro:
        The U.S. has higher levels of gun ownership than other countries and as a result, it has more gun deaths than other developed nations [34].

      1.13.2.5. Con:
      Self defense isn't just about physical defense techniques, it is also about the mindset and promoting self confidence and self esteem.

    1.13.3. Pro:
    The LGBT community suffers from recurrent violent acts [35], therefore a solution needs to be put into place.

    1.13.4. Pro:
    Their priority should be how to stay safe and secure with every moment, and with the LGBT community being taught some self-defenses, the need gets uplifted.

    1.13.5. Con:
    It's unlikely to have much of an effect, since you need several years of consistent training to be proficient enough at self defense to use it in a real fight.

    1.13.6. Con:
    There are other ways the LGBT community is attacked. Self-defense will not help in these situations.

      1.13.6.1. Con:
      Because self-defense is all about self-control [36], even online bullying can be handled in a calm, cool, and collective manner once the LGBT member is coached on how to approach online bullying.

      1.13.6.2. Pro:
      An attacker can use a weapon. Knowing self-defense does not prepare a person for this.

        1.13.6.2.1. Pro:
        In Orlando, Florida, a gunman killed 50 people [37] in a gay night club. Self-defense would not have helped.

        1.13.6.2.2. Con:
        Self-defense classes can teach a person how to escape [38] being approached with a weapon.

          1.13.6.2.2.1. Pro:
          Self-defense courses also teach their students how to correctly run away [39] from danger.

      1.13.6.3. Pro:
      LGBTQ young people are 3 times [40] more likely to be bullied online than heterosexual young people. Self-defense will not help in these instances.

        1.13.6.3.1. Con:
        The LGBT youth will be better equipped when dealing with online threats or cyberbullying given the self-control aspect of self-defense training.

          1.13.6.3.1.1. Pro:
          It is likely they will be less likely to lash out online, and instead ignore or delete harassing comments.

        1.13.6.3.2. Pro:
        Educating tolerance and acceptance can be more effective in stopping cyberbullying rather than teaching self-defense.

      1.13.6.4. Con:
      Learning self-defense will allow said LGBT member to defend against weaponised attacks [41] too.

  1.14. Con:
  Every minority group should learn a martial art no matter sexual orientation.

    1.14.1. Con:
    Violence will not solve the underlying reasons behind people attacking others, particularly members of the LGBT community.

      1.14.1.1. Pro:
      Crimes against the LGBT community are motivated [42] by prejudices and biases towards gender identity and sexual orientation.

      1.14.1.2. Pro:
      Hate crimes against the LGBT community are driven by a need to punish [43] those who defy gender norms.

      1.14.1.3. Pro:
      In order to tackle these beliefs, societal beliefs [44] need to be reformed so that people become more tolerant.

      1.14.1.4. Con:
      During the time such reforms occur, violence against the LGBT community will continue [45]. Self-defense training will give people a chance to defend themselves in the meanwhile. (p.10)

      1.14.1.5. Con:
      Some actions are already being taken to put a stop at homophobia [46]. However, violence against the LGBT is still present [47], therefore the need for this community to defend themselves from it.

    1.14.2. Pro:
    Anyone could be violently assaulted at any time. Everyone learning will decrease the chances of people being attacked.

      1.14.2.1. Con:
      If everyone learned, it would make for a more even fight but it will not guarantee non-violence.

      1.14.2.2. Con:
      The assaulters will also learn self-defense and will know how to fight back.

      1.14.2.3. Con:
      It would not decrease attack, but rather it would change the types of weapons a determined assaulter would use against the person they are attacking.

        1.14.2.3.1. Pro:
        Someone intent on harming another person may choose to use a gun or other weapon rather than fighting.

          1.14.2.3.1.1. Con:
          Guns and other dangerous weapons are not legal nor available in all the countries [48], therefore, fighting might still be the prefered method of attack in many places.

            1.14.2.3.1.1.1. Con:
            It is considered that around 46% of all 857 million guns [49] in civilian hands around the world belonged to Americans, so in the U.S, guns might be more likely to be used in fights.

            1.14.2.3.1.1.2. Pro:
            Japan, is considered to have a “zero-tolerance [50]” regarding gun ownership which has resulted in low rates of gun crime.

          1.14.2.3.1.2. Pro:
          Gun violence [51] is considered to be a threat that the LGBT is currently enduring

        1.14.2.3.2. Con:
        Self-defense also teaches techniques to prevent and avoid attacks with weapons [52].

      1.14.2.4. Pro:
      Self-defense has many great benefits [53] that everyone could benefit from.

      1.14.2.5. Pro:
      Self-defense training decrease women’s risk of assault [54], which shows that if everyone would learn it, it could also decrease the chances of attacks.

    1.14.3. Pro:
    A person's sexual orientation neither guarantees that they will be a victim nor precludes them from being an aggressor.

      1.14.3.1. Con:
      A person's sexual orientation can sometimes lead to prejudice and violence as the LGBT community is more likely to be target of hate crimes [55] than any other minority group.

        1.14.3.1.1. Pro:
        The LGBTQ+ community is at a higher risk of being victims of physical and sexual assault [56], as well as harassment, bullying, and hate crimes.

      1.14.3.2. Pro:
      Offering discounted self-defense training to all LGBT members could be sending the message that certain sexual orientations guarantee that they will always be victims, which could have negative consequences on the LGBT community [57].

    1.14.4. Pro:
    There should be no privilege between non-minority groups and minority groups alike. The LGBT community can use fundraising and other means to provide the classes themselves.

    1.14.5. Pro:
    It'd be more beneficial to give such privileges to those who have visible societal disadvantages like race/disabilities.

      1.14.5.1. Pro:
      Homophobic people don't really know if someone is gay without being told that. Arguing that they will is enforcing a stereotype upon the community, which is toxic in itself.

        1.14.5.1.1. Pro:
        The LGBT community doesn't need to be held more 'at risk', because it's not something that's obvious.

    1.14.6. Pro:
    Making special exceptions for the LGBT community will further stigmatize them.

      1.14.6.1. Pro:
      Allowing for discounted self-defense training for the LGBT community could send the wrong message to newer generations, as they might repress their sexual orientation out of fear [58].

      1.14.6.2. Con:
      Real change for general acceptance and tolerance in any given community can only occur if attitudes towards the LGBT community change.

      1.14.6.3. Con:
      Self-defense doesn't stigmatize as it teaches self-control [59], which can help people to better face different types of situations.

    1.14.7. Con:
    The LBGT community is attacked more [60] than heterosexual people even when their population sizes are accounted for (p. 5). It is particularly important for them to procure self-defense training.

      1.14.7.1. Con:
      The female heterosexual [61] population also suffers from violence and would benefit from self-defense training.

      1.14.7.2. Pro:
      The motivation behind 1 out of 5 [62] hate crimes is prejudice against the LBGT community.

    1.14.8. Pro:
    The discounting of the total cost of any self-defense class or session might be discriminatory when it comes to heterosexual people or other minority participants who are interested in learning self-defense for themselves.

      1.14.8.1. Pro:
      Well, we shouldn't see each other as higher than each other no matter what we are. So it would be unfair if they got discounts for having a different sexuality.

      1.14.8.2. Pro:
      About 35% of women globally and 70% of women nationally have experienced [63] some form of physical or sexual violence. Offering such a discount to LGBT members prioritizes this group over women.

      1.14.8.3. Pro:
      Fifty-nine percent [64] of hate crimes are committed against people of a certain racial, ethnic or ancestral minority, while only 16.7% are committed against those of a particular sexual orientation. Discounted self-defense should therefore be prioritized to racial minorities.

      1.14.8.4. Con:
      Offering a discount to the LGBT community is positive discrimination [65] because this group experiences violence at a disproportionately high rate in comparison to other minority groups

      1.14.8.5. Pro:
      This prioritizes the protection of one vulnerable group over another, which is unfair.

        1.14.8.5.1. Con:
        In patriarchal heteronormative [66] societies, heterosexual people can't be considered as a vulnerable group.

        1.14.8.5.2. Pro:
        Violence against vulnerable groups should become a public concern [67] to avoid any unfair treatment. (p.59)
