Discussion Name:
Should there be a test for people to pass before they can vote?

1. Thesis:
Should there be a test for people to pass before they can vote?

  1.1. Pro:
  politics is more complex than what most people assume

  1.2. Con:
  even ignorant people can have good ideas

    1.2.1. Con:
    Ignorant people are unlikely to understand the full range of consequences for specific policies.

  1.3. Con:
  It's already too much work to take out a vote

  1.4. Con:
  excluding people is not democratic

    1.4.1. Pro:
    In a democracy, voting is a fundamental human right [1] given to every citizen of the voting age. Instituting a test is an infringement upon this right.

  1.5. Pro:
  you can only form an opinion when you know the facts

  1.6. Pro:
  too many people vote without knowledge

    1.6.1. Pro:
    People's voting choices are sometimes influenced by misinformation generated for political propaganda [2].

Sources:
