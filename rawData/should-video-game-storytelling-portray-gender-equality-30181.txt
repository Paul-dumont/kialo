Discussion Name:
Should video game storytelling portray gender equality?

1. Thesis:
Video game storytelling should portray gender equality.

  1.1. Con:
  Video games are an art-form. Homogenizing that art by portraying unrealistic degrees of gender equality at the expense of realism lessens its visceral impact on the viewer/participant, and ultimately waters it down.

  1.2. Con:
  Certain historical games portray an inherently unequal society. Games should not shy away from exploring these themes or historical accuracy.

    1.2.1. Pro:
    Acknowledgment of inequality is a prerequisite to fighting for actual equality. Limiting stories to only portray worlds in which the problem has been solved is akin to whites pretending that racism is solved in America because they are color-blind.

    1.2.2. Pro:
    According to historians [1], video games have played in important function in educating gamers about the events of the past, and they increasingly see video games as legitimate sources for historical engagement.

      1.2.2.1. Con:
      Video games can educate gamers by exploring historical counterfactuals instead of portraying historical accuracy which acts as a harmful justification for fewer female characters.

        1.2.2.1.1. Pro:
        The game Prey [2] explores an alternate history in which President Kennedy doesn't get assassinated, leading to tons of funding into space exploration and a US-USSR alliance against alien forces.

        1.2.2.1.2. Pro:
        The game We Happy Few [3] deals with an alternate end to World War 2 where, after a successful invasion of England by the German Empire, people of a small town in England take a happy pill to cope with and forget horrible memories.

        1.2.2.1.3. Pro:
        The game Wolfenstein: The New Order [4] deals with an alternate history where the Nazi Regime wins World War 2 and war veteran Blazkowicz tries to stop the Nazis from ruling over the world.

        1.2.2.1.4. Pro:
        The game Stalker: Shadow of Chernobyl [5] delves into an alternate history where a second explosion goes off in the exclusion zone around the site of the 1986 Chernobyl nuclear power plant disaster, bending the place even further out of shape.

        1.2.2.1.5. Pro:
        The game Freedom Fighters [6] deals with an alternate history in which Russia ends World War 2 by developing the first atomic bomb, dropping it on Berlin, becoming the world's most dominant super-power. and, sixty years later, invading the USA.

    1.2.3. Pro:
    The Handmaid's Tale [7], an award-winning TV series which portrays a dystopian world of extreme gender inequality, has helped [8] bring important women's issues to the public discussion. Video games that portray similarly unsavoury periods from human history are also likely to elicit important public discussions.

      1.2.3.1. Pro:
      The Handmaid's Tale was able to elicit discussion [9] on important cultural issues today, such as women's reproductive rights, by depicting the most extreme logical endpoint of conservative arguments against female autonomy. Video games can make similar use of dystopia to drive forward public discourse on important issues.

    1.2.4. Con:
    Not all games are bound by history.

  1.3. Pro:
  The video game industry would be more profitable if video games portrayed gender equality.

    1.3.1. Con:
    As the video game industry is made up of profit-driven corporations, the fact that they're not already portraying gender equality suggests that it would not increase profits to do so.

    1.3.2. Pro:
    By catering equally to both male and female gamers as opposed to neglecting one gender in favor of another, the video game industry would be able to better capitalize on both male and female demographics.

      1.3.2.1. Pro:
      Analogously, audiences have responded [10] positively to the greater inclusion of minorities and more diversity overall in Hollywood. Video games, also part of the media industry, are likely to receive a similarly positive response with a change in storytelling.

        1.3.2.1.1. Pro:
        The second annual Hollywood Diversity Report by UCLA’s Ralph J. Bunche Center for African American Studies revealed [11] that consumers want more diversity in TV and movies.

        1.3.2.1.2. Pro:
        There is significant overlap between Hollywood and the gaming industry.

          1.3.2.1.2.1. Pro:
          Several games are the byproduct of Hollywood movie tie-ins. [12]

          1.3.2.1.2.2. Pro:
          There is an audience overlap [13] between Hollywood and the gaming industry; the most frequently targeted demographic for both is young men.

          1.3.2.1.2.3. Pro:
          Video-games have an increasing impact [14] on the technical aspects of movie-making and creative aspects of story-telling.

      1.3.2.2. Con:
      There are stark differences in target demographics that need to be considered when designing a game.

        1.3.2.2.1. Pro:
        Research [15] has shown how much more important female protagonists in games were to women than to men.

      1.3.2.3. Pro:
      The CEO of the Entertainment Software Association, Michael Gallagher, stated that [16] "the industry is strongest when it appeals to the broadest possible audience", and emphasized the importance of making inclusive games for women as well as men.

      1.3.2.4. Pro:
      Gender equality can significantly contribute to the creative succes of video games by creating original and thoughtful characters [17] that fit convincingly inside their game universe [18], thus generating more profit.

    1.3.3. Con:
    Games with female protagonists don't sell as well as those with male protagonists.

      1.3.3.1. Pro:
      In 2012 [19], EEDAR revealed findings that showed that games with female-only leads sold significantly less copies.

        1.3.3.1.1. Pro:
        Male-only hero games sold 25 per cent [20] better than those with an optional female hero, and 75 per cent better than whose with a female-only hero.

          1.3.3.1.1.1. Con:
          EEDAR also found [21] that games that included an option of selecting a playable female protagonist received better user ratings than those without such an option.

        1.3.3.1.2. Con:
        Statistics on the success of male protagonists compared to female protagonists would have been very different if similar budgets were used to make them.

          1.3.3.1.2.1. Pro:
          These figures reflect the fact that games with female-only heroes got half the spending [22] of games that had optional female characters, and only 40 per cent of the marketing budget of male-led games. Fewer sales are therefore a result of lesser marketing spending.

      1.3.3.2. Con:
      If developers assume that "games with female protagonists can't sell as well as those with male protagonists," and make storytelling and other choices on that basis, they will perpetuate a self-fulfilling prophecy and leave gaming trapped in a cycle of uneven and sexist representation in video games. In other words, if developers act based on that assumption and never take the risk, we will never truly know how well games featuring female protagonists will sell.

        1.3.3.2.1. Con:
        Because games with female protagonists haven't proven to be as profitable as games with male protagonists, they present a risk for video game producers. It's therefore financially more sensible to take approaches that have proven to be highly profitable.

      1.3.3.3. Pro:
      Jean-Max Morris, Creative Director on Remember Me, claims [23] to have been told by publishers that they wouldn’t sign the game because of its female lead.

      1.3.3.4. Pro:
      Chris Perna, Art Director for Epic Games [24], said that female lead characters would be "tough to justify [25]" on the basis of sales figures.

        1.3.3.4.1. Pro:
        Chris Perna, as an art director, has to look at costs and sales potential before making a decision on something. This is because these are the main tools for a company to analyze in order to make the best calls on what is going to be profitable for that business. To that end it's reasonable that they don't do something (even if it's good) because it'll doesn't have adequate sales potential.

    1.3.4. Con:
    Creating female characters may require a lot of extra production work, thus reducing profits because of the added cost.

      1.3.4.1. Pro:
      Ubisoft Technical Director James Therien said female assassins were abandoned in the Assassin's Creed series because of the additional work. Female assassins would have "doubled the work [26]" for Ubisoft as it would double the animations, the voices and visual assets.

      1.3.4.2. Pro:
      Byron Atkinson-Jones [27], an indie developer for Uncharted, stated that there are several additional costs to adding a female protagonist, such as "mesh data, texture data and possibly mo-cap data" which need to be duplicated.

        1.3.4.2.1. Con:
        Byron Atkinson-Jones then continues saying, "but nothing is really that difficult to do in games, it’s all down to resources, planning and willingness to do it. If the designer stipulates that the main character can be male or female from the start then the development team would build it that way.”

      1.3.4.3. Con:
      Mike Bitchell, designer of Volume, stated that [28] adding a female protagonist would take less than two weeks of work.

    1.3.5. Pro:
    Women represent a growing market [29] which is lucrative for companies to target. The amount of women in high paying jobs is increasing along with the amount of disposable income enjoyed by women.

      1.3.5.1. Pro:
      In the US, women spend more than $70 billion on home improvement purchases. This amount has risen by $16 billion [30] over the last 16 years as the industry has begun to more successfully target [31] products to women. If video game companies can tap into this market it could be very lucrative for them.

      1.3.5.2. Pro:
      Research shows that women control over 85% [32] of the spending decisions in a household.

      1.3.5.3. Pro:
      After making the shift to target female consumers, Porsche sales rose 31% [33] in 2013, which was the biggest gain for any car brand.

    1.3.6. Con:
    If this gender equality quota was implemented, most games currently in development would fail to meet it.

      1.3.6.1. Pro:
      This could obliterate entire franchises by big developers like Nintendo or Sony.

        1.3.6.1.1. Pro:
        The Super Mario series relies upon the player to be a hero saving a princess, to change that would make the whole series defunct.

          1.3.6.1.1.1. Con:
          Titles like SMB2 did not have that premise, showing that the series could have different stories.

        1.3.6.1.2. Pro:
        God of War is a series based heavily on Greek [34], (possibly) Roman, and other ancient cultures [35] - to add a gender quota would erase the cultural credibility that the games had.

        1.3.6.1.3. Con:
        Half Life: Alyx [36] was the first game in the series to successfully have a female protagonist.

        1.3.6.1.4. Pro:
        Assassin's Creed is deeply rooted in history, implementing a gender quota would tarnish the educational reputation the series upholds.

          1.3.6.1.4.1. Con:
          Assassin's Creed China had a female protagonist, but sadly did not have much approval from fans [37].

        1.3.6.1.5. Con:
        Franchises run by big developers can afford to adapt their games as needed to ensure that they meet the new requirements.

      1.3.6.2. Pro:
      Even old games will be restricted by this because developers can't add new content as easily as they did before.

        1.3.6.2.1. Pro:
        Pre-2000s titles would have the worst treatment, as when they may be re-mastered, the entire premise might be demolished.

      1.3.6.3. Pro:
      The more you add restrictions to a game the harder it will be for the developer to make new game or release games that are already created.

    1.3.7. Con:
    The video game industry would not necessarily see a net increase in profits if it portrayed more gender equality.

      1.3.7.1. Pro:
      Women tend to overwhelmingly play [38] mobile games, which are often free of charge, as opposed to video games. Because of this, increasing the number of female gamers would not necessarily cause an increase in profits.

      1.3.7.2. Pro:
      Studies have found that 25% of male video gamers [39] prefer to play with those of their own gender. Making games more female focused might deter men that currently playing video games from keeping it up.

        1.3.7.2.1. Pro:
        This number rises to 47% of gamers [40] that play more than 10 hours per week.

      1.3.7.3. Pro:
      Women tend to be more causal about games [41], investing less time in games than men. This means that focusing on female gamers may not necessary increase female engagement and profits for the industry.

      1.3.7.4. Con:
      Studies have found that 1/3 of women [42] who regularly play mobile games are willing to spend money on either games or in-game loot or gear. Portraying more gender equality and increasing the number of female gamers will increase profits.

    1.3.8. Con:
    Commodification results in storytelling depicting a highly problematic and masculinized version of femininity instead of a celebration of gender diversity.

      1.3.8.1. Pro:
      Feminine characteristics should be portrayed as they are instead of assigning male traits to women.

        1.3.8.1.1. Con:
        This would not be possible for a number of video games.

          1.3.8.1.1.1. Pro:
          In The Last of Us, the main characters are mostly women. There is not much room to develop them beyond traits that would be considered traditionally masculine, both in terms of physical traits and their 'choices'.

            1.3.8.1.1.1.1. Con:
            If the world of 'The Last of Us' actually came to pass (ie: where brute strength, combat skills, etc were the only way to survive) then the majority of women would find themselves at a huge disadvantage and would more than likely end up as chattel. Games with worlds as implausible as "The Last of Us" should thus not be made.

              1.3.8.1.1.1.1.1. Con:
              Games with implausible worlds -> should not be made, is a non-sequitur

              1.3.8.1.1.1.1.2. Con:
              It does not follow from brute strength being the primary fitness trait for an environment that women would end up as chattel.

        1.3.8.1.2. Con:
        Many archetypally male traits are virtues that people of all genders should aspire to, such as courage, and female characters that are intended to be aspirational examples for a female audience should have such traits.

          1.3.8.1.2.1. Pro:
          This is true but courage is not solely a male characteristic. There are aspirational characteristics that can be attributed to protagonists of video games that don't have to be gender-specific.

        1.3.8.1.3. Con:
        Many archetypally male traits, such as strength, are also heroic traits that will naturally be associated with protagonists, so a female videogame protagonist will and should tend to have such traits.

      1.3.8.2. Pro:
      It is important to accurately depict gender in order to ensure that young, impressionable minds are not harmed by unrealistic expectations.

  1.4. Pro:
  It is incumbent on both developers and fans to ensure that storytelling portrays gender equality.

    1.4.1. Con:
    Video games, like all creative endeavors, should only ever contain what the creators want them to contain. No external demands or restrictions apart from critiques, child- and youth protection should be legally enforceable on them, regardless of content and message.

      1.4.1.1. Con:
      Creators are already restricted by financial and marketing considerations, much of which are assessed [43] using big data and analytics - unlimited creative license does not exist.

    1.4.2. Pro:
    Video game is a co-creation process and fans can sway developers to modify their game even after it has been released.

      1.4.2.1. Pro:
      This was seen in the Mass Effect 3: Extended Cut [44]'s ending, which was added after fan pushback.

      1.4.2.2. Con:
      Fans have very little control over affecting the narrative.

        1.4.2.2.1. Con:
        The profits and reviews of a game can reveal a lot to the developers about how to best improve their games.

    1.4.3. Con:
    -> See 1.4.2.2.

    1.4.4. Pro:
    Fanmade videos (eg. Source Film Maker [45]) and mods (e.g. Nexusmods [46]) can shape the conversation.

      1.4.4.1. Pro:
      These narratives can drive a more or less gender-equal narrative (through things such as narrative revamp or by reducing sexist content respectively).

      1.4.4.2. Con:
      Many [47] mods are sexy mods [48] or just modes that promotes sex [49] in the old-fashioned way, where the men have agency and the female characters are less well-rounded and lack agency.

    1.4.5. Con:
    There is no legal obligation for fans or the developers to do this.

      1.4.5.1. Con:
      Legal obligations are not necessary to make something incumbent upon someone.

        1.4.5.1.1. Con:
        Legal obligations are the only kind which can truly impose an obligation on someone.

        1.4.5.1.2. Pro:
        Moral obligations can make actions incumbent upon someone.

          1.4.5.1.2.1. Con:
          -> See 1.4.5.1.1.

    1.4.6. Con:
    The right to consume whatever type of product you like should be at the heart of the industry because it is people's choices that determine what products are made in the first place.

      1.4.6.1. Pro:
      The only incumbent that the industry has is towards the consumers. Morals have no place in a game because in the end it's up to the gamers to decide what to play and thus they shape what games are important to make.

        1.4.6.1.1. Con:
        While what is and is not morally relevant is disputed, "morals have no place in a game" is clearly absurd; a game that drops flashing lights on epileptic people without warning is clearly morally wrong, for instance.

      1.4.6.2. Con:
      It's unclear how ensuring more video games portray gender equality prevents one from consuming "whatever type of product you want."

        1.4.6.2.1. Con:
        There's no fair way to ensure gender equality in future games without restriction people's choices, and their ability to play more gender-specific games.

          1.4.6.2.1.1. Pro:
          It is natural to assume that video games have limited amounts of time and resources to spend on a game. This means that male gamers could easily come to the conclusion that if video game developers spend more resources on including women, they'll have less to spend on men.

          1.4.6.2.1.2. Pro:
          It becomes harder to consume the type of product you want as resources are diverted to ensure gender equality is portrayed when developing video games.

            1.4.6.2.1.2.1. Con:
            That more or the same number of products of the kind you want may not be produced does not stop you from consuming the existing products of the sort you desire, or consuming the few that will inevitably still be produced.

        1.4.6.2.2. Con:
        -> See 1.4.6.2.1.2.

    1.4.7. Con:
    People don't have the right to decide what is and isn't 'moral' for others, especially if what they enjoy doesn't harm others.

      1.4.7.1. Pro:
      A good example of this is during the Prohibition Period [50] when Christian groups successfully lobbied for the banning of alcohol for 'moral' reasons.

    1.4.8. Con:
    -> See 1.4.6.1.

  1.5. Con:
  The overall quality of the storytelling might suffer as a result of video games trying to portray gender equality.

    1.5.1. Pro:
    In order to introduce gender equality, many franchises would have to completely overhaul their storylines as their games are predominantly focused around the stories of male characters.

      1.5.1.1. Pro:
      In new games famous characters such as Master Chief [51], Desmond Miles [52] and Geralt of Rivia [53] would have to be retired or have their storylines reduced or changed to add equally important female protagonists.

        1.5.1.1.1. Con:
        Master Chief has long been accompanied by Cortana [54], whose entire existence is equal, if not greater, in importance in the story to Master Chief.

        1.5.1.1.2. Con:
        Master Chief was handpicked and created by Dr. Halsey [55], making her even more important in the overall storyline.

        1.5.1.1.3. Con:
        Desmond Miles's story ends in Assassin's Creed III [56]. New assassins in future Assassin's Creed instalments could easily be female.

          1.5.1.1.3.1. Con:
          Subsequent installments [57] of Assassin's Creed have still used male characters as primary protagonists. This suggests that there are other important factors which leads game developers such as Ubisoft to continue to using male protagonists.

        1.5.1.1.4. Con:
        The Witcher 3 [58] contained numerous female protagonists. Retiring Geralt, the primary male protagonist, or changing his storyline would not be needed to make a new game with a female player character.

          1.5.1.1.4.1. Con:
          The Witcher 3 usually show the majority of the female cast down to specific chords. They have just a few elite women capable of doing other things and being accepted by men but they usually have to give up much more than men.

    1.5.2. Pro:
    Video games should not seek to portray gender equality but rather gender equity [59].

    1.5.3. Pro:
    In trying to balance the representation of gender in a video game, the developers might change the gender of characters who were originally intended to be male, without changing the character's lines of dialogue or character traits. This could make the character seem out of place.

      1.5.3.1. Pro:
      Certain lines of dialogue might not work as well when delivered from a different gender.

        1.5.3.1.1. Con:
        Unless the dialogue that remains unchanged is "I'm a man", no line of dialogue is really particular to a specific gender.

      1.5.3.2. Pro:
      A character with traits that are generally associated with a different gender might be poorly received by players and feel unrelatable.

        1.5.3.2.1. Con:
        No particular traits are exclusively assignable to any particular gender except identifying as that gender.

          1.5.3.2.1.1. Con:
          This isn't taken into account the world lore/history of the game.

      1.5.3.3. Con:
      Role Playing and many other games [60] generally allow gamers to choose or switch between male and female protagonists. This largely results in the same lines and character traits, but doesn't negatively affect the quality of storytelling.

        1.5.3.3.1. Con:
        Most games aren't structured in a way that means that you can easily switch between male and female characters. For example, with the Call of Duty game series.

          1.5.3.3.1.1. Con:
          Given that games like Call of Duty don't really depend on the gender of main character, it wouldn't be difficult to allow the player to choose between genders or even switch in future games.

        1.5.3.3.2. Pro:
        The Mass [61] Effect [62] Trilogy allowed you to choose between MaleShep and FemaleShep, with mostly similar lines and actions (with some adjustments where necessary as wholly dependent on gender) and the story quality largely unchanged between the two options.

        1.5.3.3.3. Pro:
        The [63] Fallout [64] series [65] is one [66] example [67] of a game series that gives players a lot of freedom in selecting gender and in how gendered characters are played by for instance not having gender restrictions on clothing.

    1.5.4. Pro:
    Creative narratives and strong stories would be second line to games because of the reactions taking priority. Creating and showing biases in the game would become taboo.

  1.6. Pro:
  Portraying gender equality in video games may make future games less violent which is beneficial.

    1.6.1. Pro:
    Violence and aggression are recurring themes in most video games, which are conventionally masculine traits and are explicit appeals to male gamers [68]. (p. 43)

      1.6.1.1. Pro:
      According to Electronic Entertainment Design and Research (EEDR), around 30% of female gamers [69] play violent games like Call of Duty and Halo, as of 2013, whereas nearly all young men [70] play some violent video games. (p. 292)

      1.6.1.2. Pro:
      Data by Istanbul University [71] shows a positive correlation between masculinity and the endorsement of violence. (p. 259)

    1.6.2. Con:
    Violence in video games does not lead to violence in real life.

      1.6.2.1. Pro:
      Studies have found [72] no link between video games and aggressive thought formation.

        1.6.2.1.1. Pro:
        A new study published [73] by Oxford University demonstrated that there is no link between violent video games and aggression in teenagers.

        1.6.2.1.2. Pro:
        One review of research by the American Psychological Association found [74] that people who played violent video games were slightly more likely to engage in aggressive behavior. However, the APA said playing games was not enough to lead to violent behavior.

          1.6.2.1.2.1. Pro:
          Even actions classified as 'slightly aggressive' in the study were very minor actions (actions like playing a loud sound that people they were competing against could hear over an audio system) that very few people would consider violent.

      1.6.2.2. Pro:
      Statements linking video games to social problems have [75] historically been politically motivated.

      1.6.2.3. Con:
      Multiple researches and studies link exposure to violent video games as a cause for players becoming more aggressive.

        1.6.2.3.1. Pro:
        The World Health Organization recently decided [76] to add "gaming disorder" to its official list of mental health conditions, stating that gaming behavior could qualify as problematic if it interferes significantly in other areas of people's lives.

          1.6.2.3.1.1. Con:
          The same source in the parent notes that youth violence has fallen as the consumption of video games has risen.

        1.6.2.3.2. Pro:
        A 2014 peer-reviewed study [77] in the Journal of the American Medical Association found that playing violent video games habitually increases long-term aggressive behaviour.

        1.6.2.3.3. Pro:
        A 2014 study [78] published in Psychology of Popular Media Culture found that 90% of paediatricians and 67% of parents agreed or strongly agreed that violent video games can increase aggressive behaviour among children.

          1.6.2.3.3.1. Con:
          The findings of these studies are based on people's perception of what creates aggressive behaviour, not actual determined causes. The citation of these studies, then, has little to no bearing on whether exposure to violent video games actually cause players to become more violent and aggressive.

        1.6.2.3.4. Pro:
        The American Psychological Association (APA) lists violent video games as a risk factor [79] for aggressive behavior.

      1.6.2.4. Pro:
      Games are already split up into adult games and children-friendly games. Showing violence is also a way to show the consequences of it.

    1.6.3. Pro:
    An increase in female characters in violent video games, resulting from the portrayal of gender equality may make future games more likely to reduce violence.

      1.6.3.1. Pro:
      War-themed games that focus more on violent exclusively-male armed combat could transform narratives in future games to focus on female contributions during wars.

        1.6.3.1.1. Pro:
        Focusing on the significant [80] contribution of women as wartime nurses could not only allow such narratives to move away from the excessive use of violence but also allow them to portray its harms.

        1.6.3.1.2. Pro:
        Showing women doing jobs [81] during the war which were vacated by men (e.g. labour intensive work in factories) who had gone to fight in the war, apart from portraying gender equality, may reduce the use of excessive violence in war-themed games.

      1.6.3.2. Pro:
      Since men are stereotypically thought of as being more violent than women, female characters replacing male characters could mean less violent games.

      1.6.3.3. Pro:
      Since violence against women is socially more aversive [82] than violence against men, an increase in female characters may make future games less violent.

        1.6.3.3.1. Pro:
        Punishments for sexual and gender based violence [83] are either generally more severe [84] than other types of violence or there are increasing calls for them to be.

        1.6.3.3.2. Pro:
        Under international humanitarian law, women are accorded special protection status [85] over men in cases of war and conflict.

        1.6.3.3.3. Pro:
        Researches have argued that one reason for the lack of military women serving on the front line is that society values [86] their life and safety over that of men.

        1.6.3.3.4. Con:
        For gender equality, society should be working to stop the societal prejudice that sees violence against men and women differently, not use it for a completely different purpose.

      1.6.3.4. Con:
      It is also possible that game developers will increase the number of female characters without making the games any less violent.

    1.6.4. Con:
    Game developers have no financial incentive to reduce violence in their games given how profitable [87] they are.

      1.6.4.1. Pro:
      Games such as Heavy Rain [88] are a massive success.

      1.6.4.2. Con:
      Whilst a large number of women play video games, few of them play violent video games. Reducing the amount of violence by increasing gender equality may help companies increase the consumer base for these games.

        1.6.4.2.1. Pro:
        According to data from a survey conducted by the Gamer Motivation Profile, only 7% [89] of female video gamers from over 270,000 gamers worldwide play First Person Shooter games.

      1.6.4.3. Pro:
      Video gaming franchises have no reason to make future editions of their games less violent, as their customers play these games because of the current level of violence they contain and would expect the same standard from video games moving forward.

        1.6.4.3.1. Con:
        Consumers who are used to playing a more violent version of the game can continue playing older editions of the franchise.

      1.6.4.4. Pro:
      There are violent video games that have female protagonists. Violence is profitable. No matter the gender, it will still be profitable.

        1.6.4.4.1. Pro:
        The recent games in the Tomb Raider [90] video game series, which feature Lara Croft as the main protagonist, are mature-rated [91] (for people 17 and older [92]) because of the graphic violence [93].

        1.6.4.4.2. Pro:
