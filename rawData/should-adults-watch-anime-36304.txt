Discussion Name:
Should adults watch anime?

1. Thesis:
Adults should not watch anime.

  1.1. Con:
  This comes from ignorance: only people who don't watch anime think this way

  1.2. Con:
  Many anime, like Vinland Saga [1], have better writing and plots than most live action moves in the US today.

  1.3. Pro:
  Anime are cartoons, and cartoons are for children.

    1.3.1. Con:
    Anime do not have upper age limit restrictions.

    1.3.2. Con:
    Not all Western animation is suitable for children either.

      1.3.2.1. Pro:
      Animes are wide: some animes are for children, and some are for more mature audiences.

        1.3.2.1.1. Pro:
        Many anime are completely inappropriate for children to watch.

          1.3.2.1.1.1. Pro:
          Anime, i.e. Japanese animation, includes hentai, i.e. pornographic Japanese animation, which obviously children should not be exposed to.

          1.3.2.1.1.2. Pro:
          There is a specific sub-category of anime, hentai [2], that is pornographic in nature.

        1.3.2.1.2. Pro:
        Many anime shows and movies have adult themes.

          1.3.2.1.2.1. Pro:
          1995's Ghost in the Shell [3], for example, is one of the most famous anime and discusses philosophical questions like what makes us human.

            1.3.2.1.2.1.1. Pro:
            The violence portrayed in this film makes it unsuitable for children.

          1.3.2.1.2.2. Pro:
          Anime features women in sexually suggestive attire.

          1.3.2.1.2.3. Pro:
          Many anime are rated R [4] due to violence, language, and sexual themes.

          1.3.2.1.2.4. Pro:
          Seinen [5] anime, which means 'young man' in Japanese, is targeted towards young adult men and often include adult themes not suitable for young viewers.

            1.3.2.1.2.4.1. Pro:
            Seinen anime is targeted towards young men, ages 18 to 40 [6].

          1.3.2.1.2.5. Pro:
          Anime shows like 'Beastars [7]' are animated, but address and portray violence and overt sexuality.

            1.3.2.1.2.5.1. Pro:
            Although children may be drawn to the 'cuteness' of the animal characters of the show, the series itself depicts animals being killed, having sex, and profanity.

        1.3.2.1.3. Con:
        Having shows that would appeal to an adult audience does not mean adults should watch them.

          1.3.2.1.3.1. Pro:
          Many shows sexualize minors. This may encourage pedophilia.

            1.3.2.1.3.1.1. Pro:
            In response to their popularity, Josei Kosei [8] (school girl) cafes are opening all over Japan and abroad that showcase underage 'maids' dressed as young schoolgirls and cater to adult men.

      1.3.2.2. Pro:
      South Park [9] is definitely not well suited to young children.

        1.3.2.2.1. Pro:
        South Park often touches on topics, like masturbation, sex and menstruation that clearly aren't suitable to kids that haven't been properly introduced to these topics.

    1.3.3. Con:
    Cartoons are not just for children. Adult cartoons exist.

    1.3.4. Con:
    A cartoon is simply a form of illustration. Its content can be tailored to anyone.

    1.3.5. Con:
    Cartoons are meant to be humorous, while many anime portrays darker elements of human life.

  1.4. Con:
  Many anime deal with adult subjects like death, coming to terms with ones morals, one's place in society and finding redemption such as Psycho-Pass [10] and Area 86.

  1.5. Con:
  The Anime horror section [11] is also very popular which would appeal to many adults. Some anime deal with politics as well.

  1.6. Pro:
  Many anime shows feature school-age children and the challenges they face. It is likely adults would not be interested in this.

    1.6.1. Con:
    That adults would not be interested is empirically falsified whenever an adult watches and enjoys media with school-age protagonists.

    1.6.2. Con:
    Whether people wouldn't want to watch anime is somewhat separate from whether they should; if one is telling an adult who does watch anime that they should not, it is a non-argument.

    1.6.3. Pro:
    Schools are a prevalent feature of many anime. So much so that some sites rank the most famous anime school uniforms [12].

  1.7. Pro:
  Anime creators are targeting children

    1.7.1. Pro:
    You won't grow up if you watch too much anime

    1.7.2. Con:
    Some anime creators target adults rather than children.

      1.7.2.1. Pro:
      In the US, the numbers of adult anime are increasing [13].

    1.7.3. Con:
    This is a false stereotype.

      1.7.3.1. Pro:
      The differences [14] in Japanese and Western culture attribute to anime being perceived as something only children should watch.

  1.8. Con:
  Some adults enjoy anime. They should, therefore, watch it.

    1.8.1. Pro:
    Anime is no different to other forms of entertainment that adults enjoy such as other genres of television.

    1.8.2. Pro:
    Sometime adults need some fun too, and watching anime is fun. It's relaxing just from how the animations look; it's cool and fascinating.

    1.8.3. Pro:
    Twenty-five percent [15] of subscribers to Crunchyroll, an online anime streaming service, are over the age of 35. As adults, they can watch whatever they want.
