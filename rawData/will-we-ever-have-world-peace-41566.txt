Discussion Name:
Will we ever have world peace?

1. Thesis:
Although many people dream that we will have world peace, it will never happen.

  1.1. Pro:
  World peace is impossible to achieve unless we all take responsibility for our actions [1].

    1.1.1. Pro:
    Unless we eliminate the myth of human moral superiority [2] and speciesism [3] from our moral stance, world peace is unattainable.

      1.1.1.1. Pro:
      Racism creates an illusion of moral superiority, that would deter achieving world peace.

        1.1.1.1.1. Pro:
        Racism is a multifaceted, contradictory, and historically specific system of domination and/or exclusion that creates social inequality between the majority of the population and (ethnic) minorities [4]. With the illusion of moral superiority due to racism, we could never achieve world peace.

        1.1.1.1.2. Pro:
        Racism is produced by the social practices of dominant groups and by shared social representations. There is prejudice underlying racism and the social-psychological process of stigmatisation [5] (p.1)

      1.1.1.2. Pro:
      A look at the track record [6] of counter-terrorism, the ‘war on drugs’, stabilization and state-building efforts and colonial wars ‘shows a pattern of largely very sobering failure’ says Saferworld’s Larry Attree.

      1.1.1.3. Pro:
      Global power is becoming increasingly diversified and contentious.

      1.1.1.4. Con:
      Unless one redefines "world peace" in abolitionist vegan [7] terms so that having farm animals counts as an act of war, there is absolutely no contradiction between peace between nations and the continuation of human supremacy.

        1.1.1.4.1. Pro:
        If we stop placing humans at the top of the planet’s food chain (and therefore, farm animals), we could create a different culture where peace is rooted in our attitudes and actions [8].

      1.1.1.5. Con:
      Hobbes [9] argued that if we want to live together peacefully, we must submit ourselves to an authoritative body with the power to enforce laws and resolve conflicts.

    1.1.2. Pro:
    By eliminating or reducing alcohol and drugs, we can become more peaceful.

      1.1.2.1. Con:
      Even if alcohol and drugs were eliminated, religious or political wars [10] would still exist.

      1.1.2.2. Pro:
      The war on drugs [11] creates an increase in violence, which could be reduced or eliminated by eliminating drugs.

    1.1.3. Pro:
    Taking responsibility is what creates power. The energy we expend avoiding responsibility is available to us to create a peaceful world.

      1.1.3.1. Pro:
      Political accountability [12] is important to transition towards a peaceful world.

      1.1.3.2. Con:
      Power creates conflict [13]. Therefore, if responsibility creates power, then responsibility would also create conflict.

    1.1.4. Con:
    Putting the responsibility on the citizens would be wrong, as, in a democracy [14], leaders are chosen to make changes happen.

      1.1.4.1. Pro:
      If the responsibility for achieving world peace is solely dependant on citizens, then democracies should be turned into anarchies [15].

    1.1.5. Con:
    Justice and the laws have [16] been created to ensure everyone is responsible for their actions, and yet we still don't have world peace.

      1.1.5.1. Con:
      Our attitudes and legal system support the notion that we will be aggrieved by someone or something at some point [17].

  1.2. Con:
  The world is now more peaceful [18] than it has ever been.

    1.2.1. Pro:
    Homicide rates [19] are declining around the world.

    1.2.2. Pro:

      1.2.2.1. Pro:

      1.2.2.4. Con:

    The 2020 coronavirus pandemic [24] is creating a worldwide crisis that is increasing violence.

      1.2.3.1. Pro:

      1.2.3.2. Con:
      The coronavirus pandemic could lead to a "positive peace [26]" as solidarity is increasing.

    1.2.4. Con:
    Research suggests that the world became 0.27% less peaceful during 2017 [27]. This was the fourth consecutive year global peace had declined.

    1.2.5. Pro:
    There are already some peaceful societies [28] in the world.

      1.2.5.1. Pro:
      Hunter-gatherer societies rarely engage in war [29]. The website Peaceful Societies describes highly peaceful societies which exist around the world [30], some hunter-gatherer and others not. The populations range in number from hundreds to thousands. While their characteristics vary, the societies tend not to glorify leadership or individualism, and all have convictions in nonviolence.

    1.2.6. Pro:
    Globalization [31] has created a more peaceful world.

      1.2.6.1. Pro:
      Global trade openness [32] allowed by globalization lowers the probability of conflict with the bilateral trade partner "by a larger magnitude than bilateral trade does alone."

        1.2.6.1.1. Pro:
        "Liberal peace [33]” suggests that a higher degree of bilateral economic interdependence limits the incentive to use military force in interstate relations.

      1.2.6.2. Pro:
      Mass media [34] has played an important role as a driving force in international politics.

        1.2.6.2.1. Con:

        1.2.6.2.2. Pro:

      1.2.6.3. Con:
      Globalization has increased arms trades and militarization [37], thus increasing insecurity. [38]

        1.2.6.3.1. Pro:
        The promotion of arms sales and heavy spending on aggressive military capabilities is heightening global tensions. The proliferation of arms drives conflict and makes violence more likely [39].

        1.2.6.3.2. Pro:
        Globalization weakens public authority [40] which results in social disarray, anomic violence, and general conditions that make handgun ownership and use more likely.

      1.2.6.4. Con:
      Globalization shoudn't be used as a synonym of internationalization [41], which refers to international trade, international relations, treaties and alliances.

      1.2.6.5. Pro:
      The European Union [42] is an example of a globalized institution that has efficiently created peace, as since the end of World War II, no wars have been fought in the EU.

      1.2.6.6. Con:
      Globalization increases migration [43], which directly causes conflict.

        1.2.6.6.1. Pro:
        Xenophobia [44], which is usually increased with migration, fuels violence.

      1.2.6.7. Pro:
      The UN [45] is an international institution that has had an important role in creating and maintaining world-wide peace.

      1.2.6.8. Pro:
      The world is becoming more sufficient and people are now able to handle issues better.

        1.2.6.8.1. Pro:
        The COVID crisis was handled far better than previous pandemics [46] which shows how the world has become more sufficient and able to handle issues better.

          1.2.6.8.1.1. Pro:
          Past pandemics such as the "Black Death" [47] ravaged past societies due to lack of information and procedures.

          1.2.6.8.1.2. Pro:
          The world has better medical technology to respond to now than during previous pandemics.

            1.2.6.8.1.2.1. Pro:
            Ventilators [48] have helped to save thousands of lives during the coronavirus pandemic. They weren't invented until 10 years after [49] the last pandemic.

          1.2.6.8.1.3. Pro:
          COVID-19 has shifted our world. Over the last six months, no matter where we live, our lives, assumptions, and relationships have changed. Now, more than ever, we have witnessed people from all backgrounds and all ages rise to assist each other (undp.org [50])

    1.2.7. Con:
    Data [51] suggest that the overall death rate from conflict or violence is exponentially higher in modern societies than in ancient hunter-gatherer societies.

    1.2.8. Con:
    More peaceful than ever (relatively) does not necessarily mean absolute peace or that it will ever be achieved.

    1.2.9. Pro:
    There is now less war than before.

      1.2.9.1. Pro:
      Since 1945 [52] the absolute number of war deaths has declined.

        1.2.9.1.1. Con:
        Over the last decade, the number of battle deaths each year have increased [53].

        1.2.9.1.2. Pro:
        Since the end of World War II, more people are now living in peace [54].

      1.2.9.2. Pro:
      Humans alive today are less likely to die as a result of wars or atrocities [55] than their parents or grandparents.

      1.2.9.3. Pro:
      War in East Asia [56] is at its lowest point in centuries and has been for nearly three decades.

      1.2.9.4. Pro:
      War is almost extinct [57] in the Americas, Caribbean, and western Europe. It is now uncommon in eastern Europe, southern African and increasingly in coastal west Africa.

      1.2.9.5. Con:
      The decreasing percentage of the population suffering from violent deaths can be partly explained by the fact that the human population has increased [58].

      1.2.9.6. Con:
      Peace must be more than just an absence of war. It also must be an absence of ill will and angst.

        1.2.9.6.1. Con:
        That is a normative statement [59] that does not refute the fact that war and conflict are declining internationally.

On an individual level, for example, around 20% of Europeans have been found to deeply care about "ecology and saving the planet, about relationships, peace, social justice, self-actualization, spirituality and self-expression". It has even been said that there is an "explosion in empathetic behaviour. [71]"

          1.3.1.4.3.1. Pro:

        1.3.1.5.1. Pro:

          1.3.1.5.1.1. Pro:
