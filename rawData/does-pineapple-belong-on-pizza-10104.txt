Discussion Name:
Does pineapple belong on pizza?

1. Thesis:
Pineapple belongs on pizza.

  1.1. Con:
  Pineapple on pizza leaves less room for more meat.

    1.1.1. Con:
    Consuming less meat is good [1] for human health and animal rights.

      1.1.1.1. Pro:
      The prevalence of meat toppings increases demand, hence putting meat producers in a position to increase and accept the infliction of cruelty on animals [2].

      1.1.1.2. Con:
      People who consume less meat are at risk of being Vitamin B12 deficient [3].

      1.1.1.3. Pro:
      People who consume less meat are at a lower risk of suffering from heart diseases [4].

      1.1.1.4. Con:
      Meat is one of the few sources of protein that helps improve our muscle strength [5].

    1.1.2. Pro:
    Various surveys [6] indicate that meats consistently dominate favorite pizza topping lists due to its flavor over pineapple, whereas pineapple is amongst the most disliked. [7]

      1.1.2.1. Con:
      For around 32% of Americans [8], pineapple constitutes as one of the most beloved and desired pizza toppings.

      1.1.2.2. Pro:
      Research based on 604 pizza locations across the US indicates that meat such as pepperoni (32%) and sausages (14%) [9] dominate as the most popular pizza topping.

    1.1.3. Con:
    There is more than enough room for both, no sane person covers the entire pizza with meat.

    1.1.4. Con:
    Consuming less meat is good for the environment and, as a consequence of that, [10] human health, civilization sustainability, and number of annual early deaths (or years of potential life lost).

    1.1.5. Con:
    There are lots of other alternative toppings that people may prefer as well or instead. It is inadequate to specify only meat.

    1.1.6. Con:
    Pineapple can be placed on top of the meat and meat can be placed on top of the pineapple.

      1.2.1.1. Con:
