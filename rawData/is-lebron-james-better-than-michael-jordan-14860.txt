      1.1.1.2. Pro:

      1.1.1.3. Pro:
      Jordan retired [3] from October 1993 to March 1995, missing almost two full seasons.

        1.1.1.3.1. Con:
        Jordan came back after this hiatus to win [4] the 1996, 1997, and 1998 NBA Championships.

        1.1.1.3.2. Pro:
        Jordan lost his inspiration to play in 1993, citing [5] he had "nothing more to prove".

        1.1.1.3.3. Pro:
        Jordan took a break from the NBA because his father died [6].

    1.1.2. Con:
    Michael Jordan achieved [7] more in eleven seasons than LeBron has been able to achieve in twenty seasons.

      1.1.2.1. Pro:
      Jordan had a better competitive spirit than LeBron, which allowed him to achieve greater success in a shorter amount of time.

        1.1.2.1.1. Pro:
        Jordan maintained an incredibly strict training and practice routine [8] throughout his career that kept his body in peak condition.

          1.1.2.1.1.1. Con:
          LeBron maintains [9] a strict diet and exercise schedule to keep himself in peak physical condition.

          1.1.2.1.1.2. Con:
          LeBron spends a significant amount of money [10] on his training and conditioning.

        1.1.2.1.2. Pro:
        Michael Jordan's mindset [11] and determination to win made him one of the greatest athletes of all time, in any sport.

        1.1.2.1.3. Pro:
        LeBron was told from the age of 16, by constant sports media coverage [12], that he would be one of the greatest to play the game of basketball. This led to LeBron having a worse [13] competitive spirit than Jordan [14].

        1.1.2.1.4. Con:
        Given LeBron's long list of achievements [15] in the NBA, it is difficult to argue that he lacks a competitive spirit.

      1.1.2.2. Con:
      LeBron is still playing in the league. He has time to achieve more than Jordan had in this reign.

        1.1.2.2.1. Con:
        Achieving more titles in fewer years, like Jordan, is more impressive than achieving more after a longer career.

        1.1.2.2.2. Con:
        LeBron is considering retirement [16]; his career is drawing to an end.

        1.1.3.2.1. Con:
        Jordan never faced [22] a 3-1 deficit in the Finals in the first place, indicating that he was more dominant in the Finals that he played in.

      1.1.3.3. Con:
      Jordan was far more dominant than LeBron in the NBA Finals.

        1.1.3.3.1. Pro:
        LeBron only won [23] four of the ten NBA Finals he appeared in, whereas Jordan won every single Finals [24] he competed in.

        1.1.3.3.2. Pro:
        Unlike LeBron, Jordan never [25] had to go to a Game 7 in the NBA Finals, demonstrating his dominance in the most important games.

      1.1.3.4. Con:
      If winning championships was the most important factor in comparing players, then Bill Russell [26] would be considered the best basketball player of all time, since he has won 11 championships.

      Jordan may well have gotten back to the Finals during the 1993-1995 seasons, if he hadn't been retired.

        1.1.3.5.2. Con:

    1.1.4. Con:
    LeBron had the advantage of playing in an era in which athletes had access to more advanced sports medicine, prolonging their careers [30].
