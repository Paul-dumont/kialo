Discussion Name:
Why do people believe in God?

1. Thesis:
Should people believe in God?

  1.1. Con:
  Even if he did exist, why follow him? He is a vengeful and immoral god.

  1.2. Con:
  Science and rationality point to a lot of possible explanations behind the world. Why do we need god?

  1.3. Con:
  Why would he allow so much evil in the world? Even if "eve" messed up. Isnt he a forgiving god? Sentencing humanity to the kind of fate described in the bible seems a bit extreme to me for one measly apple.

    1.3.1. Pro:
    If God is truly kind and forgiving, why does he allow so much violence and pain in the world?

  1.4. Con:
  In the bible it says servants can be beaten as long as you do not cause serious harm or death. Does that sound like something a holy god would allow? Or more like a statement from a fabricated rule book written as a controlling mechanism by men with their own agendas.

  1.5. Pro:
  "god is good all the time"

  1.6. Con:
  Believing in a god can cause people to shirk off their own obligations and responsibilities.

    1.6.1. Pro:
    Religious people can feel like they don't have to be responsible [1] for ensuring that justice is done because they know God will provide that.

      1.6.1.1. Pro:
      Religious people tend to be less charitable [2] to others than atheists.

    1.6.2. Pro:
    Religion can hinder people [3] from developing their own strong moral rules because God and religion has already provided that with them.

  1.7. Pro:
  God and christianity provide a sense of relief to people and in some aspect the bible does provide a bit of a moral compass to follow.

  1.8. Pro:
  The bible has managed to survive thousands of years intact for some reason.

  1.9. Con:
  It seems implausible.
