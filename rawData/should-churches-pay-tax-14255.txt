Discussion Name:
Should churches pay tax?

1. Thesis:
Churches in the United States should lose their automatic tax-exempt status.

  1.1. Con:

      Residing within the borders of a nation-state implies consent.

      1.1.1.2. Con:
      Just because people choose to believe, doesn't necessarily imply they agree with religious tax benefits. When organizations receive money for services and pay employees, the default position is taxation. It's the same with property ownership. The onus is on expressing a reason to deviate from the default position, not for those who would treat churches consistently with other businesses.
