Discussion Name:
Is sex with no emotional attachment good for your mind?

1. Thesis:
Sex with no emotional attachment is good for your mind.

  1.1. Pro:
  Sex with no emotional attachment is more relaxed.

    1.1.1. Pro:
    If one performs disappointingly in bed, this is less awkward if no emotional attachment is involved, for example because one can just decide to never meet that respective partner again.

      1.1.1.1. Con:
      Depending on how the two sexual partners know each other, this may lead to avoidance, which can be a nuisance.

    1.1.2. Pro:
    It is easier to voice desires and preferences without emotional attachment as one doesn't have to worry about getting embarrassed in front of one's partner.

    1.1.3. Con:
    It can also result in more pressure since the partner might expect one to perform on a certain level and might be less forgiving than is the case in a romantic relationship.

  1.2. Pro:
  It boosts your self confidence.

    1.2.1. Con:
    Studies have found that casual sex is actually linked more to feelings of shame and regret [1].

    1.2.2. Pro:
    Knowing that one is attractive enough for casual sexual encounters is a great confidence boost.

    1.2.3. Con:
    The downside of this is that there's a chance that a boost to self confidence results in hubris and arrogance.

    1.2.4. Con:
    It can harm people's self confidence if they think they're just a vessel for someone else's pleasure.

      1.2.4.1. Pro:
      Personalities and characters of people involved in a relationship solely based on casual sex are disregarded. The only thing that matters is their bodies.

      1.2.4.2. Con:
      Those engaging in casual sex may find the encounter mutually pleasurable. If there is a 'pleasure gap' then steps can be taken to make sex enjoyable for both people.

    1.2.5. Pro:
    Meaningful one night stands which include sexual activity can be most uplifting.

      1.2.5.1. Con:
      The meaning component is typically enhanced in the context of a relationship with some extension in time.

      1.2.5.2. Con:
      By definition [2], one night stands could not be without sexual activity.

      1.2.5.3. Con:
      Tying your shoelaces "can be most uplifting" (e.g under the influence of MDMA), but it does not argue that it's an activity among the most likely to uplift.

    1.2.6. Con:
    As different genders face different societal expectations, it's unlikely to boost everyone' self-confidence across the board.

  1.3. Pro:
  The responsibilities involved are much less than those of a committed relationship.

    1.3.1. Con:
    This can be negative: where no responsibilities and expectations are in place, there is less incentive to do one's best to ensure all those involved are having a good time.

      1.3.1.1. Pro:
      Since people think they are not responsible for the other person's emotions, they do not show basic human decency [3] towards them.

    1.3.2. Pro:
    There would be no need to expend emotional energy on a partner.

    1.3.3. Pro:
    No need to be in constant communication or having to meet each other's families.

    1.3.4. Pro:
    No more emotional pressure.

      1.3.4.1. Pro:
      Relationships with prolonged emotional stress can have physical harms [4].

        1.3.4.1.1. Pro:
        Women experience greater levels [5] of psychological stress as a result of relationships than men.

        1.3.4.1.2. Pro:
        Increased blood pressure and risk of heart attacks are associated [6]with emotional stress originating from relationships.

      1.3.4.2. Con:
      Emotional pressure is not necessarily bad. Many people appreciate having someone to rely on emotionally, especially in testing times.

    1.3.5. Con:
    People in romantic relationships generally don't mind taking on certain responsibilities, as they also benefit from their partners doing the same.

  1.4. Pro:
  It promotes social cohesion.

  1.5. Pro:
  It's enjoyable.

    1.5.1. Pro:
    It's a natural megadose of feel-good chemicals.

      1.5.1.1. Pro:
      There is a huge release of dopamine [7] during sex [8].

      1.5.1.2. Pro:
      The serene post-orgasmic halo is related to a large dose of Oxytocin [9] [2] [10].

    1.5.2. Pro:
    It's one of the most universal human motivational drives.

      1.5.2.1. Pro:
      Our bodies continually seek the feel-good rush of dopamine, and sex is a powerful way to achieve that pleasure. You can engage in sex purely for this physical satisfaction, without needing emotional involvement, and doing so can actually be beneficial for your mind. It can offer a valuable release of stress and tension, boost confidence, and provide a space for self-discovery and exploration without the complexities of emotional attachment, though emotions can certainly elevate the experience.

    1.5.3. Pro:
    The clitoris and the head of the penis have their own unqiue nerve endings [11] that seems correlated with the high level of pleasure.

    1.5.4. Con:
    It could distract from relationships with more to offer.

    1.5.5. Con:
    Enjoyment does not indicate that something is good for your mind. For example, many hard drugs which can damage the mind are enjoyable to use. Enjoyment is irrelevent. Drugs that can harm the mind [12]

    1.5.6. Con:
    Sex is generally enhanced by emotional attachment with one's partner. By engaging in meaningless sex, one misses out on greater enjoyment by investing in a relationship.

  1.6. Con:
  For many, emotional attachment is inevitable, regardless of their initial intent.

    1.6.1. Pro:
    People are made to bond during physical intimacy [13]. Human bodies are wired to release chemicals during physical intimacy that make people feel connected to the person we’re with. These sorts of relationships are hence unhealthy.

      1.6.1.1. Con:
      Just because people bond during sex, that doesn't mean that people ought to hold onto that bond indefinitely.

      1.6.1.2. Con:
      Bononos [14] also release oxytocin during sex, but it doesn't create unhealthy attachment or jealousy. It simply facilitates social cohesion and conflict-resolution.[2] [15]

      1.6.1.3. Con:
      The chemical release during sex is healthy and the sense of intimacy doesn't need to be some unhealthy romantic attachment or jealousy, it could simply be a closer and more trusting friendship. Thus, these relationships are healthy. [1] [16] [2] [17]

    1.6.2. Con:
    This does not mean that sex without emotional attachment is bad for those who do not develop this attachment.

    1.6.3. Con:
    If we look at our close hominid relatives [18] it seems we should share their evolutionary endowment for hypersexuality with minimal attachment. [19]

      1.6.3.1. Con:
      Hypersexuality is considered a mental health disorder [20]. It should not be a considered standard of living simply because primates we share DNA with do it.

    1.6.4. Pro:
    There is no such thing as sex with no emotional attachment, since the desire for sex is supremely emotional. Human beings are not robots, so that any belief in anything which does not exist is always bad for the mind.

      1.6.4.1. Con:
      Desire for sex [21] and emotional attachment [22] are mediated by separate neurotransmitter systems. It's possible to have deficits in oxytocin [23] while being hyper dopaminergic [24].

    1.6.5. Pro:
    The spike in oxytocin [25] during orgasm [26] predisposes you to form a deep pairbond.

    1.6.6. Con:
    This can be the case for any group activity. Departure from attachment is a major part of life.

    1.6.7. Pro:
    Cis women may be evolutionary predisposed [27] to bond with sexual partners.

      1.6.7.1. Con:
      Cis women are also programmed to desire having children, a partner who provides for them, etc. This, however, is not how many women choose to live today.

  1.7. Con:
  Sex without emotion indicates that one is without a strong family, and thus is likely to be depressed and lonely.

    1.7.1. Pro:
    Families need economic and interpersonal support. With a cultural disincentive to creating lasting bonds, what replaces them is an absence of these ties that have historically kept families together.

  1.8. Con:
  Sex-without-love is really mutual masturbation; avoiding the honest intercourse which offers the greater life experience: Avoidant attachment. [28]

  1.9. Con:
  Sex is intimate activity which consciously or subconsciously has an emotional, spiritual element attached to it which cannot be negated or separated no matter how hard you try.

  1.10. Pro:
  Having sex without emotional attachment reduces the pressure to find a permanent romantic partner.

    1.10.1. Con:
    Having sex without emotional attachment could increase the desire to find a partner because the sex is unfulfilling and fleeting.

    1.10.2. Con:
    For society as a whole, this is problematic since children are usually produced in committed relationships.

      1.10.2.1. Pro:
      Across many industrialized countries, birthrates are already below the level [29] necessary for population sustenance.

        1.10.2.1.1. Con:
        The world is already over-populated and this problem is poised to get worse. Lower birthrates, as seen from a global perspective, are therefore positive.

    1.10.3. Con:
    People search for partners in life for reasons other than sex. Even if having sex without emotional attachment reduces the pressure to find someone, it still leaves behind room for the many other reasons that drive people to seek a partner.

    1.10.4. Pro:
    Many individuals do not have the time for a long term committed relationship but need to fulfill their physical needs and hence such a relationship can be ideal.

      1.10.4.1. Pro:
      If sex is viewed as a natural bodily function, like eating and drinking, then there is nothing wrong with pursuing casual sex.

  1.11. Pro:
  Sex without emotional attachment allows one the freedom to find other people.

    1.11.1. Pro:
    Non-monogamous lifestyles lead to personal growth because one has several partners to get feedback from.

      1.11.1.1. Con:
      A person with whom you are in a monogamous relationship will likely know you better than other, more casual, acquaintances, and will thus give you more accurate feedback.

      1.11.1.2. Con:
      Serial monogamy relations allows for the same.

      1.11.1.3. Con:
      Non-monogamous lifestyles do not imply lack of emotional attachment.

    1.11.2. Con:
    Those in polyamorous [30] relationships often experience sexual freedom, while also recognising the importance of having an emotional bond with one or more partners.

    1.11.3. Pro:
    Physical intimacy is an important part of getting to know someone, thus if sex comes without commitment, both people will be better informed before they commit.

    1.11.4. Pro:
    When sex is abundant and without strings attached, there is less drive to settle for suboptimal relationships.

    1.11.5. Pro:
    It prevents people from developing emotions for the wrong person.

  1.12. Con:
  There is a social stigma attached to these sorts of relationships and they have a negative image in society, hence people in such relationships have to suffer through its implications.

    1.12.1. Pro:
    Women especially are shamed for engaging in casual sex.

      1.12.1.1. Pro:
      A study found that after having casual sex, only 26% of women [31] felt good about their decision.

    1.12.2. Con:
    This very much depends on the society and social circles you roam in, as many are today endorsing and even advocating for pure hedonistic sex with no emotional attachment.

    1.12.3. Con:
    Negative images can be changed, especially when such images have little to no real ground on which to stand.

    1.12.4. Con:
    If one's relationship doesn't become public knowledge then this will not be a problem.

  1.13. Con:
  A child could come from it.

    1.13.1. Con:
    Whether emotional attachment is involved or not does not affect the likelihood of producing a child.

      1.13.1.1. Con:
      In fact, emotional attachment probably makes an unwanted pregnancy more likely since it usually goes hand in hand with a high degree of trust.

        1.13.1.1.1. Pro:
        Where a couple has developed an emotional attachment, they are likely to be more trusting that their partner does not have an STD, that they can deal with any unexpected pregnancies and thus will be wore willing to have sex without protection.

          1.13.1.1.1.1. Pro:
          Couples in comitted relationships use condoms less often [32].

    1.13.2. Pro:
    Approximately 50 percent [33] of all pregnancies in the United States are unplanned.

      1.13.2.1. Con:
      That a pregnancy is unplanned does not indicate whether any emotional attachment was involved in the sex (or, as the case may be, sexual assault) that resulted in the pregnancy.

    1.13.3. Con:
    Raising a child can be good for your mental health.

      1.13.3.1. Pro:
      Many parents become happier with their children around.

        1.13.3.1.1. Con:
        Research in the United States suggests that [34] people who have kids report being less happy than people who don’t.

          1.13.3.1.1.1. Pro:
          On average, an American is 12% unhappier [35] than a non-parent.

        1.13.3.1.2. Con:
        Two meta-studies have found [36] that “life satisfaction” (a measure of happiness) was a bit lower among parents when they have children.

        1.13.3.1.3. Pro:
        In Portugal, parents who have and raise children are 8% happier [37] compared to those who do not have children.

    1.13.4. Con:
    The couple could avoid this by having sex acts other than vaginal intercourse.

    1.13.5. Con:
    They could find someone to adopt the child.

    1.13.6. Con:
    If pregnancy occurs despite of contraception, the fetus could be aborted at a very early stage.

    1.13.7. Pro:
    Being worried about causing pregnancy might offset the beneficial effects of casual sex.

    1.13.8. Con:
    People can use contraceptives to greatly reduce the risk of an unwanted child being born.

      1.13.8.1. Con:
      There is no [38] 100% safe way to prevent pregnancies.

        1.13.8.1.1. Pro:
        If preventing pregnancy is the primary concern, people should not only stop having sex where there is not an emotional attachment but should stop having sex altogether.

        1.13.8.1.2. Con:
        There is no 100% safe way to do anything; such an assurance is just an assumption held until broken.

        1.13.8.1.3. Con:
        Combining multiple forms of contraception will likely have the desired effect.

        1.13.8.1.4. Con:
        New forms of birth control will likely become available that do safely prevent pregnancies.

      1.13.8.2. Con:
      Abortion is 100% effective.

        1.13.8.2.1. Con:
        Abortion is not available everywhere and unsafe to do yourself.

          1.13.8.2.1.1. Con:
          Abortion can and should be made available everywhere.

        1.13.8.2.2. Con:
        Abortions have their own set of problems. (Look at this [39] discussion for more information.)

      1.13.8.3. Pro:
      Contraceptives are generally very accessible and affordable in most countries.

  1.14. Pro:
  It has health benefits.

    1.14.1. Con:
    Having lots of casual sex increases the risk of contracting an STD. This risk is much lower in a monogamous relationship.

      1.14.1.1. Con:
      The transmission of STDs can be effectively prevents by taking precautions, for example using condoms.

        1.14.1.1.1. Con:
        Condoms [40] do not provide infallible protection against STDs: they can slip off or break during sex, and can be weakened by oil-based products, topical medications or extreme temperatures.

        1.14.1.1.2. Con:
        In the general population, up to 5% [41] of people are believed to have an allergy to latex; since condoms are usually [42] made from latex, this makes them largely non-viable for these people.

        1.14.1.1.3. Con:
        A 2015 study by Skyn condoms found that 48% [43] of millennials use condoms “never” or “rarely.” Condom use would have to be much more widespread and consistent than this to effectively prevent a proliferation of STDs in a given population.

          1.14.1.1.3.1. Con:
          Not all of these millennials are sexually active, and some may be in a committed relationship with someone who has been STD tested.

        1.14.1.1.4. Pro:
        Laboratory studies [44] have shown that latex condoms provide an effective barrier against even the smallest STD pathogens.

        1.14.1.1.5. Con:
        STDs such as Herpes, HPV, pubic lice and Syphilis can be transmitted through skin-to-skin [45] contact even if a condom is used.

    1.14.2. Con:
    Casual sex can lead to psychological problems [46], such as lower self-esteem.

      1.14.2.1. Pro:
      Casual sex has been linked to [47] higher levels of anxiety and depression in both men and women.

      1.14.2.2. Con:
      The impact of casual sex on mental health is unclear. Studies use varying definitions of 'hookups' and tend [48] to use data from a limited demographic.

    1.14.3. Pro:
    Sex boosts one's immune system [49].

    1.14.4. Pro:
    If people are only waiting for sex with emotional attachment they are limiting their ability to experience the health benefits associated with sex.

      1.14.4.1. Pro:
      By looking for sexual partners with which we have both physical and emotional attraction, there are less potential partners to choose from, narrowing the options for sexual activity.

        1.14.4.1.1. Pro:
        It takes significantly more time and energy to build an emotional attachment with someone, whereas people can judge physical attraction online, without even meeting the sexual partner until they have sex.

      1.14.4.2. Pro:
      For individuals who struggle to build emotional connections, they may rarely, or never, get to experience the health benefits of sex if an emotional attachment is necessary to successfully source sexual partners.

        1.14.4.2.1. Pro:
        It is particularly harmful for those with issues such as anxiety, where the release from sex could help lower their anxiety levels significantly, but the anxiety could inhibit emotionally connecting with someone enough to have sex with emotional attachment.

        1.14.4.2.2. Pro:
        Sex without emotional attachment allows people to bypass the need to build emotional attachment in order to access these health benefits.

    1.14.5. Pro:
    Sex also lower people's blood pressure [50].

    1.14.6. Pro:
    Being active during sex is a good physical workout for the body.

      1.14.6.1. Con:
      A study [51] undertaken by the University of Montreal showed that men burn 100 calories during the average 25-minute sex session, and women burn about 69 calories. For men, this is comparable to the calories burned in a light 25-minute slow walk [52].

      1.14.6.2. Con:
      As with all other physical activity, how good a workout sexual activity is depends heavily on the activity type and vigour.

      1.14.6.3. Con:
      Sex does not burn as many calories than is often assumed. [53]

      1.14.6.4. Con:
      Sex is a form of cardiovascular exercise. Resistance training is the superior [54] mode of exercise.

      1.14.6.5. Pro:
      Sex can be considered a workout [55], especially if had before breakfast, or incorporating core strength exercises.

    1.14.7. Con:
    People in committed relationships have fewer mental health problems [56] than single people. This indicates that emotional attachment can enhance the benefits of sex.

    1.14.8. Pro:
    Sex can regulate women's menstrual cycles. [57]

    1.14.9. Pro:
    Sex [58] is a natural pain reliever, and increases the production of oxytocin, dopamine and endorphins.

    1.14.10. Pro:
    It's less stressful.

      1.14.10.1. Pro:
      It can improve cognition: separating the two in one's mind can help build analysis skills to differentiate between items in general better.

      1.14.10.2. Pro:
      Separating sex and emotional attachment allows for better processing of each aspect of one's life. This can reduce issues building up with each, which would reduce one's stress.

      1.14.10.3. Con:
      Because it's natural to at times have no emotional attachment with sex, so not having it can stress someone out when they expect to get one.

  1.15. Con:
  Sex without emotional attachment could make one dependent on something that could end at any time.

    1.15.1. Con:
    Without emotional attachments the dependencies are limited and easy to replace.

      1.15.1.1. Pro:
      Paid sex can replace a friends with benefit relationship without emotional attachments easily.

      1.15.1.2. Con:
      Emotional attachment is not necessarily a bad thing, even if it's not planned, this most likely just provides a stronger connection between the two people. and that, is a beautiful thing.

    1.15.2. Con:
    Once emotions are involved, dependencies are at least as likely (and probably stronger).

    1.15.3. Pro:
    People in sexual relationships might get used to the benefits of having regular sex, and may suffer when this is withdrawn.

    1.15.4. Pro:
    If one person is more dependent than the other, this might introduce a power imbalance to the relationship.

      1.15.4.1. Con:
      Power imbalances can be applicable to romantic relationships too.

        1.15.4.1.1. Pro:
        Sometimes the power imbalance is an integral factor for being attracted to somebody, like being attracted to your boss [59].

    1.15.5. Pro:
    People who are prone to jealousy may end up developing emotional attachment and can often get a lot of emotional pain out of such a relationship.

    1.15.6. Con:
    Dependency is not a meaningul differentiator that occurs when having sex without emotional attachment. One could equally become dependent on the emotional attachment and that could end in the future.

    1.15.7. Con:
    Sex could end at any time regardless, so relying on the supposed 'guarantee' could lead someone to even greater disappointment than not believing that emotional attachment would help with consistency.
