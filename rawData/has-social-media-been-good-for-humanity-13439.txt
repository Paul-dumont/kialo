Discussion Name:
Has Social Media Been Good For Humanity?

1. Thesis:
Social Media Has Been Good For Humanity.

  1.1. Pro:
  Social media allows humans to communicate more effectively.

    1.1.1. Con:
    There is less incentive for people to get out and interact face to face with other people. We end up interacting only through our phones rather than making more meaningful connections.

      1.1.1.1. Con:
      Depending on the way you use it, it can also be a way of finding and taking part in activities with people that have the same interests as you.

      1.1.1.2. Con:
      One does not exclude the other. Social media can lead to face to face meetings.

      1.1.1.3. Pro:
      Social interactions are inherently risky. Less face to face interactions actually helps to lower risk, allowing you a safer environment to develop core relationships more deeply.

      1.1.1.4. Con:
      If people do not find the need to interact face to face with other people, this may be because they perceive the online connections they are making to be sufficiently or more meaningful.

    1.1.2. Con:
    Social interaction loses most of its non-verbal communication.

      1.1.2.1. Pro:
      Social media does not allow for body language [1].

      1.1.2.2. Pro:
      Social media can be damaging, simply because of the lack of body language [2].

      1.1.2.3. Con:
      A trade-off doesn't always translate to a net negative. For example it is less healthy and more dangerous to drive a car, than to walk 15 miles. However the efficiency gains of driving a car allow us to be far more productive than the alternative.

      1.1.2.4. Con:
      Social media platforms often have video-calling features [3] built into them, thus allowing for some degree of non-verbal communication.

      1.1.2.5. Con:
      Social media allows for alternative forms of non-verbal communication through filter choice, font choice, sentence structure, punctuation and spacing [4].

        1.1.2.5.1. Pro:
        Emoticons [5] operate as replacements for human facial expressions.

    1.1.3. Pro:
    Social media faciliates the formation of new social relationships.

      1.1.3.1. Pro:
      Social media provides a means for meeting people from different cultural backgrounds.

        1.1.3.1.1. Pro:
        Social media provides greater scope to interact with those who hold different beliefs to us as it exposes us to a much broader pool of people.

          1.1.3.1.1.1. Con:
          Merely increasing the likelihood of interacting with those who hold different beliefs to us doesn't equate to better communication with those people.

      1.1.3.2. Pro:
      It becomes easier to find and meet people globally that share similar interests to you.

        1.1.3.2.1. Pro:
        This has led to the growth of the e-sports and subsequently the streaming industry, the first of which affords large prize pools (hundreds of thousands to millions dependant on the game) and the second of which can pay 'top streamers' thousands.

      1.1.3.3. Con:
      Social media exposes you to a wide variety of people. This may create pressure to impress people [6] who are ultimately quite different to us.

      1.1.3.4. Con:
      Social media gives the illusion of real friends while neglecting or simply not investing in real ones

        1.1.3.4.1. Pro:
        Social media creates barriers: it isolates human beings from each other and the world around them - Evidence shows that it has an anti-social effect. [7]

    1.1.4. Pro:
    Social media allows us to better maintain existing social relationships.

      1.1.4.1. Pro:
      There have been several disasters during which social media provided the only viable venue for communication.

        1.1.4.1.1. Pro:
        During Superstorm Sandy, most people didn’t have power, but some cell networks and WiFi towers were still operational. Many people used Facebook to detail the destruction and reassure friends or family that they were safe.

        1.1.4.1.2. Pro:
        During the pandemic, social media has allowed international families to share events such as weddings and funerals virtually. It has therefore helped people maintain their connections with their loved ones and aided overall well being.

      1.1.4.2. Pro:
      Social media is a great way for families separated by distance to keep informed of the well being of one another.

        1.1.4.2.1. Pro:
        Information could be shared seamlessly among friends and family, regardless of them being near or far away.

      1.1.4.3. Con:
      The degree to which social media can fulfill our basic need for friendship varies widely from person to person [8].

      1.1.4.4. Pro:
      It is easy to maintain non day-to-day relationships.

      1.1.4.5. Pro:
      Social media is a new-to-world innovation for individuals to update their friends and loved ones easily and with persistence. It replaces the group emails we used to use, and is much better for this purpose.

    1.1.5. Pro:
    Social media facilitates can provide a means of communication in difficult circumstances.

      1.1.5.1. Pro:
      For severely handicapped people, social media may be the only means of keeping in touch with people.

    1.1.6. Con:
    Some of the communication that takes place on social media is indirect and passive.

      1.1.6.1. Con:
      In many cases social interactions online may be more direct and engaging than social interactions in person.

        1.1.6.1.1. Pro:
        A social interaction involving two people who share the same interests is likely to be of a higher quality than if the two people share no common interests, regardless of whether that social interaction takes place online or in person.

      1.1.6.2. Pro:
      Posting on an instagram page is not a direct means of communication.

        1.1.6.2.1. Con:
        An individual posting on their instragram page is communicating with their followers.

      1.1.6.3. Pro:
      Some social media content is directed to an audience and is therefore impersonal.

        1.1.6.3.1. Con:
        Although content may be directed towards a larger audience, individuals within that audience may extract value from that content in a personal way.

      1.1.6.4. Pro:
      Those with larger followings may not see all communications from those who follow them.

      1.1.6.5. Con:
      Indirect communication may still be more valuable.

    1.1.7. Pro:
    Social media allows us to communicate better with those who hold different beliefs to us.

      1.1.7.1. Con:
      People have a tendency to feel empowered by a keyboard and will thus express more extreme opinions [9] (and more aggressively so as well) than they would in person when having a face-to-face conversation. Thus, social media discussions often escalate and end up with name calling and other kinds of personal attacks.

      1.1.7.2. Con:
      Social media exposes you to easy attacks from people who do not know you and might have an alternative motive in their attempts to dismantle your argument (e.g. trolls).

      1.1.7.3. Con:
      Social media allows one to be more spiteful without fear of retribution.

        1.1.7.3.1. Pro:
        Social media provides a high degree of anonymity [10].

          1.1.7.3.1.1. Pro:
          The lack of retaliation [11] for adolescents engaging anonymously on social media may influence more aggressive behavior.

          1.1.7.3.1.2. Pro:
          Many users setup their social media accounts using aliases to conceal their identity. [12]

          1.1.7.3.1.3. Pro:
          If users on social media can remain anonymous, their negative actions cannot easily be attributed to their actual identity.

          1.1.7.3.1.4. Con:
          Many people would not bully others on moral grounds, regardless of whether they are anonymous or not.

          1.1.7.3.1.5. Con:
          Many social media profiles are linked to the real identity of an individual.

      1.1.7.4. Pro:
      Social media made it easy to connect to people you wouldn't have met a century ago, causing better understanding and respect for other cultures.

        1.1.7.4.1. Con:
        Respect is not always the effect of knowing about other cultures, one may also come to believe that other cultures or groups are bad or harmful to society.

      1.1.7.5. Pro:
      -> See 1.1.3.1.1.

    1.1.8. Con:
    Anonymity has reduced accountability in spreading false information and dangerous rhetoric.

    1.1.9. Pro:
    Social media allows for greater effectiveness in communication because it can bring together multiple different types of media, simultaneously.

  1.2. Con:
  Social media has negative health effects.

    1.2.1. Pro:
    Over-use of social media is negatively affecting [13] people's sleep patterns and quality.

      1.2.1.1. Pro:
      The blue light emitted from screens [14] stimulates the brain which delays sleep as well as affects the quality of sleep.

    1.2.2. Pro:
    FOMO [15] (Fear Of Missing Out) - stressor to contend with for some when they use social media. "Everyone else is doing it and I'm not" fear.

    1.2.3. Pro:
    Social media use during driving [16] or walking [17] across the road or operating dangerous equipment [18] shows increase in injuries and deaths.

    1.2.4. Con:
    Social media can have positive effects [19] on one's mental health.

      1.2.4.1. Con:
      Exposure to online harassment - stressor to contend with for some resulting in some youths committing suicide [20].

      1.2.4.2. Con:
      Creates a reward system and withdrawals if you don't post on it or keep checking the platform for likes or new friends. People experience addictive effects when they are disconnected even for a few hours. Study: Social media addiction: Its impact, mediation, and intervention [21]

        1.2.4.2.1. Pro:
        Attention engineers are making their platform as addictive as possible.facebroek.com [22]

      1.2.4.3. Pro:
      Mindful routine use of social media is positively correlated with three [23] beneficial health outcomes.

      1.2.4.4. Con:
      People have been shown to experience depression as a repercussion of social media.

        1.2.4.4.1. Pro:
        Instagram [24] has been linked to teenagers' issues with mental health, exacerbating problems such as body image and FOMO.

    1.2.5. Con:
    Social media often promotes health conscious behaviour.

      1.2.5.1. Pro:
      There is a lot of fitness content [25] on social media.

      1.2.5.2. Pro:
      There are groups related to sports encouraging activity and other health conscious behaviour.

    1.2.6. Con:
    The use of social media itself does not involve any physical activity and thus encourages a sedentary lifestyle.

      1.2.6.1. Con:
      Social media can be used outdoors during or inetween doing physical activity.

    1.2.7. Pro:
    Social media use often involves high amounts of screen time which can result in digital eye strain.

    1.2.8. Pro:
    Social media can promote unrealistic beauty standards [26] which can then cause mental health problems

  1.3. Con:
  Social media puts privacy and data at risk.

    1.3.1. Pro:
    Many platforms use a security opt-in model, with new users being oblivious to the fact that a lot of personal information is publically exposed.

    1.3.2. Pro:
    Social media platforms collects user data to sell [27] to third parties for a profit.

    1.3.3. Con:
    Data collection on how one uses social media is not inherently a negative [28].

      1.3.3.1. Pro:
      Data collection allows social media platforms to profile a user and show them advertisments that are more relevant to them [29].

        1.3.3.1.1. Pro:
        More relevant ads equates to exposing a customer to things that they are more likely to be interested in.

        1.3.3.1.2. Pro:
        More relevant ads provides consumers with exposure to things they otherwise may not have had exposure to.

    1.3.4. Con:
    Users can restrict the amount of data they share [30] with social media platforms.

    1.3.5. Con:
    Privacy has no universally applicable standards, only the minimal requirements that are specified (and often changed) by law.

    1.3.6. Con:
    Users have a responsibility to themselves and others not to submit data that will prove to be personally embarrassing or socially compromising.

      1.3.6.1. Pro:
      Data security is vulnerable from a number of perspectives including breach or hacking, changing user privacy policies, user errors and indiscretions, and social media company errors and indiscretions.

      1.3.6.2. Pro:
      Users make the decision to put information out in such an insecure environment and should bear responsibility for the consequences of that decision.

        1.3.6.2.1. Con:
        There is no free will. People are attracted to social medias because it is free, popular and fun. They do not understand how insecure it is and therefore should not suffer the consequences.

      1.3.6.3. Con:
      Many users are not informed enough with regards to the implications of exposing their personal data to the public.

        1.3.6.3.1. Con:
        People do need to read the terms and conditions concerning personal data use before entering a certain site, wherein the term specifies that personal data will be used in order to validate the person's account. Thus, the user can decide whether or not they want to share their personal information.

  1.4. Con:
  Social media increases the risk of cyberbullying [31].

    1.4.1. Pro:
    Social media can be used to spread false rumors and undermine reputations [32].

      1.4.1.1. Con:
      This is not inherently related to social media and can be said about other means of sharing information.

    1.4.2. Pro:
    Some teenagers that spend a lot of time on social media can display [33] cyber-bullying behaviors.

    1.4.3. Con:
    Social media has also been used to raise awareness about bullying and cyber-bullying. [34]

    1.4.4. Pro:
    -> See 1.1.7.3.1.

    1.4.5. Con:
    Users are able to report cyberbullying [35].

    1.4.6. Pro:
    Cyberbulling on social media has resulted in some people committing suicide [36].

  1.5. Pro:
  Social media facilitates economic productivity.

    1.5.1. Pro:
    Social media platforms provide more avenues for advertising, which drives greater economic output.

      1.5.1.1. Pro:
      Advertising on social media helps businesses acquire traffic, which leads to greater revenue for those businesses.

      1.5.1.2. Pro:
      Advertising spend on social media amounts to over 100 billion dollars annually [37].

      1.5.1.3. Pro:
      Social media platforms gives advertisers access to large audiences.

        1.5.1.3.1. Pro:
        In 2021, there was over 4 billion social media users worldwide [38].

        1.5.1.3.2. Pro:
        Advertisers can use social media to target a global audience.

      1.5.1.4. Pro:
      Social media platforms possess data about those that use the platform which can allow advertisers to target more effectively.

    1.5.2. Pro:
    Social media creates job opportunities [39].

    1.5.3. Con:
    Social media can be a distraction that prevents people from engaging in tasks that are directly economically productive.

    1.5.4. Con:
    Many people are using social media in ways that are not economically productive.

      1.5.4.1. Con:
      Even those passively browsing social media for entertainment purposes are audiences that advertisers can target.

    1.5.5. Con:
    Social media reduces work throughput [40].

  1.6. Pro:
  Social media can be educational.

    1.6.1. Con:
    Social media can create echo chambers for particular viewpoints, regardless of whether they are rooted in fact or not.

      1.6.1.1. Pro:
      Social media algorithms designed to enhance the user's experience have inadvertently create deeply personalized Filter Bubbles [41]. Information dissemination is calculated by user desire rather than objective necessity.

        1.6.1.1.1. Con:
        There is no such thing as "objective necessity."

        1.6.1.1.2. Pro:
        Social media algorithms increasingly drive topical and publisher selection preferences in news aggregation services, reducing the user's options to set personal preferences manually and with intention. AI can make these selections with a bias that such will inform or distract but almost always satisfy.

      1.6.1.2. Pro:
      The majority of social media platforms allow users to remain within circles that don't require them to be exposed to other perspectives, limiting balanced collaborative efforts.

      1.6.1.3. Con:
      This assumes that echo chambers are inherently bad, which is not a given. For example, if a user's end goal is to collaborate with people with similar opinions, the echo chamber effect is desirable.

        1.6.1.3.1. Con:
        Searching to connect with like-minded people is a different purpose than searching for unbiased and factual information.

      1.6.1.4. Pro:
      Confirmation bias [42] is a major issue in today's society as it is pushed by the business models of Social media platforms.

      1.6.1.5. Con:
      We can counter this tendency by following people we don't agree with on social media.

      1.6.1.6. Con:
      The same systems that create echo chambers can be (ab)used to intentionally enter other peoples echo chambers. TED talk [43]

      1.6.1.7. Con:
      This extent this happens varies widely from platform to platform. It is therefore innacurate to generalise this across social media entirely.

    1.6.2. Con:
    Humans spend too long on social media, at the expense of other more valuable activities.

      1.6.2.1. Con:
      No activity is innately more valuable than another activity. The value is determined by personal preference.

      1.6.2.2. Con:
      Social media can be beneficial as a leisure-time activity.

        1.6.2.2.1. Con:
        Social media, as a leisure-time activity displaces more healthy, social and productive activities like reading, spending time with friend and sport.

      1.6.2.3. Pro:
      Social media tends to be used less for educational purposes and more for entertainment.

        1.6.2.3.1. Pro:
        Too much information floating around causes important information to be ignored in favor of information that is "trending" or more exciting (e.g. celebrity news).

        1.6.2.3.2. Pro:
        On social media there is a tendecy to spread funny videos [44] for entertainment purposes.

        1.6.2.3.3. Pro:
        Humans tend to overuse social media to fight boredom [45] and loneliness instead of finding something educational and/or fulfilling.

        1.6.2.3.4. Con:
        Whether social media is used for education purposes or entertainment, depends on how it's used.

        1.6.2.3.5. Pro:
        Social media content is often written in a more pointed and provocative way, even if it is at the expense of accuracy.

    1.6.3. Con:
    Social media was originally designed for social purposes. The end goal of the major social media websites and apps was never a colletive educational experience.

      1.6.3.1. Con:
      Things often develop beyond what their original purpose was.

    1.6.4. Pro:
    Social media provides a collective learning experience [46] where people can learn from one another.

      1.6.4.1. Pro:
      People can create pages on social media sites to inform users about science, history, philosophy, and even grammar usage.

    1.6.5. Pro:
    Social media makes it easier for people to access information and news [47].

      1.6.5.1. Con:
      Social media allows anyone to spread information, regardless of how accurate the information is.

        1.6.5.1.1. Pro:
        Social media is susceptible to misuse through the spreading of misinformation to wide audiences and targeted audiences designed to manipulate and influence.

          1.6.5.1.1.1. Pro:
          Microsoft AI experimentation on Twitter [48] lead the learning AI to become racist and misogynistic in less than a day because of malicious manipulation of its peers.

          1.6.5.1.1.2. Con:
          Other media platforms suffer from the same issue. In fact, tabloid magazines base their entire business model on the publishing of gossip (often uncorroborated), which is equivalent to "spreading misinformation to wide audiences."

        1.6.5.1.2. Pro:
        Social media spreads fake news far more than traditional media sources.

          1.6.5.1.2.1. Pro:
          Social media has a lack of accountability in sharing accurate information, and is not good at identifying what is true when content or ideas are presented as fact or fiction.

        1.6.5.1.3. Con:
        If people do not take responsibility to do their own research independently of social media, social media can become an issue, but it is not a problem on its own.

          1.6.5.1.3.1. Con:
          Not everyone has the same capability to undertake research on their own.

        1.6.5.1.4. Con:
        People will always consume conflicting information. Making a judgement regarding truth always relies with the individual.

      1.6.5.2. Con:
      -> See 1.6.1.1.

      1.6.5.3. Pro:
      Most topics of interest are covered by some group or channel. Following these channels makes keeping updated easy and accessible for everyone.

  1.7. Pro:

      1.7.1.3. Con:
      Criminals can make use of social media platforms to coordinate illicit activities.

        1.7.1.3.1. Con:
        Most platforms have methods of monitoring for criminal behaviour.

          1.7.1.3.1.1. Pro:
          The largest social media platforms have teams dedicated to detecting and reporting criminal activity.

          1.7.1.3.3.1. Pro:

      1.7.1.4. Con:

      1.7.1.5. Pro:

    1.7.2. Con:

      1.7.4.1. Pro:

  1.9. Con:
  Social media is a danger to democracy.

    1.9.1. Con:
    Social media is a vital tool for grassroots political organizing. (protest, advocacy groups, etc). This is beneficial to democracy.

      1.9.1.1. Con:
      Mailing lists, forums, and websites can all be used for grassroots political organizing.

        1.9.1.1.1. Con:
        Mailing lists, forums and good old fashion websites are a form of social media like any other. Arguably, email is also a form of social media. The difference is that they aren't centrally controlled by corporations.

    1.9.2. Con:
    Social media is less susceptible to the powerful than traditional media sources.

      1.9.2.1. Con:
      Social media can be manipulated by powerful states and companies by paying large amounts of money on advertising, hacking or social bots.

      1.9.2.2. Pro:
      Social media helps diversify the centralisation of power.

      1.9.2.3. Con:
      The fact that the most followed [54] twitter accounts are well-known, powerful celebrities suggests otherwise.

        1.9.2.3.1. Pro:
        Social media elevates the thoughts and opinions of celebrities like Kanye West more than the thoughts and opinions of people who know what they are talking about.

          1.9.2.3.1.1. Con:
          Users have the freedom to follow whomever they choose. The individual makes the choice on whose thoughts and opinions they value.

      1.9.2.4. Con:
      Some of the largest social media platform are effectively under the control of single individuals, for example Mark Zuckerberg in the case of Meta and Elon Musk in the case of Twitter.

    1.9.3. Con:
    Many discussions on social media are organised and largely democratic.

      1.9.3.1. Pro:
      Discussion forums provide a cleaner space for discourse as many of the more popular social media networks flood users with excess 'noise' and advertising.

      1.9.3.2. Con:
      Social media discussions are not structured in the way you might expect a democratic discussion to be.

        1.9.3.2.1. Pro:
        Discussions often take place in comment sections which has no formalised structure to them.

        1.9.3.2.2. Con:
        Platforms like Twitter give users the ability to 'reply' to tweets. This can be a good way to communicate where someone agrees or disagrees with a perspective.

    1.9.4. Pro:
    Social media, as shown by the Cambridge Analytica [55] scandal, can also be used as a tool to usurp democratic elections by manipulating voters.

    1.9.5. Con:
    Social media gives a political voice to more people and is thus a net positive for democracy.

      1.9.5.1. Con:
      The ability to post political opinions on social media does not equate to having a political voice as the latter assumes that the content being posted is reaching a significantly sized audience.

      1.9.5.2. Pro:
      Social media brings attention towards issues of political significance.

        1.9.5.2.1. Pro:
        Social media has been used to great effect to keep the light shining on important issues. Everything from Second Amendment issues in the US to reminding the UK government about the lives lost at Grenfell Tower in June of last year.

      1.9.5.3. Con:
      Social media has given everybody an equal voice; while democratic, this has often given equal weight to bad reasoning that then gets spread via social media.

        1.9.5.3.1. Con:
        That is what democracy is all about, everybody should be able to express themselves for good or wrong, it is up each person to make a choice.

        1.9.5.3.2. Pro:
        Social media has not been configured to allow users to find less-popular content creators [56].

    1.9.6. Con:
    Social media is simply a communication tool, like email or texting. Since it allows mass communication with friends and loved ones quickly and easily, it is actually strongly reinforcing our democracy and freedom of speech (provided the big tech sites actually allow free speech vs. censor it).

    1.9.7. Pro:
    Social media can be manipulated and unreasonable positions can be made seem more popular, more broadly supported and legitimate than they really are.
