Discussion Name:
Has Donald Trump's lying undermined the influence of his position as the US President?

1. Thesis:
Donald Trump's lies have undermined the influence of his position as the US President.

  1.1. Pro:
  Trumps lies have encouraged members of his team and conservatives to turn their back on him.

    1.1.1. Pro:
    Olivia Troye [1], the former advisor to the VP of Homeland Security has shared how Trump lied about Dr. Fauci.

  1.2. Con:
  It is normal for politicians to lie in order to push a specific agenda. Trump's lying is no different.

    1.2.1. Pro:
    Trump's lying follows a historical [2] pattern that most US presidents and politicians have followed and is therefore accepted as common politician behavior.

    1.2.2. Con:
    A growing number of Americans called the 'Exhausted Majority [3]' are tired of the increasingly hostile political climate during Trump's presidency. For many, it has hit a new low.

  1.3. Con:
  The mainstream media misconstrues and manipulates Donald Trump's words regularly to the point where its impact is inherently polarizing on the people.

    1.3.1. Pro:
    President Trump has vehemently expressed and opposed the manipulative tactics [4] mainstream media uses to distort his words.

    1.3.2. Pro:
    It is the internet that has undermined the voice of the president, as every word that is said can be fact-checked and misconstrued to the point where anything the president says becomes a polarizing issue.

      1.3.2.1. Con:
      Fact-checking is necessary as Trump has been known to give false information to the public.

        1.3.2.1.1. Pro:
        The medical community had to do damage control [5] after Trump's statements about injected disinfectant as a way to treat the coronavirus.

        1.3.2.1.2. Pro:
        Twitter had to remove [6] false coronavirus statistics posted by Trump.

    1.3.3. Pro:
    Because the media sensationalizes his words, much of what Trump says is negatively criticized.

  1.4. Pro:
  As the president, Trump's behavior should be that of a chief citizen. His lies have only reflected his disregard for this duty.

    1.4.1. Pro:
    Trump speaks in plainly partisan terms that hurt his position as representative of all citizens.

      1.4.1.1. Pro:
      In statements [7], Trump refers to "blue" and "red" states when talking about the impact of Covid-19.

      1.4.1.2. Pro:
      In a fundraising email [8], Trump says quote "They hate me. They hate you. They hate rallies and it’s all because they hate the idea of MAKING AMERICA GREAT AGAIN!" He promotes an "us versus them" mentality that is antithetical to being representative of all citizens and promotes dangerous polarization.

  1.5. Con:
  Trump is still regarded as a good president by many in the US.

    1.5.1. Pro:
    At the very least, Trump is not considered the worst president [9] the US has ever had.

    1.5.2. Pro:
    Most Republicans [10] still approve of Trump as president.

    1.5.3. Con:
    Only 14% [11] of all Americans approve of how Trump has handled things in the US.

    1.5.4. Pro:
    56% [12] of Americans say they're better off now than they were 4 years ago. The highest on record since 1984.

    1.5.5. Con:
    Monthly polls for 2020 show that the majority [13] of Americans disapprove of Trump's handling of the Presidency.

      1.5.5.1. Pro:
      Many Republicans and conservatives [14] are not voting for Trump in 2020 because they disapprove of his behavior as president.

        1.5.5.1.1. Pro:
        Some members of Republican Voters Against Trump (RVAT) believe his behavior has been largely 'unpresidential'.

  1.6. Pro:
  The president has unprecedented reach with his voice, and Donald Trump abuses this platform to push dangerous, fringe opinions and spread misinformation that has real consequences.

    1.6.1. Pro:
    Trump has repeatedly [15] tweeted [16] conspiracy theories that stoke violence.

      1.6.1.1. Con:
      There is a multitude of evidence that the Obama administration spied on the Trump administration. Attorney General Barr has said that spying did occur [17], and that it wasn't adequately predicated [18].

    1.6.2. Pro:
    In his silence [19] against white supremacy extremists, Trump is allowing them to start a race war.

    1.6.3. Pro:
    During a 2020 Whitehouse Briefing, Trump suggested injecting disinfectant [20] as a treatment for COVID-19. In the same month, poison control services saw a 20% increase [21] in calls regarding disinfectant exposure.

  1.7. Con:
  The lies that Trump has told are trivial and inconsequential to the influence of his position as President.

    1.7.1. Con:
    Trump repeatedly lied about the COVID-19 pandemic [22], and this cost thousands of Americans their lives.

      1.7.1.1. Con:
      The misinformation spread by Trump cannot be regarded as lies. Instead, they testify to his lack of scientific knowledge and general disregard for science [23].

        1.7.1.1.1. Con:
        Trump was fully informed about the seriousness of the pandemic, but his actions "sidelined, silenced and ignored [24]" scientists.

    1.7.2. Con:
    Trump repeatedly lied about his business prowess [25], which evidently painted a misleading picture of his leadership skills to American voters.

      1.7.2.1. Pro:
      Even prior to his presidency, lies [26] helped his companies "evade taxes or obtain favorable loan terms."

    1.7.3. Pro:
    The lies that Trump has told are so trivial that they often need to be misconstrued by the mainstream media in order to appear embarrassing or morally wrong enough to have some negative consequence to the influence of his position.

      1.7.3.1. Con:
      Some of Trump's lies are trivial, for instance if he were to lie about his crowd size at a rally, but Trump has also told non-trivial lies [27].

    1.7.4. Con:
    The frequency of Trump's lies has jeopardized his credibility.

      1.7.4.1. Pro:
      President Trump [28] has made more than "30,000 false or misleading claims [29]" over his presidency.

        1.7.4.1.1. Con:
        This source is not credible. Many of these fact checks are not checking facts, but instead Trump's personal opinions that he shares.

          1.7.4.1.1.1. Pro:
          The very first fact check that is displayed upon visiting the Washington Post's database is, "We built the greatest economy in history, not only for our country, but for the world. We were number one, by far." There are many possible criteria for which to judge an economy, to the point where this claim amounts to a subjective opinion, given that Trump did not specify a particular criteria his statement was based on.

    1.7.5. Con:
    Trump has repeatedly fabricated statements pertaining to Muslims which have no evidence or that have been found to be untrue. This has stoked racism and Islamophobia [30] across the United States.

      1.7.5.1. Pro:
      Trump accused a 'heavy Arab population' in New Jersey of cheering on the day of the 9/11 attacks. To date, there has been no evidence [31] to substantiate this claim.

    1.7.6. Con:
    Over the course of 3 days, Trump made factless claims [32] about election ballots for the 2020 US Presidency. This could influence voter behavior, and is therefore not trivial.

  1.8. Con:
  The president has the freedom of speech just as any citizen does, and is not bound by any "moral duty" to curtail his opinion.

    1.8.1. Con:
    As the President of the United States, it is his duty to set the best example of how to utilize free speech. He is currently not doing so.

      1.8.1.1. Pro:
      Trump often violates [33] the use of free speech online.

  1.9. Pro:
  The president should be a beacon of hope, but Donald Trump preaches predominately negative/doom-and-gloom opinions [34] that leave the people without strong oral leadership.

    1.9.1. Con:
    This is a false claim made with no supporting evidence. Trump's message is "Make America Great Again". He is the only presidential candidate with positivity. His whole platform is standing up for Americans.

    1.9.2. Con:
    Trump tends to be 'overly positive [35]' when relaying news to the public. This can be dangerous, as he downplays life-threatening situations.
