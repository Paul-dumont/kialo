Discussion Name:
Are trans women real women?

1. Thesis:
Transgender women are not women.

  1.1. Con:
  Refusing to recognise transgender women as women further exacerbates the plight of women and LGBTQ+ people worldwide.

    1.1.1. Pro:
    Increased social acceptance has been associated with improving [1] the health and life expectancy of transgender women.

      1.1.1.1. Con:
      Increased social acceptance has little bearing on many co-morbitdity factors that are specifically relevant to transgender people, such as deaths caused by endocrine disorders. [2]

      1.1.1.2. Pro:
      By combatting taboos, the identification and treatment of gender-related conditions such as gender dysphoria may become more commonplace in society.

        1.1.1.2.1. Con:
        Given the subjective nature of gender dysphoria, the deconstruction of taboos will not neccessarily improve the quality of gender care.

        1.1.1.2.2. Pro:
        Increasing social awarness and improving gender care tend to work on a mutually inclusive basis.

      1.1.1.3. Con:
      Increased social acceptance improves the health of all who are accepted. That doesn't put the onus on society to accept any and all groups, nor does it make the claims of those groups automatically true.

      1.1.1.4. Pro:
      Familial and societal rejection of someone's gender identity can affect their happiness, and has been strongly linked with the development [3] of mental health conditions.

        1.1.1.4.1. Pro:
        Transgender people are more likely to have attempted suicide [4] than other demographics, largely due to familial rejection, social ostracization, and other related phenomena.

        1.1.1.4.2. Con:
        Society (and therefore government) is not obligated to agree with an individual's self-identification, even if the disagreement would lead to negative impacts for the individual.

    1.1.2. Con:
    Hardships faced by other sections of the LGBTQ+ [5] community are influenced by factors that are unrelated to the acceptance of transgender women as 'real' women.

      1.1.2.1. Pro:
      Other sections of the LGBTQ+ community have historically opposed the acceptance of transgender people.

        1.1.2.1.1. Pro:
        Trans-exclusionary [6] and gender-critical feminists often advocate for the inclusion of other queer communities, whilst opposing the acceptance of transgender women.

        1.1.2.1.2. Con:
        Transgender women often work alongside allied sections of the queer community to promote inclusivity, acceptance, and understanding of the LGBTQ+ community.

          1.1.2.1.2.1. Pro:
          Transgender women notably led the resitance against police forces during the Stonewall riots [7], where they protested alongside other sections of the LGBTQ+ community.

      1.1.2.2. Pro:
      While identities under the queer umbrella range widely and each have struggles that look and feel different, many queer people understand that the work for liberation happens on many fronts and is partly overlapping. Some also carry several simultaneous queer identities. Identities that must actively fight today might not have to fight as fiercely later. Identities that are only fighting for awareness now might get into these heavier fights later, and will also have their liberation eventually.

    1.1.3. Pro:
    Transgender women should be entitled to unique legal protections from discrimination, violence, and poverty.

      1.1.3.1. Con:
      It shouldn't legally matter whether someone is transgender when they are facing poverty and violence. Special treatment (such as affirmative action [8]) is wrong.

      1.1.3.2. Pro:
      Transgender people are disproportionately affected by violent crime [9].

      1.1.3.3. Pro:
      Transgender people of color, in particular, experience extremely high rates of poverty [10].

      1.1.4.3. Pro:

          The linked study is not evidence that there aren't significant performance differences between males and females. The authors just state that they didn't find any research that would suggest otherwise.

In contrast, the belief of transwomen which is being disputed is specifically that they believe themselves to belong to a specific group, being real women.

        1.1.6.3.3. Con:

          1.1.6.3.3.1. Con:

We can instead recognize the apparent physical indicators of sex (e.g., certain sex characteristics like the presence of a beard), which correlate with the sexes to varying degrees of accuracy.

          1.2.1.2.1.1. Pro:
          Sex characteristics (e.g., the presence of a penis or the pattern of distribution of fat in the body) are indicators—not determinants—of the sex of an organism. They are the set of characteristics which typically correlate with—not determine—the sexes of members of a species of organism.

            1.2.1.2.1.1.1. Pro:
            In unusal cases, an organism may exhibit sex-atypical characteristics.

              1.2.1.2.1.1.1.1. Pro:

          1.2.1.2.1.2. Pro:

            1.2.1.2.1.2.1. Con:
            These indicators are used to recognize gender, not sex.

              1.2.1.2.1.2.1.1. Con:
              We can identify the sex of a person with some accuracy based on the apparent sexually dimorphic [44] characteristics they exhibit—traits which have been proven mostly specific to members of one of the two sexes.

                1.2.1.2.1.2.1.1.1. Con:
                This supports, rather than opposes, the idea that transgender women are women.

          1.2.1.2.1.3. Con:

            1.2.1.2.1.3.1. Pro:

                  1.2.1.2.1.3.2.1.1.1. Con:
                  Sex [47] is a notion of what reproductive function the organism has in general, no matter if it is fertile or not at the moment. In other words, female is an organism that was born with female reproductive organs [48].

                  1.2.1.2.1.3.2.1.1.2. Pro:

                    1.2.1.2.1.3.2.1.1.2.1. Con:

                  1.2.1.2.1.3.2.1.1.3. Pro:
