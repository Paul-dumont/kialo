Discussion Name:
Who is the better player; Messi or Ronaldo?

1. Thesis:
Messi is a better player than Ronaldo.

  1.1. Pro:
  Messi is a world-class playmaker; Ronaldo lacks this kind of game awareness and depth.

    1.1.1. Pro:
    Messi has won the award as best playmaker by the International Federation of Football History & Statistics five times [1]; Ronaldo never won it.

      1.1.1.1. Con:
      The organization has been called [2] "obscure" and "bizarre". It is questionable whether there is much expertise behind the titles it awards to players.

    1.1.2. Con:
    Playmaker describes one of many roles in a team [3]. Just because a player doesn't excel as a playmaker does not mean that they don't excel in other roles.

      1.1.2.1. Pro:
      The role of the playmaker is generally a midfielder position [4]. Players in more offensive or defensive roles usually do not do playmaker work therefore.

        1.1.2.1.1. Pro:
        Ronaldo is oftentimes cited as an excellent winger [5], and as a player who may have revolutionized this role [6].

      1.1.2.2. Con:
      The position is unique in that players who occupy it can make or break a team's efforts. Accordingly, the International Federation of Football History & Statistics has special awards [7] for this position as well as goalkeepers and goal scorers.

        1.1.2.2.1. Con:
        -> See 1.1.1.1.

    1.1.3. Pro:
    Messi has made more assists and key passes [8] than Ronaldo.

  1.2. Con:
  Ronaldo performs better in decisive matches and high-pressure situations.

    1.2.1. Pro:
    On three occasions, Ronaldo scored more than 15 goals [9] in a single Champions League season. Messi never scored more than 14 goals.

      1.2.1.1. Con:
      Across all his Champions League matches, Messi nonetheless has a slightly higher [10] average number of goals than Ronaldo.

      1.2.1.2. Pro:
      At about 117 minutes per goal [11], Ronaldo also scored much more frequently in matches against top-10 Champions League Teams. Messi scored only about every 180 minutes.

      1.2.1.3. Pro:
      Ronaldo is the only player [12] who has scored four goals in Champions League finals. This emphasizes how well he performs in high-pressure situations.

    1.2.2. Con:
    In matches against top teams [13], Messi on average scored much more frequently than Ronaldo.

    1.2.3. Pro:
    Ronaldo is a better penalty-taker than Messi. This indicates that Ronaldo performs better in high-pressure situations.

      1.2.3.1. Pro:
      Statistical analysis indicates [14] that success in penalty shots is a product of skill, not primarily of luck, as oftentimes assumed.

      1.2.3.2. Pro:
      Ronaldo scored about 85% [15] of his penalties whereas Messi scored only about 78%.

      1.2.3.3. Con:
      Penalties represent a situation so different from regular play that penalty-taking skills hardly say anything about the overall class of a player.

        1.2.3.3.1. Pro:
        Penalties occur about every four games [16] on average in European leagues.

  1.3. Pro:
  Messi plays more attractive soccer.

    1.3.1. Con:
    Attractive soccer does not equal good soccer.

      1.3.1.1. Pro:
      Especially teams well-known for their ability to play attractive soccer, like Brazil, had to reckon numerous times in the past with how successful soccer is not necessarily beautiful [17].

      1.3.1.2. Con:
      Considering that Ronaldo and Messi are both among the most successful players ever, it can be taken for granted that both play good soccer and that an attractive play style can set one apart from the other.

    1.3.2. Pro:
    Messi has produced significantly more [18] assisted goal shots than Ronaldo, and he did so more frequently. These oftentimes led to spectacular plays with his teammates.

      1.3.2.1. Con:
      To a significant degree, this difference between the two players can be explained [19] by Messi taking a lot of corners and free kicks for his teams, which Ronaldo generally did not do.

    1.3.3. Pro:
    Messi is widely [20] considered [21] one of the world's best dribblers; Ronaldo is not known for his dribbling.

      1.3.3.1. Pro:
      Statistically, Messi produces 4.5 successful dribbles [22] per game whereas Ronaldo does barely half that. This makes Messi much more exciting and impressive to watch.

      1.3.3.2. Pro:
      Messi has also been described as the world's best "on-ball value" performer [23] for his ability to move the ball closer to the opposing team's goal through his dribbles.

    1.3.4. Con:
    Ronaldo is almost 190cm tall whereas Messi is only 170cm. This makes Ronaldo a much more impressive figure.

      1.3.4.1. Con:
      This says little about their respective qualities as players. In soccer, short players can be at an advantage.

        1.3.4.1.1. Pro:
        Small players are more elusive for defenders [24] due to their quick step pattern. This benefits especially players who excel at dribbling, like Messi.

        1.3.4.1.2. Con:
        Short players are at a disadvantage in their air. Ronaldo, thus, is well-known [25] for his ability to "routinely outjump defenders" and "score spectacular headers".

      1.3.4.2. Pro:
      Ronaldo routinely scores headers that "defy gravity [26]", make him "levitate [27]", or are described as "wonder goals [28]".

  1.4. Con:
  Ronaldo traded for much more money than Messi.

    1.4.1. Con:
    For more than a decade, Messi's market value was estimated to be significantly higher [29] than Ronaldo's.

      1.4.1.1. Pro:
      Especially around 2018, the valuations of Messi were almost twice as high as those of Ronaldo (180 vs 100 Million [30]).

    1.4.2. Pro:
    In 2009, when Ronaldo transferred from Manchester United to Real Madrid for 80 Million Pounds [31], this was the most expensive transfer ever.

      1.4.2.1. Con:
      This comparison is questionable since Messi was never actually traded; he always changed clubs as a free transfer [32].

        1.4.2.1.1. Pro:
        This may also indicate that his market value was so high that a transfer was out of question for other clubs.

    1.4.3. Con:
    Transfer volume is a questionable measure of a football player's class.

      1.4.3.1. Pro:
      The money spent on transfers has been rising [33] for decades, far beyond what inflation can explain. Numerous other players have therefore traded for much more [34] than Ronaldo did.

      1.4.3.2. Con:
      Statistical analyses of past transfers suggest [35] that attributes like a player's skill at ball control determine their market value.

      1.4.3.3. Pro:
      Many factors [36] that are not directly related to a player's class help to determine a player's market value, such as their marketing value and injury susceptibility.

      1.4.3.4. Pro:
      Clubs that are flush with money, as is common in the Premiere League, oftentimes pay above market value [37] for players.

  1.5. Pro:
  Messi has won more individual awards than Ronaldo.

    1.5.1. Pro:
    The fact that Ronaldo is two years older and thus has had a longer career and more opportunities to win awards emphasizes that Messi is ahead of Ronaldo.

      1.5.1.1. Pro:
      Ronaldo appeared in the Premier League from 2003/04 on [38] for Manchester United; Messi didn't routinely play in La Liga for Barcelona until 2006/07 [39].

    1.5.2. Con:
    It is questionable whether awards say much about the qualities of a player; what matters happens on the soccer pitch.

      1.5.2.1. Pro:
      The way the Ballon d'Or is handed out has been criticized [40] for a long time by some of the sport's greatest.

      1.5.2.2. Con:
      Awards are supposedly given out for what happens on the soccer pitch.

    1.5.3. Pro:
    Messi won the Man of the Match award in about half [41] of the professional matches he took part in - even on Champions League level. Ronaldo achieved this in only about a third of his matches.

    1.5.4. Pro:
    Messi is the only player who won the World Cup Golden Ball [42] award twice. Ronaldo never won it and never won the Silver or Bronze Ball.

      1.5.4.1. Pro:
      The award is given out by a technical study group [43] at FIFA and therefore reflects expert opinions on a player's class.

    1.5.5. Pro:
    Messi has won 8 Ballon d’Or [44] awards, whereas Ronaldo has only won 5.

      1.5.5.1. Con:
      -> See 1.5.2.1.

      1.5.5.2. Pro:
      Considering that sports journalists [45] determine who gets the award, it can be considered a well-informed decision.

      1.5.5.3. Con:
      The difference between the number of Ballon d'Or awards both players have won is minor. Therefore, this is not a good metric to use in order to assess who the better player is.

        1.5.5.3.1. Pro:
        This is emphasized by the fact that no other player comes close to Ronaldo and Messi in this regard; no one else won the trophy more than three times [46]. Ronaldo and Messi are therefore in a league of their own.

        1.5.5.3.2. Pro:
        On four occasions when Messi won, Ronaldo placed second [47] in the voting for their trophy. It is questionable whether Messi's victory indicates a significant difference therefore.

  1.6. Con:
  Ronaldo has helped his teams win more awards than Messi.

    1.6.1. Con:
    Awards are a questionable metric in this context.

      1.6.1.1. Pro:
      A great player may be part of a mediocre team and therefore not win many awards.

        1.6.1.1.1. Pro:
        Highly regarded players like Robin van Persie [48] have never won any major titles.

        1.6.1.1.2. Con:
        This may be true short-term, but considering that soccer is big business, great players tend to gravitate towards great teams, for example due to financial incentives.

    1.6.2. Con:
    Messi won the World Cup in 2022 with Argentina; Ronaldo never won the sport's most prestigious title.

      1.6.2.1. Con:
      For Ronaldo, this is mostly a result of Portugal generally not performing well at World Cups. Since the 1960s, the team never even made it to the semi-final [49]. Ronaldo can hardly be blamed for that.

      1.6.2.2. Con:
      This has to be contextualized with Messi at times under-performing in World Cups, for example during the 2019 tournament where he did not score a single goal [50] over the course of five matches.

      1.6.2.3. Pro:
      On top of that, Messi was widely [51] considered [52] the best player of the 2022 World Cup - or at least far ahead [53] of Ronaldo in rankings.

    1.6.3. Pro:
    Ronaldo won the Champions League five times [54]; no player won it more often. Messi won the title only four times.

      1.6.3.1. Con:
      Considering that both players are among those who won the title the most times, it makes little sense to draw a distinction between them here.

      1.6.3.2. Pro:
      This is especially impressive considering that the Champions League is widely regarded as the world's most prestigious and competitive tournament [55].

    1.6.4. Con:
    Ronaldo may be more of an asset to his team but this does not prove that he is a better player than Messi on an individual level.

      1.6.4.1. Con:
      In a team sport like soccer, a great player cannot possibly be great on the individual level only; they have to be an asset for their team.

        1.6.4.1.1. Pro:
        Even awards like the Ballon d'Or now explicitly take into account [56] the team performance of players, not merely their individual skill.

      1.6.4.2. Pro:
      How much of an asset a player is for the team ultimately also depends on the team. Messi, in particular, has [57] been [58] described [59] in the past as being held back by the Argentinian national team - even by the team's coach.

  1.7. Pro:
  Messi is a better goal scorer than Ronaldo.

    1.7.1. Pro:
    Messi has a better goals per game [60] ratio than Ronaldo.

      1.7.1.1. Con:
      Raw goals are not necessarily a useful statistic since not every goal is decisive. Ronaldo, for example, ranks better than Messi in terms of the points won via his goals [61].

      1.7.1.2. Pro:
      On top of that, Messi required considerably fewer shots [62] to score a goal than Ronaldo throughout his career.

    1.7.2. Pro:
    Messi is the only player in history to score 91 goals [63] in a calendar year.

      1.7.2.1. Con:
      Messi appeared in significantly more matches that year than Ronaldo: 60 club matches and 16 national team matches, so 72 in total [64]. Ronaldo played 66 matches [65].

    1.7.3. Con:
    Overall, Ronaldo still has more international goals [66] than Messi.

      1.7.3.1. Con:
      International goals are a questionable metric since many national teams are weak, especially compared with the teams that both players faced in their European league matches.

      1.7.3.2. Con:
      Counting assists, Messi has a better minutes per goal contribution [67] stat than Ronaldo

      1.7.3.3. Pro:
      Ronaldo has a higher goal-per-game rate [68] than Messi in international games.

    1.7.4. Con:
    Ronaldo is considered [69] a better defensive player than Messi which partly explains and makes up for his less impressive goal record.

      1.7.4.1. Pro:
      By defensive metrics like clearances and blocked shots, Ronaldo clearly outperforms [70] Messi.

    1.7.5. Pro:
    At around 5.3 shots per goal [71], Messi takes significantly fewer shots to score than Ronald (at about 6.4).

      1.7.5.1. Pro:
      On top of that, Messi is significantly better [72] at scoring from outside the box and, thus, from difficult positions.

    1.7.6. Con:
    Ronaldo is the top scorer in the Champions League with 130 goals [73]. On the other hand, Messi is second with 115 goals.

      1.7.6.1. Con:
      Ronaldo has had more opportunities to score goals than Messi because he has played two more seasons [74]. Therefore, Ronaldo having more goals under his belt doesn't prove he is the better goal scorer.

    1.7.7. Con:
    -> See 1.2.3.
