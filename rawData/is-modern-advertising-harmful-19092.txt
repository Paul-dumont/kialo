Discussion Name:
Is modern advertising harmful?

1. Thesis:
Modern advertising is harmful.

  1.1. Pro:
  Modern advertising is not sustainable for the environment.

    1.1.1. Pro:
    Consumerism is bad for our environment because its increasing demands put extensive pressure on natural resources [1].

      1.1.1.1. Pro:
      This increase in consumerism contributes to a 'throw-away [2]' culture where everything is disposable, thus added to waste accumulation.

    1.1.2. Pro:
    Physical advertisements can be replaced by vegetation and artworks, having a more positive effect [3] on the environment and its citizens' wellbeing.

      1.1.2.1. Pro:
      'Green advertising [4]' has been increasing since the 1980s, due to the demand for more sustainable products and services.

    1.1.3. Con:
    Modern advertising per se has little environmental impact - especially given that much is now digital.

      1.1.3.1. Pro:
      Online advertising has increased [5] substantially, making paper advertisements not as effective.

        1.1.3.1.1. Pro:
        The 2020 Coronavirus pandemic has encouraged many companies to focus on online digital advertising [6] in place of more traditional methods. This is beneficial for the environment while also benefitting the health and wellbeing of citizens.

      1.1.3.2. Con:
      Online advertising contributes to more traffic, which isn’t good for the environment. sciencedirect.com [7]

    1.1.4. Pro:
    Flyers and posters become waste [8] once their short term relevance is over.

      1.1.4.1. Con:
      Most advertising is migrating [9] online.

        1.1.4.1.1. Pro:
        Newspaper, direct mail, and magazine advertising budgets make up less than 10% [10] of advertising spending. That means most of the focus is online, thereby decreasing tangible advertising waste.

    1.1.5. Pro:
    Product branding asks for excessive, exclusive and fancy packaging instead of maximizing sustainability of packaging. The packaging is still made to throw away thus causes unnecessary waste.

      1.1.5.1. Pro:
      Convenience packaging adds [11] to waste by promoting more single-serving size packaging.

    1.1.6. Pro:
    People often receive paper ads or ads-financed newspapers that they do not want and do not use. A lot of paper, fuel for supply and delivery and ink is needed for that. This also applies to physical ads that people want to receive.

    1.1.7. Pro:
    Advertising that incites exaggerated consumerism [12] leads to short-life span products that pollute the earth.

      1.1.7.1. Pro:
      Once consumers realize they do not need the product, it contributes to waste.

  1.2. Pro:
  Advertising makes profit by invading our minds.

    1.2.1. Pro:
    Consumerism causes moral degradation [13]; it tends to shift societies away from values such as integrity, instead focusing on materialism and competition.

    1.2.2. Pro:
    Advertisement usurping attention takes away from our minds capacity [14].

      1.2.2.1. Pro:
      -> See 1.1.2.

      1.2.2.2. Pro:
      Ads take up a big chunk of our time [15].

      1.2.2.3. Pro:
      Once corporate messages replace intrinsic/individual moral guidance, self actualization will be inhibited.

        1.2.2.3.1. Pro:
        without actualizing ourselves, we withhold ourselves (and other's) true happiness.

    1.2.3. Pro:
    Advertisements are annoying [16], having them around worsens moods.

      1.2.3.1. Con:
      Many people look forward to [17] advertisements that they enjoy, like the annual Super Bowl commercials.

      1.2.3.2. Con:
      People don't mind good ads, only [18] ones they deem as being bad.

      1.2.3.3. Pro:
      Modern advertising can negatively impact [19] self-esteem.

    1.2.4. Pro:
    Human subconscious becomes the warzone of brands' strife for our attention.

      1.2.4.1. Pro:
      After the advent of Clickfunnels, other funnel tools [20] were invented with a variety of ways for clients to advertise to rival this company.

    1.2.5. Pro:
    It can increase stress as you feel crammed, which has real health issues associated with it

    1.2.6. Pro:
    Subconscious advertising and product placement are both examples of modern advertising that influences the viewers decision without them knowing

  1.3. Con:
  Advertisement is a crucial organ [21] in capitalism.

    1.3.1. Pro:
    Advertising encourages [22] competition between markets to create top products and services.

    1.3.2. Pro:
    Capitalism drives innovation.

    1.3.3. Con:
    Capitalism itself is at least by now a harmful system when compared to hypothetical better alternative that is not communism and more based on real-world resources and scientific data. Therefore, being a crucial organ of that system is not an argument for but against modern advertising being beneficial to society/humanity.

    1.3.4. Con:
    There can be alternatives to modern advertising albeit these would be difficult to establish and sustain under capitalism where just neutral objective verified information about products is disseminated in some ways rather than usually quite uninformative types of manipulation.

  1.4. Con:
  Modern advertising can help raise awareness of new, helpful and useful products and services.

    1.4.1. Con:
    This kind of advertising persuades people to buy things they don't need.

      1.4.1.1. Pro:
      Buying things one doesn't need adds to waste.

        1.4.1.1.1. Pro:
        -> See 1.1.7.1.

        1.4.1.1.2. Pro:
        -> See 1.1.7.

    1.4.2. Pro:
    Digital advertising (ex. google ads) allows customers to find what they are looking for.

      1.4.2.1. Con:
      They may allow for that, but the greater effect is to increase demand for products (if someone really needs something, they will seek it, rather than wait for someone to offer it)

      1.4.2.2. Con:
      Finding what one is searching for is better achieved through a search engine. Digital advertisements are, rather, an unsolicited suggestion engine.

    1.4.3. Con:
    Since the invention of search engines, the informative function of ads has become obsolete [23].

      1.4.3.1. Pro:
      Any information one could want is easily filtered with just one search engine [24], like Google.

      1.4.3.2. Con:
      Advertising still serves the purpose of making potential new customers, that may not know to search for them, aware of their products and/or services.

    1.4.4. Con:
    Consumers are unable to discern whether the ad information is honest or deceitful, e.g doctors hired for advertisements, claiming that smoking was healthy.

    1.4.5. Pro:
    Sometimes it can raise the price consumers are willing to pay for a product which can at times enable or support more optimal products such as more durable ones instead of cheap short-lived ones for example.

    1.4.6. Con:
    This is usually just a very small fraction of ads any given person will be seeing. Ads are not the only way people can learn about products so this small fraction if any is not a good reason for it to not be usually and overall harmful.

    1.4.7. Con:
    This is more like a description of what advertising could be doing and is doing to a very small degree but doesn't really fit "modern" advertising – this would apply more to a separate novel type of advertising which for example only informs about sustainable/healthy/good and vetted products to consumers likely benefiting from these.

    1.4.8. Con:
    The point of most modern advertising is not to inform consumers and to promote choice, but to sell an enticing dream [25].

      1.4.8.1. Pro:
      Advertising can not deliver sufficiently neutral and useful product information [26] since it's set out to advocate for one particular product as it's purpose. That product is usually suboptimal or otherwise problematic and even if that is not the case, the ad-viewer cannot know.

      1.4.8.2. Pro:
      For example, most car commercials usually focus on showing a car performing incredible turns, climbing a mountain, being ridden by a supermodel, and so on. There's very little (or none) information about its mechanical aspects.

    1.4.9. Pro:
    Modern advertising can bring new medical treatments into public awareness.

      1.4.9.1. Con:
      As a rule, consumers aren't qualified to prescribe medical treatments. For this reason, all such treatments advise one to consult one's doctor, but if one's doctor is trustworthy, they will and should be the one to recommend the treatment, rather than an advertiser.

      1.4.9.2. Con:
      Advertisers have a conflict of interest: they are looking to sell products and so can't be relied on to look out for the best interests of people's health.

    1.4.10. Pro:
    Modern advertising can help [27] nonprofits accrue the finances necessary to improve their dedicated cause.

  1.5. Pro:
  Media platforms [28] push harmful (e.g. polarizing) content to increase user's engagement time thus ad revenue

  1.6. Pro:
  Advertising often advocates for products that are harmful to health directly (such as disposable e-cigs or fast food) or indirectly (such as climate change misinformation or cars). Such ads are largely not restricted [29] and prevalent.

  1.7. Con:
  Modern advertising can help small businesses grow.

    1.7.1. Pro:
    With widespread access to the internet, small businesses have access to alternative means of growing their clientele and reach.

      1.7.1.1. Pro:
      Small businesses can place targeted social media ads to reach their true customer base.

        1.7.1.1.1. Pro:
        Social media marketing helps [30] small business convey their branding to millions of users already showing interest in their product or service.

    1.7.2. Pro:
    Modern techniques, such as guerrilla marketing [31], are a helpful tool to help small businesses make lasting impressions on its target market when up against big corporations.

      1.7.2.1. Con:
      This tactic, though less expensive, is no less accessible to big companies. meaning small companies have no advantage over big companies in this case.

    1.7.3. Con:
    The prices of ads are so high that they promote big businesses. Small businesses don't have the money, nor capacity in skilled teams to run against corporations.

      1.7.3.1. Con:
      There are many creative free and affordable ways a small business can advertise until they increase their revenue.

        1.7.3.1.1. Pro:
        There are affordable [32] advertising agencies tailors toward small businesses.

        1.7.3.1.2. Pro:
        Small businesses can generate [33] buzz through word-of-mouth.

        1.7.3.1.3. Pro:
        Many larger corporations started small and needed affordable ways to advertise in order to grow. This cycle is true for many new businesses but should not hinder them.

  1.8. Con:
  Modern advertising can improve the economy.

    1.8.1. Pro:
    Improving the economy [34] increases the incomes of citizens while decreasing unemployment rates. This is a social good.

    1.8.2. Pro:
    Consumerism means greater consumption and spending, leading to economic growth [35].

      1.8.2.1. Pro:
      Economic growth improves the standards of living within a society.

        1.8.2.1.1. Pro:
        Consumerism encourages innovation [36], because of the pressure of competition.

          1.8.2.1.1.1. Pro:
          As companies see the success of certain advertising methods, it inspires them to improve upon the flaws of that method in an attempt to outperform their competitors.

            1.8.2.1.1.1.1. Pro:
            -> See 1.2.4.1.

      1.8.2.2. Con:
      Infinite growth of the economy is impossible on the finite planet. See relevant thread on Kialo [37] and Degrowth definition on Wiki [38].

    1.8.3. Pro:
    Modern advertising contributes to job creation.

      1.8.3.1. Pro:
      In the European Union (EU), advertising has contributed to 5.8 million jobs [39].

      1.8.3.2. Pro:
      Advertising helps promote jobs and create jobs for modellers and media

    1.8.4. Pro:
    Modern techniques, such as e-marketing can reach more customers [40] than older methods, meaning increased profits and more money driving the economy forward.

      1.8.4.1. Con:
      Funding services through advertisement is an equalizing force. Users indirectly pay proportionally to their available budget, through marketing costs in things they buy.

    1.8.5. Con:
    -> See 1.8.2.2.

  1.9. Pro:
  Modern advertising exaggeratedly incites consumerism.

    1.9.1. Con:
    This is not a bad thing. Most modern societies are consumerist [41] in nature. Inciting consumerism is beneficial.

      1.9.1.1. Con:
      -> See 1.1.1.

      1.9.1.2. Con:
      -> See 1.2.1.

      1.9.1.3. Pro:
      -> See 1.8.2.1.1.

      1.9.1.4. Con:
      -> See 1.1.7.

    1.9.2. Con:
    -> See 1.8.2.

    1.9.3. Pro:
    -> See 1.1.1.

    1.9.4. Pro:
    -> See 1.2.1.

    1.9.5. Con:
    -> See 1.8.2.1.1.

    1.9.6. Pro:
    Effective advertising maximizes the delta between what we actually need versus what we buy.

      1.9.6.1. Pro:
      An exceptional salesperson is often one that is able to convince people to buy products they don't need. The sames applies to advertisement.

      1.9.6.2. Pro:
      The tobacco [42] industry is one of the more egregious [43] examples of this, especially with advertisement that increasingly targets younger audiences [44].

    1.9.7. Pro:
    Adverts can use subliminal messaging [45] to falsely engender a feeling of want/need where there is none.

      1.9.7.1. Con:
      -> See 1.4.

      1.9.7.2. Pro:
      Adverts use color psychology [46] to visual stimulate and entice consumers.

    1.9.8. Pro:
    -> See 1.1.7.

  1.10. Pro:
  Advertising can be used to augment information.

    1.10.1. Con:
    This is dependent on the product and advertising method of the company. It is not a problem attributed to all modern advertising.

    1.10.2. Pro:
    Advertising, manipulative in nature [47], is a threat to true democracy.

      1.10.2.1. Pro:
      Advertisement has risen up to it's contemporary form by British propaganda campaign [48] to recruit soldiers for WW2.

      1.10.2.2. Pro:
      -> See 1.9.7.

    1.10.3. Con:
    Augmenting information can be beneficial to the consumer if it convinces them to purchase a product that is beneficial to them.

      1.10.3.1. Pro:
      Some pharmaceutical companies use visual imagery [49] to convey complex information that patients may not otherwise understand.

        1.10.3.1.1. Pro:
        This enhances the information in a way that allows the patient to make an informed choice, which is important for their health.

      1.10.3.2. Con:
      The advertisement is commissioned by a company that wants consumers to buy the product, rather than being an impartial party in the customers best interest.

      1.10.3.3. Con:
      Augmenting information can present anything as beneficial. overthrowing individuals authority and responsibility on what is beneficial to them, takes away the only agent that can decide whether it ís indeed beneficial.

    1.10.4. Pro:
    Adverts can heavily skew public opinion on what products and services are objectively "good".

      1.10.4.1. Pro:
      -> See 1.4.8.

    1.10.5. Pro:
    On many mediums, loose regulations mean adverts can be intentionally misleading and sensationalizing.

      1.10.5.1. Pro:
      It can be hard to discern when one is being advertised to rather than being presented with facts and accurate information on mediums that should be trustworthy (news, informational websites etc)

      1.10.5.2. Pro:
      Merchants intentionally create a false sense of urgency [50] in order to encourage consumers to 'buy now'.

      1.10.5.3. Pro:
      Adverts often use sex and sexual innuendos [51] to sell goods and services.

        1.10.5.3.1. Pro:
        Women are often depicted as sexual objects, which can promote body dissatisfaction [52].

      1.10.5.4. Pro:
      They also play deceiving word games, such as products that say "made with natural ingredients", when actually saying "includes 2% of natural ingredients" would be more accurate.

    1.10.6. Pro:
    Propaganda (in the sense of subliminally subjecting to people the propagators will) is unfair as it bypasses the individual's authority of choice.

      1.10.6.1. Pro:
      Advertisers use card-stacking propaganda [53] to omit any information that may paint them in a negative light to the consumer, thus skewing their perspective.

      1.10.6.2. Pro:
      Advertisers use influencers [54] or other experts in their niche to vouch for their product; sometimes in exchange for pay although they haven't used the product.

    1.10.7. Pro:
    Advertising often and usually aim to market products that aren't the best or among the best ones in terms of metrics like durability and efficacy. This means in skews global production toward less optimal products.

  1.11. Con:
  Modern advertising allows for massive free services.

    1.11.1. Con:
    It is not free, users pay for the service through paying for the advertised product.

      1.11.1.1. Pro:
      To simply pay for the service separately and the product separately should be cheaper, since the ad company can be moved out of the chain.

      1.11.1.2. Pro:
      This system depends on consumption of product B for product A to exist; an unnecessary detour.

        1.11.1.2.1. Pro:
        This system codependent on each other's consumption stimulates over consumption.

    1.11.2. Pro:
    Media (partially) funded by advertising [55] becomes more easily accessible to all.

      1.11.2.1. Pro:
      Advertising is thus good since it helps more people to be informed of global and local issues.

        1.11.2.1.1. Con:
        This doesn't help the billions in poorer countries that have no access [56] to advertising and thus cannot keep up with issues beyond their city.

          1.11.2.1.1.1. Pro:
          During the pandemic, not having this access, is life-threatening. Advertising only is helpful in this case to those who can access it.

    1.11.3. Con:
    Wikipedia shows that donation based monetization is an alternative to ad based monetization.

      1.11.3.1. Con:
      This can't be scaled up much more than currently the case. People won't donate much more frequently for all sorts of things.

      1.11.3.2. Con:
      Open source software shows this isn't really an alternative – even heavily-used very useful popular software developed by just few developers barely get any donations.

    1.11.4. Con:
    State funding could be and alternative for services that are beneficial for the public, just like libraries, schools and public broadcasting.

      1.11.4.1. Con:
      It would massively drain state funds that are needed elsewhere.

      1.11.4.2. Con:
      It's not so simple to decide what is or isn't beneficial for the public. This becomes clear for example with the EU debate whether or not to consider nuclear power "green" energy and many people's opinion that cars and car infrastructure is beneficial for the public.

    1.11.5. Con:
    Many of these free services could become free by reducing costs a lot. For example, peer-to-peer technologies allow distribution of media at essentially just the user's costs for data storage space, computer electricity, and their Internet connectivity. With various technological and cultural-systemic changes, many other services could cut costs a lot as well.

    1.11.6. Pro:
    Universally available and free Gmail, Facebook, Youtube, and other social media platforms are funded [57] through advertising.

      1.11.6.1. Con:
      Such platforms become at risk of corrupting into a device for harvesting attention to sell to advertisement, rather than their initial function.

        1.11.6.1.1. Pro:
        As far back as in 1830, when Benjamin Day made the first cheaply accessible newspaper relying on advertisement [58] instead of normal sales, subjects shifted over to more sensational, up to even creating fake news to attract greater audience [59].
