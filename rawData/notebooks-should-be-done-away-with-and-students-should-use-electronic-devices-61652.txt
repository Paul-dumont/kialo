Discussion Name:
Notebooks should be done away with and students should use electronic devices.

1. Thesis:
Notebooks should be done away with and students should use electronic devices.

  1.1. Con:
  Electronic devices can be costly, and not all students may have access to them.

  1.2. Con:
  Some students may have difficulty adapting to working on electronic devices.

    1.2.1. Con:
    Being able to use electronic devices is a necessity in today's world of work, so this is all the more reason to help students with such problems as early as possible.

  1.3. Con:
  Electronic devices are prone to technical difficulties, which could hinder learning.

    1.3.1. Con:
    Learning to identify and manage technical problems, as they arise, could be a way for students to improve their knowledge about electronic devices and the technology they employ. This is an important skill in the information age.

      1.3.1.1. Con:
      Although this may be a valuable skill set to learn; it will take away time from learning and following the written curriculum for each subject.

    1.3.2. Con:
    Schools should have technical help centers that help students fix problems. At the very least, they should have spare devices that students can use until they can be fixed.

  1.4. Con:
  Students may be more likely to get distracted [1] when using electronic devices to study.

  1.5. Pro:
  Electronic devices are more versatile and easier to use than notebooks.

  1.6. Pro:
  Electronic devices can be used to access a wider range of educational materials and resources.

  1.7. Pro:
  Electronic devices are more environmentally friendly, as they don't require the printing of paper.
