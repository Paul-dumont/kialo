Discussion Name:
The healthiest diet for man contains only meat and animal fats.

1. Thesis:
The healthiest diet for man contains only meat and animal fats.

  1.1. Pro:
  Paleo Medicina, in Hungary, claims to cure cancer with this diet.

    1.1.1. Con:
    Claims are not proof.

  1.2. Pro:
  Modern day diets cause disease.

    1.2.1. Con:
    This does not prove that an only meat diet is healthier or even sufficient.

  1.3. Pro:
  Sugar and vegetable oils are extremely harmful to mankind.

    1.3.1. Con:
    There are other foods one can eat that do not contain sugar or vegetable oils. Eating only meat isn't the only option.

      1.3.1.1. Pro:
      Whole foods [1] like vegetables, fish, legumes and nuts and seeds don't contain sugar or vegetable oils.

    1.3.2. Con:
    Vegetable oils are not unhealthy. This is a false claim.

  1.4. Con:
  Only eating meat is dangerous for human health.

    1.4.1. Pro:
    Eating lots of meat can lead to constipation [2].

    1.4.2. Pro:
    Meat [3] is high in saturated fat, cholesterol, and sodium. Too much of these nutrients can result in or aggravate disease.

      1.4.2.1. Pro:
      Researchers have found [4] that saturated fat can raise heart disease factors, like LDL.

  1.5. Con:
  Only eating meat and animal fat is detrimental to the environment.

    1.5.1. Pro:
    Only eating meat is unsustainable [5] for the planet.

      1.5.1.1. Pro:
      Eating a vegan diet cuts carbon emission by 60% [6].

      1.5.1.2. Pro:
      Meat factories produce substantial amounts of air pollution [7]. People exposed can suffer [8] from lung irritation, headaches, dizziness, and nausea.

  1.6. Con:
  A diet balanced in all of the macronutrients is the healthiest.

  1.7. Pro:
  Many people have lived a healthy life for years living only on meat and fats.

    1.7.1. Con:
    Many people have lived a long life smoking without complications. That doesn't prove that smoking is healthy.

  1.8. Con:
  Excessive meat consumption is associated with heart disease [9], cancer [10] and diabetes [11].

  1.9. Con:
  Meat does not contain all essential nutrients.

    1.9.1. Pro:
    Meat does not contain enough vitamin C

    1.9.2. Pro:
    Meat does not contain enough vitamin E

    1.9.3. Pro:
    Meat does not contain enough folate.

    1.9.4. Pro:
    Meat does not contain any fiber.

  1.10. Pro:
  Our ancestors lived on meat.

    1.10.1. Con:
    The earliest humans consumed mostly [12] vegetation.

    1.10.2. Con:
    Early humans evolved [13] to eat meat purely out of survival in tough climates. It is unnecessary to do so given today's modern conveniences.

    1.10.3. Con:
    Our ancestors did not live on only meat.

  1.11. Pro:
  The carnivore diet has been known to cure autoimmune diseases.

    1.11.1. Con:
    This is based purely on anecdotes and not science.
