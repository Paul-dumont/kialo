Discussion Name:
Should school uniforms be banned?

1. Thesis:
School uniforms should be banned.

  1.1. Pro:
  School uniforms are expensive.

    1.1.1. Pro:
    The costs of school uniforms are discriminatory.

      1.1.1.1. Pro:
      Most public schools with mandatory uniform policies are in low-income neighborhoods [1] (p. viii).

      1.1.1.2. Pro:
      Girls' school uniforms are often more expensive [2] than those for boys.

    1.1.2. Pro:
    It is impractical that students need to have school uniforms in addition to their free time clothes, instead of being allowed to wear the same clothes for school and free time.

      1.1.2.1. Con:
      With uniforms, students don't have to worry about what to wear everyday.

        1.1.2.1.1. Pro:
        School uniforms save students' time [3] which may in turn improve punctuality.

        1.1.2.1.2. Con:
        School uniforms cancel out the stress of having to follow a certain dress code, by adding the stress of having to follow a certain uniform code.

        1.1.2.1.3. Pro:
        School uniforms simplify morning routines, as the child wears the same thing every day.

          1.1.2.1.3.1. Con:
          The benefit to morning routines is marginal, as children still need to do other things to organise themselves for school, such as showering, getting their books together, ensuring their uniform is clean.

          1.1.2.1.3.2. Pro:
          Anything done to make a school morning less stressful is beneficial [4], as it allows children to have more sleep, essential [5] to a successful day, or to prepare themselves mentally for school.

          1.1.2.1.3.3. Pro:
          Simplified morning routines allow less stress for the parents [6], who do not have to worry about their children picking out clothing each morning.

        1.1.2.1.4. Pro:
        School uniform help students pay more attention to their studies instead of fashion, which helps them learn more.

        1.1.2.1.5. Con:
        Students still have to worry about whether their uniforms are clean or ironed.

      1.1.2.2. Pro:
      Many students change out of their uniforms after school, so that uniforms are of no benefit beyond the hours they are in school.

      1.1.2.3. Con:
      The typical school uniform should be changed to a more practical attire, but not banned.

    1.1.3. Pro:
    School uniform costs force families into debt [7].

      1.1.3.1. Pro:
      A study by The Children’s Society has found [8] that one million children across England live in families who are getting into debt to meet the rising cost of school uniforms.

      1.1.3.2. Pro:
      According to Speak Out, the average cost of the school uniform for a student is around $104 [9].

        1.1.3.2.1. Con:
        Considering that a middle-income family spends $12,980 [10] per year raising a child from birth to age in 18 in the United States. $104 for school uniforms is fairly inconsequential, even when assuming families spend $104 annually, which would make uniform expenses around 1/124 of the annual spending.

      1.1.3.3. Pro:
      The UK Department for Education found [11] that school uniforms cost almost £230 per pupil.

        1.1.3.3.1. Con:
        This is a hasty generalization fallacy. The source cited is a report that considers data from the United Kingdom solely, ergo, this could only justify school uniform expensiveness in the United Kingdom.

        1.1.3.3.2. Con:
        Few [12] parents find that the cost of school uniforms affects which school their child attends.

      1.1.3.4. Pro:
      According to a report by The Children’s Society [13], families with children at secondary school are paying an average of more than £300 per child, per year in school uniform costs.

      1.1.3.5. Con:
      Getting secondhand [14] school uniforms can help to reduce the cost.

        1.1.3.5.1. Con:
        Students who wear second hand uniforms may be bullied for doing so.

        1.1.3.5.2. Con:
        Some children wear out [15] their school uniforms, and so there may not be enough items for sale second hand.

        1.1.3.5.3. Pro:
        Many children simply outgrow [16] their uniforms, and so there would be lots of good-quality items of clothing for sale.

        1.1.3.5.4. Pro:
        Buying second hand school uniforms can also help reduce waste, which in turn is beneficial for the environment [17].

      1.1.3.6. Pro:
      Some schools have children change [18] their uniforms every three years to ensure that different aged children can be distinguished. This drives up the cost even more.

      1.1.3.7. Pro:
      The cost of school uniforms has been steadily increasing [19].

        1.1.3.7.1. Con:
        The inflation rate in the United States is 6.80% [20] as of November 2021, which implies that general prices are increasing, not only the cost of school uniforms. Therefore, this rise in school uniform prices should be of little concern to parents, schools and students.

    1.1.4. Con:
    Schools could provide subsidized [21] or free uniforms and supplies.

    1.1.5. Con:
    Purchasing normal clothing for non-school uniform schools can often end up being more expensive [22].

      1.1.5.1. Pro:
      A study [23] of uniform costs in the United Kingdom found that uniforms cost parents $128.79 per outfit, while out-of-school outfits averaged $165.79.

      1.1.5.2. Con:
      Students will have non-school clothes for their free time anyway, so the school uniform leads to additional cost.

      1.1.5.3. Pro:
      Students may want to often buy new clothing [24] to adapt to new fashion trends.

    1.1.6. Con:
    School uniforms are already on lists of items included in many sales-tax-free items [25].

  1.2. Con:
  School uniforms are aesthetically pleasing.

    1.2.1. Con:
    Most students [26] do not find school uniforms aesthetically pleasing.

      1.2.1.1. Con:
      It is not significant if students don't find school uniforms beautiful. A survey [27] found that 70% of students dislike homework, yet this does not justify the abolition of homework.

        1.2.1.1.1. Pro:
        This argument falls into the argumentum ad populum [28] fallacy. Just because the majority of students don't like uniforms does not mean they should be banned.

        1.2.1.1.2. Pro:
        Teachers and school administrators are entrusted to act in the best interests of their students, even if it means enforcing something disliked by the students.

      1.2.1.2. Pro:
      Most students find uniforms to be ugly, as they are not tailored to their body type [29].

    1.2.2. Pro:
    Many school uniforms around the world are considered stylish [30] and fashionable.

      1.2.2.1. Con:
      Many people choose their clothes because they find them aesthetically pleasing [31]. Giving students the choice over what they wear will lead to more wearing stylish clothes.

      1.2.2.2. Pro:
      School uniforms in Russia incorporate red handkerchiefs [32] to add a touch of colour to the outfits.

      1.2.2.3. Pro:
      The white and green colours of the Myanmar [33] state school uniform is pleasing to the eye.

    1.2.3. Pro:
    School uniforms tend to avoid extremely bright colours [34] which can be garish and unpleasant to the eye.

      1.2.3.1. Con:
      Bright colours can help spread positivity [35] and help increase one's work performance [36].

    1.2.4. Con:
    The question of aesthetic value is subjective. As not everyone thinks that uniforms are aesthetically pleasing, they cannot be justified on these grounds.

      1.2.4.1. Con:
      While aesthetics may be subjective, the study of what makes particular clothing or styles aesthetically pleasing is can be objectively studied [37].

  1.3. Pro:
  School uniforms are unfair for students.

    1.3.1. Pro:
    School uniforms are an infringement of an individual's freedom and right to self-expression [38].

      1.3.1.1. Pro:
      It enforces conformity in society at the cost of individuality which is vital to young developing human beings.

        1.3.1.1.1. Pro:
        School children are an a crucial age where they are forming their personal identities and defining who they are. Uniforms inhibit their ability to do this and thus hinder their development.

          1.3.1.1.1.1. Con:
          Time at school where you would have to wear a uniform is just a small part of a kid's waking hours, thus the impact is minimal.

            1.3.1.1.1.1.1. Con:
            Not considering at least eight hours [39] dedicated to sleeping (may be more in the case of small children [40]), a normal day has 16 hours, of those, children spend an average of six, eight hours [41] (in the US) at school, which is almost half of their time wearing a uniform, and therefore its impact may not minimal.

              1.3.1.1.1.1.1.1. Pro:
              Additionally, the time children spend at school is, in general, a big part of the time they spend away from their homes, and thus it is the period where they learn to be autonomous and form the their personal identities apart from their families.

              1.3.1.1.1.1.1.2. Con:
              Throughout the year, children spend more days [42] off school than in school.

        1.3.1.1.2. Con:
        Both skills, conforming as well as being a unique individual are required to succeed in life. School teaches you both.

        1.3.1.1.3. Con:
        There are plenty of ways of being unique, besides dressing in an individualistic way at school.

        1.3.1.1.4. Pro:
        A culture of school uniforms can easily turn into a conformational "mob" culture. [43]

          1.3.1.1.4.1. Pro:
          In a mob mentality, where students are trained to conform, they are also more likely to give into peer pressure. [44]

        1.3.1.1.5. Pro:
        Uniforms limit students to not be themselves: a persons' style can represent what they're into (hobbies, likes, dislikes, etc...) but uniforms make it so they can't do that.

          1.3.1.1.5.1. Con:
          Clothing are superficial and shouldn't be important for the personality.

      1.3.1.2. Pro:
      In the absence of school uniforms, students will be able to express themselves, which can lead to an improvement in their self-confidence.

        1.3.1.2.1. Con:
        Research has found that the self-confidence of students actually improves when they wear uniforms (p. 15 [45]).

          1.3.1.2.1.1. Pro:
          Students with school uniforms do not have to spend time focusing on differences in self-chosen clothing, leaving them more time [46] to focus on academia and social life confidence.

        1.3.1.2.2. Con:
        An increase in self-confidence happens instead when students are made to do something out of the comfort zone.

        1.3.1.2.3. Pro:
        When students are forced to wear clothes that may not be flattering, they experience a dip [47] in self-confidence.

        1.3.1.2.4. Pro:
        Children, at the age they are in school, are growing and figuring out who they are [48]. School uniforms stymie this.

        1.3.1.2.5. Pro:
        School uniforms violate a student's right to creativity and self-expression [49], especially concerning LBGTQ+ matters.

      1.3.1.3. Pro:
      School uniform policies infringe on American student's First Amendment rights [50].

        1.3.1.3.1. Con:
        School uniform policies only infringe on First Amendment rights if they are meant to suppress students' individual opinions [51].

          1.3.1.3.1.1. Pro:
          School policies that prevent students from wearing clothing or accessories that are vulgar, promote illegal drug usage, clothing likely to cause serious disruption to the learning environment are not unconstitutional [52].

          1.3.1.3.1.2. Con:
          School uniforms inherently suppress students' individual opinions as they deny them the choice of what to wear, which may convey their opinion about something, be it something basic such as what colours they like, or something more significant, such as politics.

        1.3.1.3.2. Pro:
        In Tinker v. Des Moines, the Supreme Court found that schools could not stop students from wearing black armbands to protest the Vietnam War [53] regardless of their uniform policy.

        1.3.1.3.3. Pro:
        The ACLU has found clothing choice to be a "crucial form of self-expression" [54] and the "most basic self-determination."

      1.3.1.4. Con:
      School uniforms develop an individual's personality more effectively [55] by teaching students that their personalities are more than what they wear.

        1.3.1.4.1. Con:
        Clothing and accessories are still a popular avenue of personal expression for both children and adults, which should not be suppressed.

        1.3.1.4.2. Pro:
        In the absence of school uniforms, materialism is encouraged, with a focus on tying ones' clothing to ones' sense of self.

      1.3.1.5. Pro:
      School uniforms are an outdated practice.

        1.3.1.5.1. Con:
        This by itself is not a logical reason to ban school uniforms. If it is being suggested as an argument by itself, this argument contains the appeal to novelty fallacy [56], as the "newer" student-choice clothes policy is claimed to be superior due to it being new or modern.

        1.3.1.5.2. Pro:
        Many school uniforms were intended to signify religious affiliation [57] and were used in monastic and priestly training.

          1.3.1.5.2.1. Con:
          School uniforms, as they are known in the modern world, are not related to those historical religious examples [58].

        1.3.1.5.3. Con:
        The US historically did not have school uniforms, but there has been a movement [59] towards them in the last few decades.

          1.3.1.5.3.1. Pro:
          School uniform usage is increasing [60] across the U.S. currently.

      1.3.1.6. Pro:
      Uniforms prevent students from fully tranisitoning into adulthood [61], when they are expected to choose clothing and dress themselves.

      1.3.1.7. Con:
      School uniforms often allow for accessorization [62] through which children can develop individually through controlled means.

      1.3.1.8. Con:
      Students can still express themselves with accessories like jewelry.

        1.3.1.8.1. Con:
        Many schools do not allow students to wear accessories.

      1.3.1.9. Con:
      Freedom and liberty should have their boundaries and limitations. If school uniforms are considered an infringement of personality and liberty, the same argument should apply to compulsory subjects in schools.

    1.3.2. Pro:
    School uniforms enforce stereotypes [63] and gender roles, which can be harmful for students.

      1.3.2.1. Pro:
      The usual dress code for schools require girls to wear skirts and boys to wear shorts, which reinforces entrenched masculine-feminine stereotypes [64] in young children.

        1.3.2.1.1. Pro:
        Gendered uniforms such as skirts are meant to highlight [65] a girl’s femininity, while pants or shorts enforce a boy’s masculinity.

        1.3.2.1.2. Pro:
        The length of girls’ skirt [66] is often looked upon in schools, and links a girl’s morale with the length of her skirt. This allows the school to discipline her body, while simultaneously enforcing a feminine stereotype.

      1.3.2.2. Pro:
      Uniforms allow schools to police [67] young men and women in accordance with the gendered stereotypes within the society.

      1.3.2.3. Pro:
      Uniforms can present a real challenge to students who are unsure of their gender identity or who want to experiment with different forms of gender presentation.

        1.3.2.3.1. Pro:
        Children who do not identify with their birth gender need a school environment which supports them [68], especially because they may be facing difficult decisions in their lives.

        1.3.2.3.2. Con:
        Many schools have adopted a uni-sex uniform approach [69] to address such concerns.

          1.3.2.3.2.1. Pro:
          At Treetops College [70], part of the PE/sports uniform is a unisex tracksuit that students can wear in place of the formal school uniform.

        1.3.2.3.3. Con:
        The solution to this is to allow students to have more choice concerning what their school uniform should consist of. The solution is not to ban school uniforms.

        1.3.2.3.4. Con:
        Some schools allow boys to wear skirts and girls to wear trousers.

          1.3.2.3.4.1. Pro:
          Parson Street primary school in Bristol [71] has a gender-neutral uniform policy for all its students, allowing girls to wear whatever the boys could wear, and boys to wear skirts.

          1.3.2.3.4.2. Pro:
          St Brigid’s National School [72] in Dublin allows students to wear skirts or trousers regardless of their gender.

      1.3.2.4. Con:
      Uniforms do not have to enforce [73] stereotypes; one of the oldest manufacturers of uniforms in the UK instated a policy of only selling gender-neutral uniforms [74].

        1.3.2.4.1. Con:
        Some schools have seen protests [75] upon introducing gender neutral uniforms.

          1.3.2.4.1.1. Con:
          The protests were not specifically against the idea of gender neutral uniforms, but rather against the fact that trousers were made mandatory [76] for all, which undermined student choice and led to wasted skirts.

        1.3.2.4.2. Pro:
        The Liberal Democrats political party has proposed a ban [77] on all uniforms that are not gender-neutral.

      1.3.2.5. Con:
      Dress codes also enforce stereotypes and dress codes [78], and even without mandatory uniforms schools are likely to enforce some type of dress code.

        1.3.2.5.1. Con:
        School uniforms can prevent unequal enforcement of dress codes by mandating all people to wear a uniform and removing the grey area [79] for enforcement between girls and boys.

      1.3.2.6. Con:
      Many school uniforms look nearly the same for girls and boys.

        1.3.2.6.1. Con:
        This argument contains the two fallacies: hasty generalization [80], as it is generalizes all schools, and ipse dixit [81], as it provides no evidence to support the claim.

    1.3.3. Con:
    School uniforms have a positive effect on the self esteem of students (p. 15 [82]).

      1.3.3.1. Pro:
      School uniforms may prevent students from being teased or ostracised for wearing inexpensive, or unfashionable clothes (p. 15 [83]).

      1.3.3.2. Con:
      Some research has found that uniforms do not have any effect on students' self esteem (p. 27 [84]).

    1.3.4. Con:
    School uniforms are fair as they make everyone look and feel equal.

      1.3.4.1. Pro:
      School uniforms encourage students to evaluate people by their character, not their appearance.

        1.3.4.1.1. Pro:
        New research [85] suggests that students judge others' competence based on their clothes within one second of viewing them. Therefore, uniforms allow students to focus more attention on education.

          1.3.4.1.1.1. Pro:
          Well designed and maintained uniforms also give the public the impression of a well-run school and well-behaved students, increasing the standing of the school.

        1.3.4.1.2. Con:
        School uniforms would not prevent bullying on the nature of non-clothing related subjects [86] like height, weight, and hair.

          1.3.4.1.2.1. Con:
          This argument contains a nirvana fallacy [87], as it is rejecting uniform policies because they do not guarantee perfection.

        1.3.4.1.3. Con:
        Uniforms prevent children from expressing themselves through clothing.

          1.3.4.1.3.1. Pro:
          School uniforms limit self-expression in young individuals. Children need this time to grow and explore their creativity.

      1.3.4.2. Con:
      School uniforms do not make everyone equal. People are still unequal, they are just wearing the same uniforms.

        1.3.4.2.1. Con:
        It is true that school uniforms aren't magic cloaks that instantly dissolve all social inequalities. However, if everyone wears the school uniform, all students are equal in terms of what they are wearing, which is often an indicator of socioeconomic status and a cause of bullying.

        1.3.4.2.2. Pro:
        School uniforms do not hide the difference in social classes and does not stop materialism. These are things which will always be present and no school or government will be able to hide these things.

      1.3.4.3. Con:
      Some school uniforms are designed to be used all over country and not in one particular school. This means that there are various price that relates to clothes' quality [88]. However, even if the poor could afford just low quality one, it is even more expensive [89] than simple shirt and pants.

        1.3.4.3.1. Con:
        Thailand is an individual case that confirms the proposition. However, the other countries where the proposition would prove to be false, such as the United States, are ignored.

      1.3.4.4. Pro:
      School uniforms help students to feel like they belong as they make it harder for students to judge one another.

        1.3.4.4.1. Pro:
        School uniforms provide a sense of shared pride and identity. [90]

          1.3.4.4.1.1. Pro:
          Many school uniforms have a long and storied tradition [91] dating back hundreds of years that can help connect students to the school.

          1.3.4.4.1.2. Pro:
          Students in mandatory uniform schools reported "significantly more positive perceptions of belonging [92] in their school community" than those at non-uniform schools.

          1.3.4.4.1.3. Con:
          School uniforms can tarnish the reputation of schools if students wearing it are found to be involved in bad behavior.

            1.3.4.4.1.3.1. Con:
            The fact that it would tarnish the school's reputation would also force the school to be more thorough about recruiting students or to penalize such behavior.

              1.3.4.4.1.3.1.1. Con:
              Increased punishment of students would have negative outcomes [93], such as reduced interest in learning and increased dropout rates.

            1.3.4.4.1.3.2. Pro:
            Students who care about their school and its reputation are less likely to engage in bad behaviour in public, such as smoking [94].

            1.3.4.4.1.3.3. Con:
            Making students wear uniforms may help prevent bad behavior, as students would fear punishment from their school if knowledge of their misdemeanours got back to the school.

        1.3.4.4.2. Con:
        There are many other factors apart from school uniforms that might help students feel a sense of belonging.

          1.3.4.4.2.1. Pro:
          If the school environment is conducive to the learning of students [95], it will automatically inculcate a sense of belonging in them.

        1.3.4.4.3. Pro:
        By wearing school uniforms, schools and students can be unified, helping them to work together.

      1.3.4.5. Con:
      Student dress can be an indicator of how a child is doing, whether they are being neglected or abused [96] at home. Uniforms eliminate this barometer.

        1.3.4.5.1. Pro:
        If a child comes to school in clothing that is obviously in a poor state then school authorities may know to check up on that child's family situation.

        1.3.4.5.2. Con:
        There are other things that teachers [97] are trained to notice as signifiers of abuse or neglect, such as childrens' emotions, relationships, and physical signs.

      1.3.4.6. Pro:
      School uniforms have been shown to reduce [98] tensions between and within students by concealing wealth and ethnic differences.

        1.3.4.6.1. Pro:
        Uniforms reduce fashion competition, snobbery, and bullying [99].

        1.3.4.6.2. Pro:
        Many UK schools [100] that rejected uniforms a decade ago are now returning to them as 'part' of a regime to calm schools and enable learning.

        1.3.4.6.3. Pro:
        Some families are not able to afford some expensive brands of clothes. School uniforms makes those differences in income less apparent.

          1.3.4.6.3.1. Con:
          While rich families are able to purchase new school uniforms as needed, many lower-income families [101] often have to purchase worn, second-hand school uniforms or rely on ill-fitting hand-me-downs from siblings.

            1.3.4.6.3.1.1. Pro:
            Affluent families are reported to have more uniforms per child [102] than less affluent families.

          1.3.4.6.3.2. Pro:
          Making socio-economic differences less apparent decreases the likelihood of a student being bullied or assaulted [103] for perceived "poor" clothing.

        1.3.4.6.4. Con:
        As long as there as significant social and personal benefits [104] to bullying, it will continue.

      1.3.4.7. Pro:
      Issues such as wealth and social standing can create a negative perception of people, especially with children.

        1.3.4.7.1. Pro:
        Facebook, which owns Instagram, shared in a 2019 corporate data presentation obtained by The Wall Street Journal [105], that it has conducted years of in-depth research, and found Instagram isn't helpful for teens' mental health. "We make body image issues worse for one in three teen girls," one slide from the presentation said.

    1.3.5. Con:
    School uniforms can help promote campus safety [106].

      1.3.5.1. Pro:
      When students wear uniforms, educators and administration can easily identify an intruder on campus, preventing potentially dangerous situations.

        1.3.5.1.1. Con:
        As teachers and other staff members are not required to wear uniforms, intruders may still be undetected.

      1.3.5.2. Pro:
      School uniforms make it much harder to conceal weapons under baggy clothing.

        1.3.5.2.1. Pro:
        Schools with mandatory uniform policies were found to have 12% fewer firearm-related incidents [107] than schools without uniforms. (p. 2)

        1.3.5.2.2. Con:
        Clothing alone will not stop children who determined to bring weapons to school. A gun that is small enough to be hid under baggy clothing could equally be hidden in a schoolbag.

      1.3.5.3. Pro:
      School uniforms can prevent the wearing of gang-related imagery or colors. [108]

        1.3.5.3.1. Con:
        Students can very easily find other ways to express alignment to a gang or group to get around any restrictions. [109]

        1.3.5.3.2. Con:
        School faculty and administration often go overboard [110] and label a wide variety of clothing as gang related when in reality very few students have any relation to gang activity.

        1.3.5.3.3. Con:
        School uniforms are much less effective [111] than other preventative practices against gang-violence.

          1.3.5.3.3.1. Pro:
          Metal detectors, increased funding for after school programs, and developing positive student mentor relationships [112] were all significantly effective at lowering gang related school activities.

      1.3.5.4. Con:
      While campus safety may increase, wearing school uniforms could make students a target for violence and bullying off-campus. [113]

      1.3.5.5. Pro:
      Rates of sexual assaults reduce [114] when school uniform policies are implemented.

        1.3.5.5.1. Pro:
        In Long Beach, California, when school uniforms were introduced sexual offense reports dropped by 74% [115].

          1.3.5.5.1.1. Con:
          There are other causal factors [116] for this drop in offences, such as greater parental involvement, school decentralisation and a determined effort to improve the learning environment.

        1.3.5.5.2. Con:
        35% [117] of girls wearing school uniforms have been sexually harassed in public, as they make them to easy to identify.

      1.3.5.6. Pro:
      The push for school uniforms was borne out of an attempt to combat the crime wave of the 1990's. [118]

      1.3.5.7. Con:
      A report of tenth graders who wore school uniforms found no decresaed rates [119] for substance abuse, or distribution on campus.

    1.3.6. Pro:
    School uniform policies and their enforcement can be racist [120].

      1.3.6.1. Pro:
      Some school uniform policies mandate the exclusion of certain hairstyles [121] often associated with minority students.

          1.3.6.1.1.1. Con:

      1.3.6.2. Pro:

        1.3.6.2.2. Pro:
        Students of color already experience almost three times as many disciplinary actions [126] as white students in the US. Requiring them to wear uniforms only provides more opportunities to take them out of classrooms for minor behaviors.

    1.3.7. Con:
    School uniforms can help create a bond between a student and their schoolmates.

      1.3.7.1. Pro:
      Nobody should be excluded because of their clothes.

  1.4. Con:
  Uniforms play a very important [127] role in maintaining school discipline [128], a vital [129] skill for students to learn.

    1.4.1. Con:
    People who are over-disciplined may develop a fear of change or disruption [130].

      1.4.1.1. Pro:
      Trying to force discipline on people can cause low self-esteem [131] or behavioural issues [132].

    1.4.2. Pro:
    School uniforms make it significantly less likely that children will wear offensive clothing.

      1.4.2.1. Con:
      Dress codes based on avoiding "offensive clothing" often play into sexist stereotypes [133].

        1.4.2.1.1. Pro:
        Women are often told to restrict the clothing they wear to avoid becoming a "distraction" [134] to the boys in the class. This type of thinking perpetuates a patriarchal trope [135] that women are to blame for the actions of men.

        1.4.2.1.2. Con:
        Both boys and girls [136] are required to follow the rules against offensive clothing so it's not unfair to either gender.

          1.4.2.1.2.1. Con:
          Girls are called out more often [137] for breaking dress code rules than boys are.

            1.4.2.1.2.1.1. Con:
            This does not necessarily imply any form of discrimination. It could just be that girls break the dress code rules more and are therefore "more often called out."

      1.4.2.2. Con:
      The avoidance of many of obviously offensive items of clothing could be done just as easily with a school dress code.

      1.4.2.3. Pro:
      Clothing with offensive messages could be distracting to other students and impair their ability to focus on their work.

        1.4.2.3.1. Pro:
        If a student wore clothing that had profanity on it, younger students may find this funny and consequently struggle to focus on their work.

        1.4.2.3.2. Con:
        Clothing with offensive language may help bolster a student's confidence [138] and, in turn, help them stay focused.

      1.4.2.4. Pro:
      Clothing with offensive messages or language could make other students feel uncomfortable.

        1.4.2.4.1. Pro:
        If a message on a piece of clothing offends someone else's religious beliefs, it may upset them.

        1.4.2.4.2. Con:
        Students have a right to wear whatever clothing they want, irrespective of how it makes other people feel.

          1.4.2.4.2.1. Con:
          In the US, the Supreme Court has ruled that students do not have the right to wear clothing with profanity [139] on it at schools.

      1.4.2.5. Con:
      At school, children are still finding out their personalities and need to know what feels right. Since clothing influences personality, letting kids not have the option could will affect their future.

    1.4.3. Con:
    The amount of time and effort teachers would have to spend to police school uniform policies would take away from more important schooling.

      1.4.3.1. Pro:
      It will be harder to tell who perpetrated a bad action as everyone wears the same thing [140] and are fairly indistinguishable which will take up more of a teacher's time.

      1.4.3.2. Con:
      School dress codes often involve constant policing, and dealing with grey areas in a way that a blanket uniform policy would not.

        1.4.3.2.1. Con:
        If dress codes are clear and decided by the school community as a whole - including parents and pupils - they may be easily enforcable.

        1.4.3.2.2. Pro:
        If teachers have to spend time disciplining students for wearing the incorrect dress, and adjudicating marginal cases, their time [141] is taken away from teaching and learning.

    1.4.4. Con:
    There is no evidence [142] of improved cultures of discipline in schools that require school uniforms.

      1.4.4.1. Pro:
      Often students attempt to subvert their uniforms, rebelling by wearing them improperly.

      1.4.4.2. Pro:
      Schools with mandatory uniform policies saw no improvement [143] in rates of student attendance, achievement, behavioral problems, or substance abuse.

      1.4.4.3. Pro:
      A 10-year study of mandatory uniform schools found an increase in disciplinary infractions [144] among students, especially male ones.

      1.4.4.4. Con:
      While uniforms may have little effect on students, it has been shown that schools with uniform policies have large increases in teacher retention, which is vital for school success (p. 22 [145]).

        1.4.4.4.1. Pro:
        Low teacher retention makes it harder for students to form interpersonal relationships with teachers over time, meaning they will have less support.

        1.4.4.4.2. Pro:
        Low teacher retention causes schools to hire underqualified candidates [146] who may not be as skilled or experienced at their jobs.

        1.4.4.4.3. Con:
        The effect on teacher attrition was observed only in elementary schools, not in schools with older age groups (p. 21 [147]).

      1.4.4.5. Pro:
      Discipline cannot be effectively enforced by outside means. It must be engendered by someone's own self-discipline [148].

        1.4.4.5.1. Pro:
        The imposition of school uniforms may lead to rebellion [149], and make discipline harder to come by.

    1.4.5. Con:
    School uniforms were often used to instill patriotic pride [150] in the youth, prepping them for service in their countries' militaries. There are now fewer [151] people joining the military, so focusing an entire element of schooling towards it is pointless and outdated.

      1.4.5.1. Con:
      It is important for people to be patriotic [152], and many people still go on to join the military [153].

        1.4.5.1.1. Con:
        Schools should not seek to push students towards certain lives, especially risky ones like the military.

    1.4.6. Pro:
    School uniforms prepare students for jobs in the professional workforce [154], many of which have dress codes or even uniforms.

      1.4.6.1. Con:
      The field of digital technology has led to a rise in employees wearing garments more for comfort. For example, in many Silicon Valley companies, the norm is to "dress down" [155].

      1.4.6.2. Pro:
      Teaching a student to feel comfortable in the clothing worn by the powerful, such as formal clothing, encourages [156] them to feel comfortable with success and power.

        1.4.6.2.1. Con:
        Not everyone aspires to power; noted rich man Elon musk is commonly disliked by individuals [157], and as such, students may not wish to dress like him.

      1.4.6.3. Con:
      Many commentators have noted that COVID-19, and the rise of working from home, will likely make workplace dress codes [158] less commonplace.

      1.4.6.4. Pro:
      Students will receive a huge "shock to the system [159]" if they go through their entire schooling not wearing a uniform but are then forced to wear one at work.

    1.4.7. Pro:
    Students who wear uniforms are less focused on what they themselves and others are wearing, and so are more focused on the academic [160] side of school.

      1.4.7.1. Con:
      The appearance and accessories of other students would still likely distract students even if everyone wears uniforms.

      1.4.7.2. Con:
      The clothing of other students is likely not an especially big distraction for students.

      1.4.7.3. Pro:
      If everyone wore the same clothing, there would be nothing exciting that anyone else could wear to distract other students.

      1.4.7.4. Con:
      There are many other distractions in a classroom that school uniforms would not solve, such as students looking at what others are doing or what is outside the window.

    1.4.8. Pro:
    School uniforms enforces a school's morals on to the students, which will, in the future, make them better citizens in society.

      1.4.8.1. Pro:
      Students could demonstrate that they are proud of their school, wearing its clothes every day. This could motivate them to learn.

  1.5. Con:
  The decision to have school uniforms should be chosen by the school itself.

    1.5.1. Con:
    Standardized school rules has a strong precedent, with many tests, curricula, and teaching training regimes being standardized throughout countries or districts.

    1.5.2. Con:
    Schools are often denied their discretion on non-academic matters, as seen in the banning of corporal punishment [161], or the mandated provision of lunch food [162].

    1.5.3. Pro:
    School uniforms could become optional. The school could encourage student to wear a uniform, but not force anyone to wear it.

      1.5.3.1. Pro:
      Some students may like the uniform, but not want to wear it every day.

      1.5.3.2. Pro:
      Such optional uniforms would especially useful because people need to display their own culture, like if they are Muslim [163] or Jewish [164].

      1.5.3.3. Con:
      The benefits of a school uniform, like equalising students, reducing costs, and fostering school spirit are contingent upon every student wearing one.

        1.5.3.3.1. Pro:
        A student voluntarily wearing a school uniform would likely become a target for bullies.

  1.6. Pro:
  School uniforms are often uncomfortable [165].

    1.6.1. Pro:
    School uniforms can be uncomfortable when you are forced to wear them on a really hot day.

      1.6.1.1. Con:
      School uniforms are different [166] depending on the season. In summer, the uniform typically consists of a t-shirt with shorts which is what most people wear normally during a hot day.

        1.6.1.1.1. Con:
        Not every country has seasonal school uniforms. Many students in Ireland [167], for example, have to wear skirts to school year-round.

        1.6.1.1.2. Con:
        Having seasonal uniforms doubles the amount of clothes a student needs to buy, increasing the total cost on families.

        1.6.1.1.3. Pro:
        In Japan, school uniforms change [168] to summer clothes on June 1st, and to winter ones on October 1st.

      1.6.1.2. Pro:
      During heatwaves [169], students have been prevented from taking their heavy blazers off, or wearing anything but long trousers. This level of heat can also have health impacts on students.

        1.6.1.2.1. Con:
        Heatwaves are rare [170] occurences. A uniform policy should not be avoided based on an incredibly rare event, when they offer incredible benefits.

          1.6.1.2.1.1. Con:
          In recent years, heatwaves have become more common, due to global warming [171], thus requiring the policy to be more heatwave friendly.

    1.6.2. Pro:
    The fabric used in school uniforms can sometimes be very stiff, which restricts motion.

      1.6.2.1. Pro:
      One's movement can also be restricted by skirts, which require to wearer to be constantly aware [172] of how they sit, play, and move.

      1.6.2.2. Pro:
      Shirts, if improperly fitted, can restrict [173] one's range of movement.

      1.6.2.3. Con:
      Stiff fabric, often found in school uniforms, can be softened [174] over time, by simply washing the clothes.

    1.6.3. Pro:
    Uniforms can be too short and inappropriately revealing.

      1.6.3.1. Pro:
      Children attend school during their teenage years, when growth spurts [175] are common. This leads to them rapidly outgrowing their garments.

      1.6.3.2. Con:
      There is a very low chance that a schoolboard would support a uniform that is too revealing.

      1.6.3.3. Pro:
      Skirts often do not to accommodate many different body types. Taller [176] pupils who wear skirts could be particularly at risk of facing punishment or embarassment.

    1.6.4. Pro:
    School ties can be tightened [177] around students necks. Students often do this to each other.

      1.6.4.1. Con:
      If this were true, the solution would be a uniform policy without a tie, rather than avoiding uniform policies as a whole.

    1.6.5. Pro:
    The materials used in uniforms can be itchy [178].

      1.6.5.1. Con:
      This isn't an argument against uniforms. It is rather an argument against itchy material in general.

        1.6.5.1.1. Pro:
        High-quality school uniforms can be designed to minimize the use of itchy material.

      1.6.5.2. Con:
      The only fabric listed as "itchy" is wool, which the uniform makers do not recommend for children.

      1.6.5.3. Pro:
      Some children have been known to be allergic [179] to the materials commonly used in school uniforms.

    1.6.6. Con:
    To say that all uniforms are uncomfortable implies that there is something inherent to school uniforms that makes them uncomfortable, which is untrue.

      1.6.6.1. Pro:
      Uniforms [180] vary across the world, between countries and geographical regions, and so it cannot be said that they are mostly uncomfortable.

      1.6.6.2. Con:
      Uniforms all share the goal of instilling discipline in students, and so they largely lean towards using restrictive fabrics, which make uniforms inherently uncomfortable.

    1.6.7. Con:
    Students could decide themselves how the uniform should look like.

      1.6.7.1. Con:
      Uniforms are often meant to be used as a way to instill more control on students, meaning students will often not be able of choosing the uniform's design.

    1.6.8. Con:
    There could be a selection of different options that students could choose from, for example, skirts and trousers.

    1.6.9. Con:
    Uniforms can be comfortable and liked [181].

      1.6.9.1. Pro:
      Many uniform designers [182] are responding to the demand for more comfortable school clothes for students, and it is becoming more commonplace that uniforms are comfortable.
