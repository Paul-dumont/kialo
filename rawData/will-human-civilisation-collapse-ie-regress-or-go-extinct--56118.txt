Discussion Name:
Will human civilisation collapse (i.e regress or go extinct) ?

1. Thesis:
Will humanity make it or will civilisation collapse (i.e regress or go extinct) ?

  1.1. Con:
  Existential risk: 1)A faulty economic system (Fractional reserve banking, Fiat currencies, intervention of Central Banks through quantitative easing, the petro-dollar,Stock Market A.I algorithms since 2000,modern monetary theory,finite resources &infinite needs) all of it hinging on the Breton-Woods, Pax Americana type of world (where the US was the world's policeman, and guarded the oceans, making it more profitable for everyone to trade instead of go to war, even small countries with no navies

    1.1.1. Con:
    Developing a General Artificial Intelligence G.A.I, that has different goals than humanity, or feel humanity is the greates existential risk for itself , or maybe an A.I is given a mission to maximize papecrlip production and will turn the whole matter in the universe into paperclips. [1]

    1.1.2. Con:
    Even if our human civilisation's financial systems crash, this is unlikely to spell the end of civilisation as a whole; economies have experienced, and bounced back from, many deep recessions in previous centuries.

    1.1.3. Pro:
    The way we designed our economy in the first place will at some point reflect a self-terminating system (the fact that it is designed for perpetual growth in a resource finite planet).The fact that it counts on oil, the petro-dollar as the world reserve currency and the US to maintain the order, The fact that it already requires central bank intervention to stay alive, the fact that it will not only be cyclical (with bull and bear markets), but will implode at some point.

  1.2. Con:
  Institutions are manned by corrupt or incompentent people, the climate change Paris Agreement is a good example, of declarative talks, and not implementing much change, even subsidising, coal and oil companies. As long as lobbying,crony capitalism,kleptocracies,corrupted governments and large corporations ,that hijack said governments- exist; As long as there are short term individual benefits and long term collective costs (a tragedy of the commons situation) nothing will be done .

  1.3. Con:

And tribalisation (in-group/out-group mentalities), where the violent,unuseful type of conflict and competition will never become extinct.     1.3.2. Pro:

    The only things that have somewhat ammeliorated these tendencies :1) religions ,which doubled amongst other roles ,as sociological engineering methods- for stabilising societies and populations (i.e enshrining monogamy to keep young men which could be the most violent, happy ,otherwise some sort of hypergamy happend (multiple women gravitating towards one high social status/rich resource male ); condemning murder and other traditions ,and habits that were coupled with the culture of that region.

    2)Intra-societal conflict can be ameliorated by the monopoly of violence by the state (Max Weber). Disputes to be settled by pre-established known laws (i.e duels between nobles), and impersonal judges ,(the descendants of Roman Law) and not nepotism, or other ways of managing things.
